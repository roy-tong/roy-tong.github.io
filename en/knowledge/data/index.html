Research data and statistical boundaries

The first batch of re-counting results, field descriptions and limitations of the three groups of corpus were released. Only publish the summary after review, and no longer distribute the original platform content.

What is disclosed this time?

This is the structural recount of three local research snapshots on August 31, 2026, and the statistical unit isTake notes. The data helps to check the scope and deviation of the research materials, and is not used to infer the market universality or the establishment of demand.

What tests have been done by the recount?

Analyze the input line by line, recalculate the number of records, non-empty text, source distribution, record ID and missing and duplicate existing text hashs, and bind the SHA-256 of each input file.

No invalid JSON lines, empty body or duplicate record IDs were found in all three snapshots. There are missing text hash fields: 2 glasses corpus, 1 home robot corpus, and 0 local AI corpus. Therefore, "already hashed but not repeated" cannot be interpreted as a complete content to be re-certify.

Cross-repetition is calculated from the body: Unicode NFKC normalizes, merges continuous blanks and removes the first and last blanks, and compares according to the only non-empty text. Here, only the exact consistency is checked, and the approximate rewriting is not checked, and the personal identity is not related.

How should the machine label be read?

The main snapshot selected by the local AI does not have an E0–E5 code field, so it is uniformly recorded asnot-coded. This does not mean that other derived files have never been encoded.

The E0–E5 numbers in glasses and home robots are produced by historical processing.The number of candidate labels. There is no one-by-one verification of the semantics in this recount. The existence of the E3 label does not mean that the program acceptance has been established, and the existence of the E5 label does not mean that there is evidence of the product we are studying.

Before using the label, you need to check the speaker, program, scene, role and context. See the meaning of the levelDemand evidence, see the method protocolSURE。

Why can't it be mixed with the old article directly?

In historical research, there are original collection, product feedback screening set, open scene material, balanced sampling and coding set at the same time. The product feedback subset cited in the previous demand research article is not the same denominator as the 209,862 eyeglass combination corpus in this round.

When updating the conclusion, confirm the snapshot and filter criteria first. You can't just replace the body number with the larger one.

To what extent does it reapplain?

JSON retains the summary of the input file, statistical caliber and all summaries. Open readers can recalculate the ratio, the sum of groups, and the consistency of CSV and JSON. The original snapshot that requires authorization to be completely recounted one by one;The original data set cannot be independently reconstructed by public summary alone.。

The original text, platform account, user identification, original post address collection and content hash by article are not included in this release. There are no documents involving internal materials of the enterprise. The input file-level SHA-256 is only used to fix this snapshot, and does not provide personal associated information.

How to use

It is suitable for understanding the scale, source concentration and structure of this batch of materials, or checking whether the numbers are wrongly amplified in subsequent articles. It is not suitable for directly estimating the purchase rate, retention rate, market share, national user demand or product effect.

The next release will give priority to supplementing reviewable task-level analysis and manual review methods, retaining cases of rejection, satisfactory alternatives and insufficient evidence. The value of the research depends on the quality of judgment, not on the size of the downloaded file.
