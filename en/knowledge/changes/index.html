Substantive amendments that have been done

  • Business maturity and platformization are divided into two judgment axes. The platform is not automatically more mature than vertical products with stable profits.
  • Automatic working time needs to be combined with effective output, manual intervention and the cost of failure, and is not used as a general value indicator.
  • Agent's attempt, operation, billing and value are handled separately to avoid the same "usage" changing meaning in different contexts.
  • Research records, machine candidate coding and verified requirements are separated; the three groups of corpus recalculate the source distribution and cross-group text overlap.
  • The product feedback subset and the combination corpus retain different denominators, and do not need to cover the old caliber with larger numbers.
  • The fixed number of people in the hardware organization and the stage schedule converge to the example under specific conditions, not as a general law.
  • The world model classification indicates the source of the proposal, distinguishes the picture, state and action, and does not regard the good-looking generated video as proof of physical ability.

These gaps do not enter the public site in the form of hidden drafts. The first version will first release the derivative content that can be read independently and clearly explain the boundaries of the evidence. Follow up and add according to the questions, and continue to leave records here.
