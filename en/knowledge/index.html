How it works

From working material to public research

  1. 01Sources

    Inventory local projects, Feishu documents, public pages, and data snapshots, with versions and ownership.

  2. 02Dossiers

    Group material around one question and record the current judgment, evidence, and gaps.

  3. 03Public materials

    Confirm what can be published, then release reports, data, notes, or source indexes.

  4. 04Essays

    Write only when a complete argument is needed, drawing on one or more materials.
