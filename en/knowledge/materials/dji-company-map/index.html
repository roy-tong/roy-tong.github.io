What is this information?

This online atlas sorts out the public research clues of Xinjiang-related talents to start a business according to the company and product. It provides a list of objects, which is convenient to return to specific companies and public sources to continue verification.

The original atlas and articles are kept at the original address; the knowledge base only establishes a stable information description and topic attribution for this form, and no other documents in the local research catalog are released.

How to use

Check the objects and fields first, and then check the product, time and related properties according to your own questions. If you need to use a certain piece of information for external analysis, please continue to consult the corresponding first-hand sources, and do not regard the catalog as the completion of fact verification.

Applicable boundaries

A common resume does not mean that there is an equity relationship between companies, and the success rate of entrepreneurship cannot be estimated based on a few cases included. Talent flow, organizational experience and industrial results need to be proved separately.

The atlas is kept in the public version. Other local resume information, internal documents or company strategies are not publicly licensed. When quoting, please attach the atlas address and version date, and explain your own filter criteria.
