The common problems I continue to study

Cutting-edge technology provides new capabilities, but whether users adopt it, whether tasks can be delivered repeatedly, and how the results are verified need to be studied separately. This map organizes public results with these questions, and does not stack catalogs according to the number of warehouses or technical hot words.
