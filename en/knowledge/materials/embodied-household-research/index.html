4. In-depth analysis by morphology

4.1 Sweeping robot/sweeping and mopping: the form of the only task closed loop (26.1% corpus, E5=1,285)

SURE link (representing evidence):

  • Users: family buyers, long-term owners,The second buyer bought for parents, enterprise procurement (small quantity)
  • Tasks: whole house vacuuming/mopping automation, regular cleaning, multi-floor, pet hair, self-cleaning
  • Alternative: manual vacuum cleaner/mop, cleaning ("bought my Roomba in 2013, it cost as much total as I pay my cleaning ladies to come once every two weeks" - E5 long-term comparison), sweeper replacement
  • Friction (open word discovery Top signal):water tank / dirty water / clean water / mop pads / dust bin / auto empty / base station(Maintenance complexity),obstacle avoidance(Ability),factory reset / customer service(After-sales service),pet hair(Scene),black friday(The timing of purchase)
  • Commercial evidence: E5 1,285 ("I bought the S8 plus", "My Narwal T10 is an excellent mop", "I bought it, there are several problems... The threshold is stuck/the rag is not clean/the fallen leaves are smeared"); E4- 861 ("I returned this robot...the app", "Chasing T70 sewage tank moldy smell directly persuaded to withdraw")

Key findings:

  1. The complaining structure has changed from "do you want to buy it" to "after buying"——Maintenance/subscription/App/reliability topic is dominated, which is a typical feature of the closed loop of the task (in stark contrast to the robot dog).
  2. "Buying for parents" is real and quantifiable commercial evidence of care.: eldercare×sweeper E5=44, representative example:“My BoB SLAM Pet sweeps have been amazing… My parents also got one based on my recommendation.”——The separation of buyers and users is the cheapest cut-in study for the care scene.
  3. Privacy disputes persist (map upload, camera, hacking), and HN and Reddit have discussed it, which constitutes a brand trust risk.
  4. Chinese channels (Bilibili 942) appear "low-end = disaster, directly on the flagship" consensus and specific complaints (moldy smell, threshold, rag), which complements the English corpus; at the same time3,999 yuan bucket version vs P20 ProThe price anchor point discussion.

Counter-evidence:E4- Recording coexists with the alternative discussion of "the vacuum cleaner is more thorough and faster manually" - the sweeper solves "frequency" rather than "depth"; the dislike of subscription/App lock is one of the main reasons for the return.

4.2 Humanoid robot: onlookers - suspicious structure, family scene evidence is not formed (1.5% corpus, E5=4)

  • The corpus has a "news-onlookers-doubt" structure: Optimus/Figure/Atlas is mainly questioned (hype/demo/a few years later) under the release of the news; E4-class "humanoid hype" is prominently discussed.
  • The real task description of the family scene (cleaning up, carrying, cooking) exists butDo not bind the human form——The user said "I hope there is a robot to help me", but he didn't say "I want a humanoid".
  • The only thing close to closing is the developer ecology (E5=1: Unitree G1 purchase and deployment problem), but the sample size is 2 orders of magnitude lower than the sweeper.
  • Brand word collision has been handled.: Nvidia Optimus (notebook graphics card) ≠ Humanoid Optimus, coding layer plus exclusion rules; random inspection shows that the humanoid form still has a 31% availability rate (the loudest noise), and it must be reviewed manually before any conclusion.

4.3 Robot dog/four-leged: the developer's ecology is mature, and the household consumption is not closed-loop (0.6% corpus, E5=2)

  • Consistent with the last audit: developer/ROS/SDK is the main evidence direction (Unitree Go2/G1 purchase deployment, SDK issue, ROS2 configuration).
  • Family scene (accompanying/security/care) problem chain exists, but the plan chain and business chain are scarce; the Chinese "robot dog IQ tax/eating dust" is an important counter-extence.
  • E5=2 is all for developers/educational purposes,No household consumption E5——The conclusion that "there is insufficient evidence of the domestic consumption demand of robot dogs" continues to be valid.

4.4 Accompanying robot/electronic pet: real payment but small scale, product concentration (1.7% corpus, E5=75)

  • After this supplementary collection of AIBO/AnkiVector/vectorrobot/Cozmo community, the real E5 appears:“I bought a used cozmo from ebay to help cope with loneliness”(Accompanying motivation), "had both my second hand Cozmo and vector for about 2 months", "bought my Cozmo from the Anki Website".
  • E4+/price evidence focuses on second-hand and subscription ("getting a japanese aibo is cost prohibitive", "3 years upfront is less expensive").
  • The general "accompanying robot" form lacks killer products; loneliness/accompanying open scenes (E1/E2) are rich but the scheme chain is weak (accompanying × accompanying/emotion E3=1).

4.5 Other household robots (0.2%)

The mowing robot (Automower/Navimow) has a small number of real uses ("I got an Automower this year and it's been one of the best purchases I ever made", "Just deployed a Segway Navimow"), and the rest is mainly news discussion. Keep an observation and don't set up a project.

4.6.3 Enlightenment on product definition (corresponding materials" "Four Product Definition Principles")

  1. Role>List of functions: The role expectation expression (role_expectation scenario) of "robot butler / butler / assistant / errand runner" in the corpus can be retrieved. It is recommended that the concept test directly use the role framework instead of the function list.
  2. Narrow Product Promise: Among the four entrances, the task boundary of pick-up and delivery logistics is the clearest ("pick-delive-return common items"), which is in line with the "General Hardware + Narrow Promise" route.
  3. The boundary of initiative: The semantic_safety scene (knife/drug/gas/child presence/rejection task) has a fragmentary expression in the corpus, and it is recommended to enter the permission framework design of the concept test.
  4. Failure/Recovery UX: Sweeper E4- Record ("returned this robot...the app") proofFailure experience directly drives the return——Recovery UX should be included in the acceptance standards of the first version of PRD.

4.6.4 Limitations

The humanoid × new scene count is all from the rule code and the volume is very small (2–65),Any ranking is a hypothetical priority, not a market conclusion.; It is recommended to make the four entrances directly into 4 sets of concept tests (closed-loop / recovery UX / HITL takeover / abnormal confirmation), with 15–20 family samples in each group, compared with the conclusion of the public corpus.

9. Limitations (complete)

  1. Rule coding noise: E0–E5 is determined by the rules, and the availability rate of random checks is 64% (humanoid 31%); all counts are "candidate evidence pool", not the accuracy rate. V2.1 New scenes (pick-up/abnormal/remote/independent life) have a small level (2–65), and the sorting is a hypothetical priority.
  2. Query framework blocked: Arctic body search 422 + PullPush 429 throughout the whole process, rejector/substitute directional query is not executed; derived coverage from time slice + code (E4- 4.7% of direct, less than 8% design line).
  3. Bilibili risk control: 412 multiple interruptions, 1,329 Chinese articles<10,000 quota; the Chinese conclusion is preliminary.
  4. GitHub no token: Only issue title + text, 10 req/min speed limit; StackExchange quota 300 req/day.
  5. Platform concentration: Reddit 88.1%; Conclusions are listed by source, but the overall representativeness is still limited.
  6. Time/event confusion: The collection window data in 2026 is naturally more; the monthly distribution is interpreted according to the quota, and the event is not marked.
  7. The scene label is loose and co-appears.: The scene word and the morphological word appear together in the full text, and there is a weak correlation (for example, the word eldercare appears in the family context of the sweeper community, and the direction is a real signal but needs to be confirmed manually).
  8. No labor fee standard: In this issue, it will be replaced by 95 desktop random checks (53 will be judged); 600–1,000 layered manual codes must be completed before making business decisions.
  9. The date of YouTube is approximate: The relative time ("1 year ago") is converted to an approximate date and marked, which cannot be used for accurate monthly trends.
