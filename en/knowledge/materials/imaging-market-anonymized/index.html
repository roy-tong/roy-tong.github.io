Why do you need to talk about the caliber first?

"Hand-held intelligent imaging equipment" is not a single category. Different institutions will put sports cameras, panoramic cameras, gimbal cameras, and even some mobile phone image accessories into different boundaries. As long as the category definition, resolution threshold, channel or year are different, the size of the same market may be several times different.

Therefore, this page does not provide a seemingly accurate market sum, but answers four questions first:

  1. The statistics are the shipment of the whole machine, sales, or the income including subscriptions;
  2. Whether it contains low-resolution white-license devices;
  3. Whether it is a global, a single country, or an online channel;
  4. The share is the manufacturer's self-report, channel reading, or audited financial data.

After anonymization, the specific institution name and report link were removed. These caliber questions are retained because they are more likely to determine whether the analysis is valid than a certain ranking figure.

Market structure

The part of the public information that is more consistent is:

  • The top three account for the vast majority of the shipments, and the remaining market is shared by a large number of small and medium-sized brands;
  • The subsequent single-brand share is usually not enough to be disclosed separately by mainstream tracking agencies;
  • Low-priced white cards will significantly change the caliber of "by number of units" and "by amount";
  • The high-end pricing model in the industry report may seriously conflict with the average selling price disclosed by listed companies.

This means that "who is the fourth place in the world" is often not a reliable research question. The more reliable question is whether it has been fragmented outside the head, and what channels and prices the remaining manufacturers rely on to survive.

Four types of automatic shooting products

"Automatic shooting robots" include at least four categories, each of which sells different things:

Type The result of the user's purchase Core verification
Autonomously move the camera position Third-person moving lens Does the movement produce content that cannot be obtained in the fixed position?
Robotized mirror equipment Repeatable professional sports lens Is the crew willing to pay for the setting and stability?
Fixed AI photographer Unattended record of the whole session Are users willing to publish automatically generated results?
Pan-robot accompanying shooting Robot ontology, shooting is just a function. Does the shooting ability affect the purchase decision?

Putting these four categories into a competitive list will assume that they are competing for the same market. In fact, their alternatives, price anchors and buyers are all different.

The value and debt of mobile seats

The mobile chassis can bring paralallas, masking, height change and real sense of propulsion, while also increasing noise, safety, endurance, failure rate and on-site care costs.

A more rigorous judgment indicator is the "proportion of non-intervention sessions": the proportion of users who do not touch the device throughout the whole process and are still willing to release it in the end. This indicator checks the acquisition, composition, failure recovery and piece quality at the same time. Among the public materials, there are very few manufacturers who are willing to fully disclose the index.

The fixed panoramic camera position plus algorithm reconstruction provides another route: without moving the physical camera position, it can also create a sense of following. At least in the sports scene where the venue is standardized and the vision can be covered, the unit economy of this route is easier to establish. It does not prove that the mobile position is worthless, but pushes the responsibility of proof back to the mobile scheme: the new physical movement is the result of which fixed scheme cannot do.

Failure mode

There are three types of failures in public cases:

  1. Consumer-level price multiplied by engineering-level cost: The reliability cost of equipment that can move, avoid obstacles and follow is much higher than that of ordinary image hardware;
  2. Take the applause of the demonstration as a demand.: The success of crowdfunding only proves that the category can be imagined, but does not prove the delivery, retention and repurchase;
  3. Take "moving" as a selling point: What users really buy is the clip that can be released, not the robot's athletic ability.

Therefore, when evaluating automatic shooting products, priority should be given to delivery, failure recovery, real release and continuous use, rather than tracking the smoothness of the demonstration.

Product research suggestions

  • Compare the fixed camera position, fixed gimbal and mobile camera position in the same scenario to test which version the user is willing to release in the end;
  • Prepare, wait, care, rework and finish all in the cost;
  • Align the category, year, channel and currency of any industry share first;
  • Do not take the manufacturer's parameters, crowdfunding amount or media citation as actual measurement results;
  • The original materials involving specific companies and products will continue to be kept confidential, and only the anonymized structural conclusions will be retained in the public layer.
