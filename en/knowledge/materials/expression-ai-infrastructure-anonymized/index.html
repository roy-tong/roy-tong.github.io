Expression data and behavioral intelligence: products, rights and paid evidence

The expression collection is disassembled into three product lines: film and television performance, body intelligence and model data, explaining customer tasks, acceptance, rights chain and paid evidence.

Core judgment

"Emoji Data" is not a single product. There are at least three types of buyers and three types of delivery:

Product line The result of the buyer's purchase Main acceptance
Film and television and games Acceptable performance minutes or driveable assets Expression restoration, time synchronization, modification cost
Body intelligence Cross-body expression drive and behavior closed loop Calibration, stability, long-term consistency
Model and data authorization Authorization data to fill specific gaps Rights integrity, field quality, traceability

Synthesizing the three lines into a "data platform" will cover up different customers, prices and evidence requirements.

Film and television and games: selling deliverable performances

What the buyer really needs is not the technical word "micro-expression", but the results that can enter the production process:

  • Whether the actor's performance can be collected stably;
  • Whether the data can drive the target role;
  • After the director revises it, can it be partially redon?
  • Whether the lens, time code and assets can be handed over to the downstream;
  • Is the whole process cheaper or more controllable than the existing scheme?

There has been real payment and adoption that can prove the existence of the category, but it cannot prove that the new collection equipment will become the default scheme. Many projects can be completed with traditional performance capture, manual animation or lightweight substitution.

Body intelligence: the problem is true, and the external purchase has not been established.

The body system requires facial movements, emotional expression and human-computer interaction, but "technically useful" does not mean "customers will buy outside". Need to distinguish:

  • Research team: willing to try, budget less, pursue programmable;
  • Robot company: cross-ontology adaptation and long-term consistency are required;
  • Scene operator: care about stability, maintenance and final experience.

The strongest pain points are usually not single collection, but calibration, long-term drift and cross-device consistency. If the product only delivers data files and is not responsible for these engineering problems, the value will be greatly compressed.

Data authorization: rights are costs, not attachments

Data involving faces, voices and expressions include at least:

  • Informed consent and withdrawal of the collected person;
  • The scope of rights of portraits, sounds and performances;
  • Data use, geography, duration and secondary training restrictions;
  • Whether the de-identification is reversible;
  • Cross-border, storage and security requirements;
  • Data deletion and audit.

The chain of rights is incomplete, and no matter how large the amount of data is, it cannot become a tradable asset. The buyer buys legally usable, traceable and acceptable data, not the number of video hours.

The level of paid evidence

  • Research interests and letters of intent do not constitute income;
  • A one-time collection contract is not equal to continuous data service;
  • The income of customized projects is not equal to reusable products;
  • Real renewal, additional authorization and cross-customer replication support platform assumptions.

This page does not publish price, customer and contract information, because these materials may be identified by the combination of size, time and industry, even if the name is removed.

Product suggestions

  • Choose a product line first, and don't serve three buyers at the same time;
  • Use the final delivery to define the collection specifications, and do not use sensor parameters to define the product;
  • Turn the chain of rights, deletion and audit into product functions;
  • Verify payment, acceptance and repurchase for each product line;
  • Make an independent legal, ethical and security review of biometric data.

Border

This page retains the product structure and evidence logic, but removes specific companies, products, budgets, customers, contracts, prices and partners. No reader should infer the data rights or commercial results of an organization from this.
