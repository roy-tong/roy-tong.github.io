Interpretation Limits

  • E0-E2 records reveal activity context, unmet tasks, and substitute friction; they do not prove that glasses are the preferred solution.
  • E3 indicates solution acceptance but not payment. Only E4-E5 are direct willingness-to-pay or paid-behavior evidence.
  • Search-region and community labels are sampling context, not verified respondent residence.
  • Analysis weights balance platform/month or scene/month strata; they are not population weights.
  • Automated labels are discovery aids. Product decisions should use human review of full text and targeted interviews/concept tests.

Updated: 2026-07-12 Asia/Shanghai
Study window: 2023-01-01 through 2026-07-12

Executive Judgment

The previous corpus was useful for product feedback but structurally biased toward people who already knew the AI/AR-glasses category. Product and brand queries could not reveal users who experienced a hands-busy, eyes-busy, sunlight, prescription, accessibility, or safety problem without describing glasses as the solution.

The optimized design therefore separates two questions:

  1. What tasks, pains, and substitute failures exist inside an activity?
  2. What evidence shows that glasses are accepted, purchased, paid for, rejected, or returned as the solution?

The resulting strict 2023+ evidence table contains 209,862 deduplicated full-text records: 74,719 product-feedback records and 135,143 open-scene discovery records. Open-scene volume is not counted as product demand unless the user text reaches the relevant evidence level.

Problems Found In The Previous Data

  1. Keyword-conditioned selection: searches centered on AI glasses, AR glasses, smart glasses, and product names. This favored category-aware users and missed latent jobs.
  2. Platform concentration: Reddit represented 58.2% of the old strict primary sample. The updated strict primary remains concentrated at 57.7%, so pooled unweighted percentages are still not market estimates.
  3. Event-driven time spikes: 2026-06 represented 17.3% of strict primary feedback. Product launches and archive availability can create false trend acceleration.
  4. Owner survivorship: owner and enthusiast communities over-represent retained users; rejectors, returners, bystanders, and users satisfied with substitutes remain under-covered.
  5. Mention-versus-pain ambiguity: lexical labels such as battery or camera previously mixed neutral mentions, requests, and complaints.
  6. Scenario-versus-demand ambiguity: a user needing navigation while cycling does not imply preference for glasses, much less willingness to pay.
  7. Geography over-interpretation risk: search region, platform, language, or a travel community is not verified respondent residence.
  8. Missing-date leakage: logged-in search pages often omit exact dates. These records remain in raw storage but are excluded from the strict 2023+ evidence table.
  9. Record count is not people count: comments, replies, reviews, and posts are observations, not unique users or a population panel.

Optimization Implemented

  • Added 14 open-scene families: swimming/open water, travel/translation, cycling, running, skiing, motorcycle/driving, hiking/climbing, sailing/fishing/diving, accessibility, prescription eyewear, field trades, healthcare/caregiving, parenting, and creator/event use.
  • Collected open-scene Reddit data with equal month and equal scenario quotas across 43 continuous months. Communities sample different thirds of each month to reduce end-of-month and latest-post bias.
  • Added scenario-targeted YouTube, Bilibili, X, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu, Weibo, Instagram, Taobao, and JD collection. Marketplace cards are supply/price signals, not buyer reviews.
  • Preserved full text and source URL while hashing available author identifiers.
  • Enforced strict 2023+ dates in the main evidence table; undated and older records remain only in raw files.
  • Deduplicated by record ID and normalized full-text hash. Final duplicate ID and duplicate text counts are both zero.
  • Added evidence levels:
    • E0: scene context only.
    • E1: unmet task or pain.
    • E2: substitute workaround or failure.
    • E3: glasses-solution acceptance.
    • E4: explicit willingness to pay, or explicit rejection/return.
    • E5: paid owner behavior.
  • Added analysis weights that equalize month/scene strata for discovery and platform/month strata for product feedback. These are design weights, not population weights.

Scenario Interpretation

The existing product-centered scenarios remain commercially strongest because they already contain paid owners: spatial display/productivity, gaming/media, hands-free capture, prescription daily wear, commuting, and travel/navigation. They also carry mature product frictions such as connectivity, comfort, battery, prescription integration, returns, blur, and privacy.

Among the expanded must-wear scenarios:

Scenario Unmet task Substitute friction Acceptance WTP / paid owner Current judgment
Swimming/open water 891 129 21 69 Most promising new category-specific wedge; validate waterproofing, anti-fog, readable pace/navigation, controls, prescription and underwater audio constraints
Travel/translation/wayfinding 884 78 49 88 Strongest broad consumer extension; translation, navigation, photochromic tint, battery and offline reliability must close together
Accessibility/vision/hearing 1,586 166 14 20 High problem intensity but trust, accuracy, affordability and specialist distribution matter more than raw feature count

Ski/snow sports 573 40 3 2 Plausible HUD use but seasonal, safety-critical and low direct demand evidence
Motorcycle/driving 703 61 0 3 Strong task tension but helmet HUD, phone projection and legal/safety concerns may be better form factors

These counts are observational evidence, not conversion rates. Scene labels can overlap and automated coding must be followed by full-text review.

Recommended Decision Process

  1. Use the new table to select 200-400 full-text records per priority scenario, balanced across evidence level, platform, month, and positive/negative stance.
  2. Conduct human coding for job, trigger, current workaround, consequence, desired outcome, solution acceptance, price reference, rejection reason, and safety/privacy constraints.
  3. Run concept tests that compare glasses against the strongest substitute, not against doing nothing.
  4. Validate price with Gabor-Granger or Van Westendorp only after the concept and task closure are concrete.
  5. Treat swimming and travel as separate product propositions. Their optics, waterproofing, controls, audio, battery, tint, certification, and channel requirements differ enough that one generic AI-glasses concept would obscure demand.

Remaining Limits

  • Strict product primary still fails the 200k and single-platform caps; the combined table exceeds 200k because it includes a separately identified open-scene corpus.
  • Reddit remains the dominant discovery platform. Analysis must use platform-aware views and qualitative triangulation.
  • X, Instagram, JD, and verified marketplace buyer-review yields remain low without licensed/API exports.
  • East Asia coverage improved through Bilibili, Zhihu, Xiaohongshu, Weibo, Taobao/JD and Chinese queries, but Japan/Korea local-platform coverage remains thinner than Chinese and English coverage.
  • Willingness-to-pay language is sparse and socially biased. Real payment validation still requires concept tests, preorder experiments, retailer data, or controlled surveys.

1. Acceptance standard

Time window

The main sample is only accepted.created_at >= 2023-01-01T00:00:00+00:00The record of. Lack of time The record can enter the original pool, but it does not enter the trend conclusion by default.

Trend analysis must be seen at the same time:

  • The original primary full amount.
  • feedback_primary_month_balanced_2023plus.jsonlMonthly balance view.
  • Event comments for the month of major events, such as new product release, platform function update, recall, crowdfunding delivery, and privacy disputes.

Content integrity

Each record must keep the complete feedback text:

  • Community comments: complete comment body.
  • YouTube: complete top-level comments and optional replies.
  • E-commerce/App Store: complete review body.
  • Crowdfunding: complete backer/comment/update discussion.
  • Social media: the complete post/comment/reply text allowed in the export.

Only short excerpts are cited in the report; JSONL retains the original text to facilitate secondary coding.

Hard upper limit:

  • The single-source family does not exceed 35%.
  • The single platform does not exceed 30%.
  • A single product does not exceed 30%.
  • When the event is not commented, it does not exceed 12% in a single month.

2. Collect compliance boundaries

Can be used:

  • Official API.
  • Export provided by the platform.
  • Comments that can only be exported by brands/creators/projects.
  • Third-party authorized social media listening or e-commerce comment supplier export.
  • Forums and Q&A stations with no login, no challenge, and public JSON/API.

Don't use:

  • Bypass Cloudflare, CAPTCHA, login wall, paywall or platform access control.
  • Save the original user name, email, phone number, address and other PII to normalized dataset.
  • After making up 200,000 yuan with a single platform, it claims to represent the whole market.

3. Signal extraction process

  1. Raw collection/import: only do source normalization, time, author hash, complete text saving.
  2. Primary build: filter 2023+, weight removal, quality score, relevance score, sample bucket.
  3. Rule labels: product form, price band, scenario, functional appeal, pain point, purchase stage, market area.
  4. Candidate signals: n-gram/keyword open extraction, challenging fixed classification.
  5. Human audit: original sample inspection by source × month × product form × sample barrel.
  6. LLM-assisted coding: Only do finer subject coding on desensitized samples, and keep manual review.
  7. Quant + qual synthesis: counting, trend and representative original text are presented separately.
