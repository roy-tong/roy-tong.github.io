What is this information?

This white paper discusses how Agent can record capability discovery, scheme selection, execution operation, result consumption and business value separately when using software. It is suitable for understanding measurement objects and statistical denominators, and avoiding confusing a call, a task with a business result.

The full text is kept at the original address. The knowledge base adds information descriptions and topic associations, and does not copy the body of the report that needs to be maintained independently.
