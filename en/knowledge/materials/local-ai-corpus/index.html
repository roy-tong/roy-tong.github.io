What does this snapshot fix?

The snapshot fixes a batch of records used by local AI and private computing power research from 2024-01 to 2026-07. The total amount of public layer retention, the count of seven types of sources, time fields, structure check and input snapshot summary; original posts, accounts, user identifications and text-by-article are not published.

Highly concentrated sources: Reddit accounts for 90.21%, and Hacker News accounts for 5.90%. Therefore, it is suitable for finding problems in deployment, configuration, privacy and continuous operation, and not for estimating the market ratio of different groups of people.

Denominator and cross-group repetition

211,088 refers to feedback records, not independent users. The main snapshot does not have an E0–E5 coding field, and the machine label in the downstream file cannot be inverted into the original data fact.

This check does not include fuzzy matching or character association, so the three sets of records can neither be directly added to each other, nor can it be declared to complete the semantic de-weighting.

Where is the review?

Public JSON and CSV can recalculate the source total, proportion and structural quality fields; the input summary is bound to this round of private snapshots through SHA-256. There was no re-crawling this time, and no semantic review was carried out one by one. Complete recalculation still requires legal access to the original snapshot.
