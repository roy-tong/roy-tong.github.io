Overview of the database

The database converts public videos and podcast interviews into retrievable text for research findings, opinion comparison and follow-up verification. The content that has been sorted out is divided into:

Subject Quantity Main research purposes
Body intelligence, robot and physical AI 38 Technical route, product form, deployment lesson and commercialization
Large models, Agent and AI products 15 Model capability, product boundaries, organization and market changes
Other technology and industrial topics 4 Display, brand, hardware and adjacent industry reference
Amount to 57 Topic discovery and interview outline

This is just a database count, not the number of independent respondents, nor an industry sample survey.

Why is the index only public?

The word-by-word draft is a third-party program content, and the public redistribution may exceed the reasonable scope of citation, and may also expose guests, companies or undisclosed information. Therefore, the public layer does not provide:

  • Program name and original link;
  • Guest's name, company and position;
  • Word-by-word manuscript and ASR intermediate documents;
  • The exact date and duration combination of the source may be checked.

The knowledge base only records "what research topics exist, how many materials are available, and how they can be used".

How to use

  1. First, select the research questions from the topic distribution, and do not draw conclusions from the point of view of a single celebrity;
  2. Go back to the authorized original program to check the context;
  3. Separate the interviewee's views, the host's paraphrase and the researcher's inference;
  4. When it involves the company's plan, finance and product route, the second source is required;
  5. When quoting, abide by the copyright rules of the original program and platform, and do not reprint long paragraphs word-by-word manuscripts.

Boundary of evidence

Interviews are suitable for discovering new problems, understanding the decision-making process and technical route differences, but not for estimating the overall proportion. The respondents have choice bias, and the program also has narrative preferences. Multiple programs expressing the same point of view does not mean that it has been independently verified.

Follow-up maintenance

The new transcrassion will first enter the batch to be checked, and after completing the classification, quality inspection and source authorization judgment, it will enter the anonymization index. Unchecked materials are not counted, and do not enter the public research conclusions.
