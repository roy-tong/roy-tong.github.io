1. Hardware products, how to go from good ideas to stable mass production

Organization, process and design management methods in hardware product innovation

This sharing focuses on a question: how good ideas can eventually become products that can be stable and mass-produced through product definition, teamwork and engineering verification. The discussion is based on general methods and combined with the practical experience of mature hardware enterprises.

2. The change of direction in the high-cost stage is the main risk of hardware development.

The first task of organizational management is to push forward key decisions.

The main risk of hardware projects comes from the delay in decision-making. If the size, device, architecture and other key matters continue to the stage of mold opening, certification or stock preparation, any adjustment will bring high rework costs. The role of organization and process is to make key decisions complete when the modification cost is low.

  • It is decided to freeze.: The size, device, mold and structure are more and more difficult to change in the future.

  • Results depend on integration: Product experience is jointly delivered by multiple majors

  • Mass production means commitment.: After the product is launched, quality, inventory, channel and after-sales service jointly bear long-term responsibility.

3. The way of organization depends on the delivery characteristics of the product.

The delivery method, modification cost and customer commitment are different, and the management method is naturally different.

When designing the organization, you should first analyze the delivery method of the product, modify the cost and customer commitment, and then determine the team size and division of responsibilities. Different constraints correspond to different management methods.

4. Intelligent hardware needs to be managed at the same time. Two development rhythms

The hardware is frozen in stages, and the software and algorithms are continuously iterated; the unified architecture, indicators and version plans are responsible for coordination.

The development speed of a single team cannot guarantee the delivery of the whole machine on schedule. The system leader needs to manage the interface between the two sets of rhythms, and clarify the freezing range, the continuous iteration range, and the integration time of the whole machine version.

5. Team configuration should start from three types of key risks.

The early team gives priority to covering the risks that affect the success or failure of the project, and clarifies the person in charge for each type of risk.

The size of the team depends on the key risks that the project needs to deal with. First, list the problems that are most likely to cause the failure of the project, and then assign the person in charge who can make judgments and promote the solution for each type of problem. The technical viability is only a condition for the establishment of the product, and the user value and delivery ability also need to be verified.

Six Product design should run through insight, development and mass production.

The design work starts from understanding the user and continues until the mass production of physical objects is consistent with the design goal.

Design positions should participate from the user scenario and product principle stage, jointly deal with structure, device and cost constraints in engineering development, and be responsible for designing and restoring quality in the mass production stage. If the design intervenes too late, it will significantly compress the adjustment space of the product experience.

Seven Transform aesthetic requirements into enforceable standards

Starting from the intention of users and brands, the standards that suppliers and quality teams can jointly accept are formed layer by layer.

The product manager is responsible for clarifying the user, brand and product intention. The person in charge of design transforms the intention into design principles and schemes, and the engineering, supply chain and quality teams then form measurable and acceptable mass production standards.

8. 0-1 Stage priority to clarify key responsibilities

Usually, six to ten key roles cover the main responsibility, and each result corresponds to a person in charge.

In the early stage, the key responsibilities can be covered by the elite team, and some roles can be also played by the founder. Before entering the project development, the three responsibilities of product, technology and project management should be gradually separated to ensure that there are people responsible for the goal, program and delivery.

9. The size of the team should be adjusted according to the product stage.

Every time the project enters a new stage, the corresponding roles and management mechanisms will be supplemented.

The size of the team should be determined by the product stage. When the project is from verification to mass production, or from a single product to multi-product parallel, the corresponding roles and mechanisms are added. The early introduction of complex processes will reduce the efficiency of exploration, and the late addition of management mechanisms will increase the cost of rework.

10. A mature hardware team needs three organizational lines to work together.

The business and product line are responsible for operation, the professional platform is responsible for building capacity, and the cross-functional project team is responsible for product delivery.

The product line is responsible for the product portfolio and business results. The professional platform is responsible for talent cultivation, standard construction and capacity reuse. The cross-functional project team organizes and collaborates around specific products, and is responsible for the complete delivery from definition to listing.

11. The design level depends on the scope of the problem of independent responsibility.

The length of experience is for reference; the scope of responsibility, the ability to make up and the responsibility of evaluation determine the grade.

The design level should comprehensively evaluate the scope of responsibility such as problem definition, program pick-up, engineering coordination and mass production results. Software proficiency and years of experience can be used as a reference, but it is not enough to determine the grade separately.

12. Select industrial design talents according to product constraints

Different industries form different ability structures. When selecting people, priority should be given to whether the constraints and experience match.

Excellent industrial design talents are distributed in many industries. When recruiting, you should start from the product constraints, evaluate what complex conditions the candidate has dealt with in the past, how to make a make-off, and whether these experiences can be transferred to the current product.

13. The team needs to complete seven key handovers.

Each stage of handover should close a class of risk and form a clear input for the next stage.

The stage gate is used to confirm the processing results of the risk of this stage and provide clear input for the next stage. After the user's evidence meets the requirements, the project is officially launched, and the system baseline is confirmed before entering the mold opening and trial production.

14. First, turn the requirements into a design task that can be reviewed.

The first four assessments gradually transform the requirements into goals, principles, programs and engineering constraints.

The start-up of the project needs to transform the original requirements into goals, boundaries and design principles first, and then form a cross-departmental product baseline through concept review and feasibility review.

15. EVT, DVT and PVT verify three types of problems respectively

Answer different questions in each round of verification, and enter the next round after meeting the evidence standard.

Each of the three rounds of verification has a clear goal. EVT verification engineering realization and system integration, DVT verification experience, reliability and certification, PVT verification of the stable replication ability of the production line. Verify the target to determine the prototype content, evidence requirements and passing criteria.

16. The final decision should be divided according to the type of decision.

User value, design expression, system security and mass production commitment are four different types of decisions.

The final decision needs to be divided according to the type of decision. The person in charge of design maintains the design expression, the person in charge of the product maintains the user value, the person in charge of the system maintains the architecture and performance, and the NPI and quality team maintain the production commitment. Conflicts that cross the boundaries of responsibilities shall be coordinated by the person in charge of the business.

17. A complete demand translation chain is needed between products and research and development.

User language is converted into product requirements, system requirements, subsystem specifications and verification standards in turn.

The product language directly enters into research and development, which will cause the team's understanding of the needs to be biased. The requirements need to be transformed into system requirements, subsystem specifications and verification standards in turn; the person in charge of the system is responsible for maintaining the integrity of this translation path.

18. An efficient R&D team requires three types of ability cooperation.

Hard power solves complex problems, self-drive promotes risk fronting, and the mechanism ensures cross-departmental coordination.

The ability of the R&D team is composed of three aspects: professional ability is responsible for solving complex problems, proactively promoting the early exposure of risks, and the unified baseline, review and upgrade mechanism is responsible for organizing cross-departmental coordination. The three jointly determine the efficiency of R&D and the quality of delivery.

19. The project manager is responsible for coordinating the five delivery rhythms.

The core work is to integrate hardware, software, algorithms, manufacturing and listing into an executable plan.

Hardware, software, algorithms, manufacturing and listing have different rhythms. The project leader is responsible for forming an integrated main plan, keeping cross-departmental dependencies, risks, changes and decisions to be upgraded transparent, and promoting the team to deliver at a unified pace.

20. The priority of resource investment is high impact and high modification cost.

The greater the impact and the more difficult it is to reverse, the more priority should be given to the strongest person and the earliest verification.

The resource allocation of innovative projects should comprehensively consider the impact of risk and the cost of modification. For problems that have a great impact on success or failure and the later the adjustment, the higher the cost. Priority should be given to the most experienced personnel and verified as soon as possible.

21. Design management should evaluate value, quality and delivery at the same time.

The evaluation of the quality of the key evaluation during the exploration period, and the stability of the evaluation results during the mass production period; the weight of the index is adjusted according to the stage.

The evaluation dimension of design management should change with the product stage. During the exploration period, the key evaluation problem judgment and program trade-off; after entering the engineering and mass production stage, gradually improve the weight of node reliability, engineering coordination and mass production consistency.

Twenty-two. Make the three questions clear first.

First, identify the most expensive and most influential organizational problems in the current stage.

Finally, return to the organizational practice of the enterprise itself. These three questions can be used to judge the current stage, responsibility gap and high cost risk, and then enter the question-and-answer discussion accordingly.
