How can this snapshot enter the task research?

The snapshot covers the open scene record from 2023-01 to 2026-08. Its function is to help find clues to family tasks, failures and alternatives, and then return to specific scenarios to make manual judgments; it itself cannot answer whether the family is willing to adopt or pay for a long time.

Among the six types of sources, Reddit accounted for 88.15% and Hacker News accounted for 9.11%. The frequency of occurrence of platform discussion cannot be extrapolated to the distribution of home users, nor can it replace the number of takeovers, maintenance burden and long-term use behavior.

How to deal with labels and old reports

E0–E5 is the candidate result of historical machine processing. This round of labels has been checked, and the products, roles, context and actual behaviors referred to in the statement have not been confirmed article by article, so the conclusion of "demand has been closed" without review in the old report is not followed.

There is a record that lacks the original text hash. According to the standardized identical text check, this group shares 133 texts with the local AI group and 72 with the AI glasses group; this is not character de-weighting or semantic de-weighting.
