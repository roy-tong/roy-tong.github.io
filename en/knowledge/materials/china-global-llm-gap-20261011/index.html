General judgment

There is no single answer to the gap - it is divergating and changing the track:

I. Basic model: from generation difference to monthly difference, 2026 top dual-speed parallel

Anchor points by year (all with sources, please refer to the appendix timeline for details)

  • 2023: GPT-4 (March) MMLU 86.4 vs domestic best (Xunfei Xinghuo) SuperCLUE lags behind ~23pp; Li Yanhong admits that Wenxin is "one or two months" behind, and the external consensus is more than a year.
  • The end of 2024: DeepSeek-V3 (December) MMLU 88.5 ≈ GPT-4o (~88.7), training cost $5.58M (final run only) triggers global revaluation.
  • 2025: o1→R1 only 130 days (AIME 79.8 vs 79.2); Kimi K2 Thinking HLE 44.9% short global SOTA; but Claude Opus 4.5 SWE-bench 80.9% domestic at 65-69%.
  • 2026-10: AA intelligent index Claude Opus 5.5 58 vs domestic best MiMo-V2.6-Pro/GLM-5.3 45-46 (difer 12-14); but SWE-bench Verified top difference 0-3pp (vals.ai: Opus 5 97.0% vs DeepSeek V4 Pro 2nd follow); LMArena difference ~30 Elo (≈2%).

Three sub-conclusions

  1. Polarization of pure ability score difference(High confidence): Saturation benchmark (MMLU/AIME/GPQA) ≈0; Real workload (Terminal-Bench, HLE with tools, long-range agent) The new generation overseas in 2026 (Opus 5.5/GPT-6/Gemini 4) will be double-digit again.
  2. The frontier time difference is reduced from grade to monthly grade.(High confidence, Epoch AI): GPT-4→alignment for 21 months; o1→R1 for 4.3 months; 2025-11 bean bag Seed-Code is even 13 days earlier than Claude Opus 4.5 to take SWE-bench SOTA (manufacturer's caliber). Since January 2026, the strongest open source (mostly domestic) has been behind the closed source frontier for an average of 4 months.
  3. China is clearly leading and expanding in the open source weight.(High trust, HF 2026-08 report): Qwen downloads 2.045 billion times a year, 151,448 derivative models, exceeding the sum of Google+Meta; there is no big version after Llama 2025-04.

II. Model engineering: the total amount of hardware is expanded, and the algorithm and efficiency are overtaken

  • Total computing power: the gap is widening (high confidence). The regulation has evolved from a single-point supply interruption in 2022-10 to a special supply route, which is also difficult to produce (H20 repeated, B30A 2025-11 was blocked, and the Chinese customs stopped $54B orders after 2026-01 H200 authorization); Epoch: Huawei 2026 AI chip production<NVIDIA 4%, the five giants of the United States account for 71% of the world's cumulative AI computing power; SemiAnalysis: China's operating computing power is 24+ GW vs the United States ~56 GW.
  • Self-training ability: narrowing the gap (middle confidence). Pangu Ultra MoE (718B) full-process lifting training; GLM-Image (2026-01) first fully domestic chip training multi-modal SOTA; CloudMatrix 384 with system-level stacking (rack computing power 1.7-2 times that of NVL72) to hedge the single chip ~60% gap; smuggling H100 equivalent to about 660,000 ≈ 1/3 of China's total power (Epoch estimate).
  • The right to speak in algorithm innovation: significantly reduced (high confidence). MLA (DeepSeek V2), fine-grained MoE (V3), NSA/MoBA sparse attention (2025 double original), GRPO (R1) are all pioneered by China and adopted globally - OpenAI gpt-oss-120b adopts fine-grained MoE is a verifible logo; R1 on the cover of Nature (2025-09). The first right of the paradigm (Transformer, o1 test-time compute, native multimodal) is still in the United States.
  • Training efficiency: China is in the lead (high confidence). DeepSeek/Qwen computing power disclosure is transparent (V3 2.78M H800 GPU time; Qwen3 0.718M), GPT-5 has no official figure; China's unit computing power output is higher; the United States will shift the computing power to post-training RL from 2025, and the track itself is changing.
  • Reasoning and deployment: China is leading or equal (high confidence). vLLM/SGLang core developers are mainly Chinese; domestic flagship API $0.1-1/million token vs U.S. flagship $1-15 (1-2 orders of magnitude difference); end-side Qwen is the first in the world; video generation Kling 3.0 blind test exceeds Veo 3.1, HunyuanVideo open source is the strongest (in trust).

III. Application layer: the number of users is shrinking and the revenue is expanding - the largest and still expanding gap

  • User volume: significantly reduced (high confidence). ChatGPT 1.2 billion WAU (2026-09 DevDay)/ Gemini 1 billion MAU vs Doubao 382 million MAU, Qianwen DAU 73.52 million beanbao, totaling 710 million MAU in the top four - from one order of magnitude in 2023 to the same order of magnitude; DeepSeek 2025-01 rushed to 45.41 million DAU in a few weeks to prove the explosive power.
  • Consumption income: expansion (medium-high confidence). ChatGPT 2025 ~8 billion US dollars income; Doubao 2026-05 only pushed the paid file for the first time (68 yuan/month ≈ China's monthly disposable income 1.9%, ChatGPT Plus ≈ United States 0.37%); Kimi 49 yuan/month member's 20-day income exceeds the whole year of 2025 (the absolute value is still small).
  • Coding: expansion (high confidence). Cursor 18 months $100 million → $4 billion ARR, Claude Code $2.5B+, Menlo: enterprise coding annual expenditure of $4 billion, Anthropic accounts for 54%;No domestic coding product has disclosed ARR.(Strongest disclosure = Trae 1.6 million MAU) - The lack of disclosure itself is evidence.
  • Agent Ecology: United States Consolidation (Middle and High Trust). MCP (Anthropic) and A2A (Google) were both launched in the United States and joined the Linux Foundation; OpenAI Dots was directly connected to 1.2 billion WAU; China's highlight Manus 8 months ARR broke the industry record of $100 million, but Meta's acquisition was blocked by Chinese regulation (2026-04) and returned to independence in 2026-08 - the geographical directly rewrites the fate of the unit.
  • Enterprise-level: expansion, maximum gap (medium confidence, large difference in caliber). OpenAI annualized $40-70B (caliber conflict interval) + Anthropic $65B+, both secretly submitted IPO; Alibaba AI revenue annualized ~$7.3B (+45%), Baidu AI quarterly ~$1.9B; Global enterprise GenAI expenditure $37B (Menlo) vs China AI IaaS half a year ~$2.8B (IDC) ≈ 1/10 or less. The price war (from 2024-05, the "Cli Era") is exchanged for the call volume (Volcano 120 trillion token/day, the world's top three) at the expense of customer unit price; from 2025-08, 70% of manufacturers will raise prices, 2026 DeepSeek will increase prices, and OpenAI will reduce the price by 80% - the turning point has begun, but the gap has not narrowed.
  • Structural factors: Record system (868, 2026-04, normalized), payment habits, cloud market structure (API and cloud binding sales vs U.S. independent model factory dual-track), cross-border review (Manus case).

IV. Mechanism interpretation: why is it the "gap of differentiation"

  1. Catch up with the speed>Cutting-edge speed: Domestic 3-7 months round of iteration vs overseas 12-18 months generation - the time difference is bound to converge; but each overseas generation can still rely on paradigm innovation (agent RL, long-term task) to temporarily open up, forming a "serrated convergence".
  2. Control is exchanged for project dividends.: The constraint of computing power forces China to take the lead in terms of architectural efficiency (MLA/MoE/sparse attention) and unit computing power output - which is the direct reason for the reduction of the right to speak of the algorithm in 2024-2026.
  3. The root of the income gap is not in the model in the market.: Pricing right (global price vs price war), payment habits (5 times the difference in revenue), payment subject (U.S. enterprise GenAI expenditure 3.2 times annual increase vs Chinese cloud binding sales).
  4. Open source is China's home.: China is the catcher in the competition at the forefront of closed source, and China is the rule-staker in the competition of open source weight - the two levels should be judged separately.
