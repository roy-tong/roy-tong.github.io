What does this index solve?

A number of long articles also have official account manuscripts, blog manuscripts and research drafts. If you upload them all one by one, a large number of near-duplicate pages will be generated, and the articles will also be mixed with knowledge materials.

The principle of this index is:

  • Only one in-station reading entrance is reserved for the same study;
  • Enter the article/notes/, research materials enter/knowledge/；
  • The rewritten manuscript of the official account is not counted as research materials alone;
  • Customer projects, unpublished materials and internal strategies are not indexed.
