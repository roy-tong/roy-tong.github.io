What's the use of this list?

When you encounter a technical or method judgment from the research notes, you can follow the list back to the paper, public specification or official document to confirm what it supports. Each source has a role and scope description, so as to avoid confusing the introduction of the manufacturer, the experimental results and the author's analysis.

This index is used across topics. A source can be cited by multiple materials. One content is not copied for each topic, and each citation is not counted as a new research product.

Inclusion does not mean endorsement.

The facts in the source only cover the scope of its statement. The introduction of the manufacturer's functions does not prove the general production effect, and the experiments of the research paper cannot be automatically extraplated to other tasks. You still need to check the date, input conditions and restrictions of the original text when using it.

Rights and updates

Links and reading instructions are provided here. Complete third-party reports or video word-by-word manuscripts are not reprinted. The rights of the original text belong to the corresponding author; the website does not extend the license of its own materials to third-party content. When the link is changed, the source is withdrawn or the scope of evidence changes, the relevant research materials need to be revised together.
