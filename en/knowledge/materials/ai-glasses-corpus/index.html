The method of viewing three sets of data, cross-repetition and complete boundaries →

This snapshot is not the same denominator as the old article.

This combination snapshot covers the open scene records from 2023-01 to 2026-07, which is used to check source deviations and historical candidate labels. The previous article used a narrower product feedback screening set; the inclusion conditions of the two were different, and the number of old text samples could not be replaced with 209,862.

20 categories of sources are uneven: Reddit accounts for 83.29%, YouTube accounts for 10.14%, and VITURE public comments accounts for 2.26%. These proportions describe the collection results and do not represent the distribution of device users or potential buyers.

What can the machine label support?

E0–E5 is a candidate label generated by historical processing. This round only reviewed whether the number of labels can be added to the total number of records, and did not confirm the products, roles, behaviors and context in the speech one by one. Therefore, E3 or E5 cannot be directly interpreted as purchase, retention or demand.

Two records lack the original text hash field; "the rest of the hashs are not repeated" is not a semantic re-proof. According to the standardized identical text check, this group shares 104 articles with the local AI group and 72 articles with the home robot group.

Where is the review?

Public documents allow you to recount the summary relationship of source, label and structural quality, and do not include the text, platform account, user identification and original post address collection. The recorded month comes from the field, which does not mean that each month is continuously and evenly covered; the complete boundary can be found inData method。
