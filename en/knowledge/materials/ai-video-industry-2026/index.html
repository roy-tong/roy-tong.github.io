With the rise of General Agent, what is the value of professional video software?

Research time: August 2026

I. Core judgment

In the past three years, the competitive focus of the AI video industry has changed three times.

In 2024, the industry mainly solves the problem of "can the video be generated"; in 2025, the focus will shift to "can the generation results be controlled"; by 2026, the general agent will begin to take over the tool call and software operation. The industry needs to answer a more direct question: when users no longer operate the video software in person, what is the value of professional video products?

Our judgment is that the general agent will take away the user entrance and a lot of operation work, but will not replace the professional video system. Agent can understand tasks, formulate plans, call tools and check results. Real video production still requires professional systems to manage materials, timelines, audio and video tracks, versions, rendering and output.

Five conclusions can be drawn from this:

  1. A large number of functions of traditional video software will be rapidly devalued. Editing buttons, subtitles, filters, transitions, stickers and ordinary material libraries will be covered by automated or generative capabilities.
  2. Professional video software will not disappear, but it will change from a front-end tool directly operated by users to a production system called by Agent.
  3. Industry barriers are shifting from the number of functions to six types of assets: engineering capabilities, project data, brand and character assets, professional judgment, copyright governance and result data.
  4. The general-purpose video Agent, which is only responsible for calling Kling, Veo, ElevenLabs and other models, is most easily absorbed by ChatGPT, Claude and other platforms.
  5. A more valuable entrepreneurial direction is to eat a complete high-value process, such as advertising creativity, intelligent editing, enterprise video, animation production or brand content.

The future software relationship can be summarized as follows: users make demands to the general agent, the agent organizes tasks, and the professional video system is responsible for stable execution and accurate editing.

II. Industry evolution: from generating pictures to completing tasks

2.1 2024: Model solves "Is there a picture"

After the appearance of Sora, Runway, Kling, Hailuo, Vidu and other products, users can directly generate high-quality videos through a sentence or a picture for the first time. At that time, the industry compared the reality of the picture, the naturalness of the action, the length of the video and the ability to understand the prompts.

The typical workflow is very simple: enter a sentence, wait for it to be generated, and extract it again if you are not satisfied. At this stage, AI videos are more like material generators, which can replace part of the shooting, but cannot take over the complete video production.

2.2 2025: The focus of competition shifts to controllability

When users began to use AI videos to produce advertisements, short films and content, a number of practical problems were quickly exposed: characters changed faces across the camera, commodity colors and logo drifted, movements were difficult to control accurately, local modifications often led to the whole segment redoing, and continuous shots were difficult to maintain consistency.

Therefore, model manufacturers focus on supplementing the capabilities of first and last frames, character and product reference drawings, action reference, mirror operation, multi-material input, character consistency, partial modification and video extension. Models such as Runway Gen-4 focus on the consistency of characters, locations and objects across the lens.

AI video thus advances from "generating a moving picture" to "making a usable shot".

2.3 2026: General Agent began to take over the software operation

Codex, Claude Code and other general agents can already call command lines, codes, APIs and external applications. In the video scene, an Agent can read the material, translate the voice, extract the key points, generate the editing scheme, call FFmpeg, make subtitles, find or generate B-roll, soundtrack and output it into a film.

Users only need to propose tasks, for example: "Cut a two-hour interview into a 5-minute video, and then make three short videos of Little Red Book." As for what tools to call, what models to use, and what steps to take, it can be left to the Agent to decide.

This will change the value distribution of video software. In the past, software relied on interfaces and functions to handle users; in the future, users may only face general agents, and professional software will complete production in the background.

III. Which values will be weakened by Agent and generative ability first?

3.1 Software operation knowledge

"Where is the variable speed", "How to automatically add subtitles" and "How to change the horizontal screen to a vertical screen" are all button knowledge. Agent can convert natural language intentions into specific operations. Users no longer need to remember where Ripple Delete is, just say "delete nonsense and make the rhythm tighter".

Functions still exist, but the number of functions and button layout are difficult to continue to form barriers.

3.2 Inventory of ordinary materials

Music, sound effects, fonts, stickers, filters, transitors, templates and Stock Video used to be important assets of editing software. AIGC is changing a considerable part of it from inventory retrieval to on-demand generation: users do not need to find suitable fragments in 100,000 music songs, and can directly request an 18-second background music with a sense of technology but not too intense; city night scenes B-roll can also be generated temporarily.

The value of the material library will not completely disappear, but the focus of competition will shift from quantity to usability: whether the commercial copyright is clear, whether the characters are authorized, what models are used, whether they meet the brand specifications, and who is responsible for disputes. Adobe emphasizes that Firefly's commercial security, enterprise intellectual property protection and Content Credentials are laid out in this direction.

3.3 Single-point AI tools

Automatic subtitles, long video cutting and short videos, automatic matching B-roll, URL to video, automatic soundtrack, broadcast generation, cover generation and horizontal and vertical are all single-point abilities of "input one content and output another content after processing".

General Agent does not need to retrain all models. By calling existing services, these capabilities can be compiled into a longer workflow. Therefore, a large number of AI functional SaaS will become a tool for Agent, instead of a product that users must open separately.

3.4 Pure model aggregation

"Ten models for one member" is still attractive at present, but it is easy to copy. Adobe has integrated a variety of third-party models into creative products, and the universal Agent can also automatically select models according to quality, speed and price.

When users no longer care about whether a shot is generated by Kling, Runway or other models, multi-model aggregation will gradually become the basic function. If Pollo, Medeo, Vidnoz, Clipfly and other products want to establish long-term advantages, they need to continue to delve into advertising, e-commerce, project management or professional workflow.

IV. What does the professional video system still master?

4.1 Stable production project

Video production includes decoding, multi-track timeline, audio and video synchronization, proxy files, caching, color adjustment, mixing, subtitles, keyframes, GPU acceleration, encoding, rendering and multi-platform output. Codex can write FFmpeg commands, but FFmpeg is not equal to Premiere.

A more reasonable division of labor is: Agent decides how to produce, and the professional system ensures that the process is controllable and the result is stable. This is similar to the relationship in software development. Claude Code does not replace Git, Docker, database, compiler and GitHub, but organizes them.

4.2 Complete project records

Behind a video is not only the final MP4, but also the original material, material usage interval, script, subtitles, characters, goods, music, lens, prompt, reference diagram, historical version, modifier, review record and release version.

This information together constitutes the production records of the project. The value of Figma is not that it can draw rectangles, but that the product design is kept in the same collaborative environment for a long time. Video software can also retain users through brand content, products, characters, lenses, versions and production relationships.

TapNow, FLORA, LibTV, Flova and other products continue to strengthen Canvas, project, material library, character library, split frame and version management; Flova has also joined the cross-project Asset Library. For such products, it doesn't matter how many models are accessed, but whether users are willing to keep the complete project in it.

4.3 Professional judgment

It is not difficult to generate a video, but specific criteria are needed to judge whether it is qualified.

Advertising cares about the first three seconds of retention, product exposure, selling point and CTA; interview videos care about information density, paragraph pick-up and rhythm; movies need to deal with cross-axis, emotional continuity and camera connection; animation has another set of production rules.

General Agent can understand the concept of "advertising", but the system with millions of advertisements, delivery results and creative performance data has completely different judgments. The advantage of vertical products is not only that they can be generated, but also to know when to redo, where to change, and how to be effective.

4.4 Accurately modify the interface

Natural language is suitable for expressing intentions, such as "the pace here is too slow" and "let the product appear as soon as possible". It is not suitable for all precise operations, such as "put the cutting point 7 frames ahead" or "reduce the second track by 4dB from here".

Professional video software will not only have chat boxes. It is more likely to retain three types of interactions: dialogue is responsible for expressing goals, canvas and frame are responsible for organizing characters, scenes and materials, and the timeline is responsible for checking and fine-tuning. Runway Agent has reflected this combination: Agent becomes and organizes the video, and the user returns to Timeline to modify the details.

The timeline will not disappear, but it will change from the main production interface to the inspection and revision interface. AI completes most of the work first, and people are responsible for auditing and handling the remaining problems.

4.5 Copyright and Corporate Governance

In addition to caring about the quality of the film, enterprise customers also need to confirm the source of the model, material authorization, character authorization, brand specification, audit process and attribution of responsibility. These requirements are difficult to solve with a one-time model call.

The video system that can provide permissions, audits, copyright certificates and enterprise compliance capabilities still has clear value. The lower the generation cost and the larger the scale of the content, the more important the ability is.

4.6 Result data

Advertising is the scene with the clearest value of this. It is not scarce to simply generate advertising videos; what is more difficult is to judge which content can bring clicks, conversions and ROAS.

If a company masters commodity information, user portraits, historical advertisements, video generation, Meta/TikTok placement data, click rate, conversion rate and ROAS at the same time, it can continuously implement "production-placement-evaluation-re-production". Creatify, Higgsfield and Arcads are worth paying attention to, and the reason is also here: they compete for the judgment of "what kind of video can make money".

According to the data cited in the manuscript, the digital video advertising market in the United States has exceeded $80 billion in 2026, and a large number of advertisers have also begun to use generative AI to produce advertisements. Advertising creativity is therefore one of the clear directions of commercialization at present.

V. How will the general agent and professional software work together?

The future video production is roughly divided into three layers.

The top layer is ChatGPT, Claude and other general agents. They understand user goals, formulate plans, choose tools, check results and initiate modifications.

In the middle are various professional abilities: ChatCut to understand and edit real materials, Runway to generate or modify shots, HeyGen to produce digital characters, ElevenLabs and other services to process sounds, and the advertising platform to provide effect feedback.

The bottom is the production infrastructure such as project, timeline, rendering, asset management and permission governance.

The user mainly operates the Agent, and the Agent then calls the professional software. ChatCut has provided access to its capabilities through ChatGPT and Claude on its official website; Flova has also opened Agent Access, allowing Codex and Claude Code to create projects, promote creation, view status and output videos.

The business logic of SaaS will also change accordingly. In the past, products wanted users to open the App every day; in the future, even if users never enter the interface, as long as the Agent continues to call, the professional software can still retain value. The product needs to support three usage methods at the same time: direct human operation, agent call, and enterprise mass production through API. UI is no longer equal to the product itself.

VI. The new stratification of AI video companies

To judge the position of a company, we can not only look at the quality of the model, but also by which layer it has mastered in the production chain.

6.1 Video Model Company

The representative companies include Kling, Runway, Vidu, Luma, PixVerse, Pika, Hailuo and Wan. They are responsible for improving the quality of video generation and are still at the basic level of the industry, but the business will rely more and more on computing power, data and capital investment.

According to the information cited in the manuscript, Kling has formed an annualized revenue of hundreds of millions of dollars and obtained a high market valuation. Runway is also continuing to raise funds and invest in video and world models. In the long run, this layer may be concentrated on a small number of players who have computing power, data, model, user size and distribution ability at the same time.

6.2 Digital people and identity videos

HeyGen, Synthesia, Tavus and Hedra have proved that the company does not need to have the strongest underlying video model, but can also establish large-scale business around specific needs. They serve "let the same person continue to make videos", and the accumulated assets include image, sound, corporate knowledge, workflow and customer relationship.

According to the data cited in the manuscript, HeyGen has exceeded $200 million ARR, and Synthesia's valuation has reached billions of dollars. The next-generation video model is launched, and these customer assets and business processes will not be automatically taken away.

6.3 AI creative workbench

LibTV, TapNow, FLORA and Krea are trying to become the management environment for AI creation projects. Creators may use ChatGPT, Midjourney, Seedance, Kling, ElevenLabs and Premiere at the same time, and need one place to organize text, pictures, characters, videos, sounds, Prompts, models and versions.

The success or failure of this route depends on the retention of the project, not on the number of model accesses. Adobe, CapCut, Runway and even ChatGPT may expand to this level, and the competition will be fierce.

6.4 AI Video Agent

Flova, Medeo, Pollo Agent, OiiOii, Runway Agent focus on end-to-end tasks. The product experience is in line with the agent trend, but the strategic risk is also the most direct: why can't ChatGPT complete it by itself?

If the product is only responsible for writing scripts, calling Veo and ElevenLabs, and then synthesizing the results, the long-term value is limited. The products that can be left need to continue to master user projects, characters and assets, professional methods, evaluation standards, or cultivate a vertical industry. OiiOii focus animation is a clearer choice than "Universal Video Agent".

6.5 Intelligent editing and generative post-production

ChatCut, Adobe, Runway Aleph, Mirage/Captions deal with another type of problem: understanding the materials that users have taken and giving results that can continue to be edited.

What a large number of professional creators face is not "generating a movie from scratch", but "finding content worth retaining from three hours of material". The system needs to understand what the character said, judge the highlights and repeat Take, form a rough cut, and then give it to the user to continue to modify. This direction is clearly divided with Agent: Agent is responsible for understanding and judgment, and the editing system is responsible for reliable execution.

6.6 Effective advertising

Higgsfield, Creatify, Arcads and Topview have the opportunity to connect content production, release data and the next round of creativity. Real advertising performance data can help the system judge what content is effective and will not automatically disappear with Kling or Veo upgrades.

The goal of such companies should not stay in AI advertising production tools, and it is more suitable to develop into an advertising creative optimization system.

6.7 Multi-model aggregator

Pollo, Medeo, Vidnoz and Clipfly provide users with multi-model entrances, which have short-term traffic value and weak long-term barriers. Once the model selection is handed over to the Agent, the aggregation itself is easy to degrade the basic function. They need to enter deeper business links to avoid being absorbed by the platform.

VII. Product changes in the next 3-5 years

7.1 Migrate the user entrance to the general Agent

In the past, users would open Premiere when they thought of cutting videos; in the future, users are more likely to directly tell ChatGPT what they want to do, and the specific software is selected by Agent. Professional applications should not only serve people, but also be called by Agent.

7.2 Professional software retreats to the background

High-income video software may not need to be familiar to ordinary users. It can obtain call volume through Agent or enterprise API, and undertake project management, rendering, compliance and mass production.

7.3 The timeline becomes the audit interface

AI will complete more and more initial cutting and generation tasks, and people spend time on review, error correction and fine-tuning. This is similar to the changes in the use of IDE by programmers: manual input is reduced, and review and debugging are increased.

7.4 Edit objects from pixels to content

"Select the mask and erase the character" will become "remove the person behind"; "adjust the color temperature and curve" will become "change the time to evening"; looking for materials will become "make up a shot that reflects loneliness".

The objects of video editing will gradually move from frames, pixels and layers to characters, objects, events, emotions and narratives. Products such as Runway Aleph have developed in this direction.

7.5 Prompt and model name gradually exit the ordinary interface

Ordinary users do not need to learn prompts such as "cinematic lighting, 35mm, dolly shot" for a long time, and there is no need to understand the differences between each model. Users only need to provide reference content, products, audience, selling points, duration and release platform, and Agent decides the focal length, Prompt, model and soundtrack.

The model will become more and more like a cloud computing instance. Users are concerned about the quality, speed and price of the result, not the name of the instance.

7.6 Video model develops into world model

The current model mainly outputs fixed video, and the next stage will support perspective change, continuous character action, environmental response and real-time generation. Runway, Luma, Vidu, Odyssey, Decart and other companies are exploring interactive worlds and world models.

Video generation may be just a commercial application for the early landing of the world model.

7.7 Video changes from fixed files to dynamic content

Future advertisements can change in real time according to region, actors, audience, inventory and CTA. The same content no longer corresponds to a fixed MP4, but more like a dynamically generated program. The boundaries between videos, advertisements, games, interactive content and software will continue to blur.

7.8 Judgment ability and professional skill appreciation

After the cost of video generation decreases, the content supply will increase rapidly. Scarce resources will shift from production capacity to selection ability: what is worth shooting, which section should be kept, which idea is better, and which advertisement will make money.

The experience of directors, editors and advertising creators will also further change from personal experience and templates to Agent's callable Skill. A valuable Skill should not only prompt, but also include input analysis, script splitting, lens selection, redo conditions and quality judgment. A new market for creative skills may be formed around this kind of ability.

VIII. Investment and product judgment framework

8.1 Priority direction

Advertising creativity and effect feedback.Higgsfield, Creatify and other companies are close to the growth results, and customers buy advertising effects, not just video output.

Intelligent editing and generative post-production.ChatCut and Runway Aleph handle the understanding and reliable modification of real materials, and the demand will not disappear because the generation model becomes stronger.

Digital identity and corporate video.HeyGen and Synthesia can accumulate characters, voices, corporate knowledge and workflow, and the business model has been verified.

AI creative workbench.LibTV, TapNow and FLORA have the opportunity to become places for long-term preservation of projects, but they will also directly face competition from Adobe, CapCut, Runway and General Agent.

8.2 High-risk direction

Single model generator.Product advantages are easy to disappear with the upgrade of the upstream model.

Pure multi-model aggregator.If the main value is to reduce account registration, it is easy to be replaced by the platform or Agent.

Universal Video Agent.Without projects, assets, industry data and professional judgment, it is difficult to form barriers just by composing other models.

Single-point AI function.Subtitles, long to short, B-roll and other capabilities are most likely to be built into the platform.

8.3 Seven judgment questions

To evaluate an AI video company, you can ask seven questions first:

  1. Why can't ChatGPT do this directly?
  2. What long-term assets will users leave behind: characters, brands, goods, historical projects, or leave after generation?
  3. Does the product manage the complete project, not just output MP4?
  4. Can the results continue to be edited and enter the professional production process?
  5. Does the product master the judgment of advertising, animation, editing or film and television and other industries?
  6. Can it obtain result data such as advertising conversion, user retention, course completion rate, etc.?
  7. Does it provide stable Agent and API call methods?

Conclusion

The AI video industry has moved from "can the model generate images" to the stage of "how the production process is reorganized around the Agent".

Buttons, filters, templates, ordinary materials, single-point AI functions and model aggregation will continue to depreciate. The more difficult parts to copy are project and version records, brand and character assets, stable video execution systems, professional judgment, copyright governance and result data.

The general Agent will master the entrance and operation rights, while the professional video software needs to occupy the ability of the Agent that will not be redeveloped and difficult to copy easily. More opportunities to grow into large companies' products usually do not stay in "better-to-use video generators", but choose a high-value scenario to connect creativity, materials, generation, editing, delivery and result feedback into a complete process.

Therefore, when observing companies such as LibTV, TapNow, ChatCut, Flova, Higgsfield, Creatify, OiiOii, etc., the focus should not be on what AI functions have been added to the interface, but on which position it eventually occupies in the video production chain, and whether this position is difficult to replace.
