AI terminal and new interaction

This topic is being questioned.

For which task does the new terminal form create value that existing equipment does not have?

Cover: New terminals such as local computing, wearable and spatial interaction, under what conditions of use create value that existing devices do not have. Physical comfort, maintenance and permissions are all considered as conditions of use.

How to read: Starting from the problem of "Local AI" - when the task is established, the device has a reason to buy; then look at the wearable device, spatial interaction, and the complete cost comparison with existing computers and shared devices.

This is the original research field catalog. Please refer to the report and data inThe new version of the databaseCheck it out.

    Local AI: After the task is established, there is a reason to purchase the equipment.Starting point

    The content of notesDisassemble offline, privacy and computing power into task conditions, and then compare the complete cost of existing computers, shared devices and dedicated hardware.

  2. 02

    AI wearable: available time is more important than the function list

    The content of notesStudy glasses, headphones and other wearable devices according to body position, input and output conditions and daily use resistance.

    Spatial interaction: the object pointed to needs to be operated continuously.
