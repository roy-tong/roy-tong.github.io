World model: what do pictures, states and actions prove?

Use the output contract to distinguish between video generation, computable simulation and action planning, and avoid using visual effects as physical abilities.

Author's Analysis · Content Date2026.08.31 · Description of inclusion and citation

The "world model" is suitable for describing the research direction, but not enough to define a product acceptance list. It is also a video of the cup being pushed. One system may only generate the picture, the other may save the state of the calculable object, and the third will select the robot's action accordingly. The mistakes of the three will cause different consequences.

First, ask what the downstream got.

In June 2026, World Labs proposed to distinguish between renderer, simulator and planner by function. I use this classification to help analyze the product; it is a proposal of a research team, not a unified standard.The original

Things obtained downstream My acceptance problem
Picture and sound Is the lens continuous, is the specified object consistent, and is the modification controllable?
Commasable scene status Can scale, collision, material and state changes support target calculation?
Action or sequence of action Under a given observation and limitation, can the task be completed safely and can the failure be identified?

This table is not a model ranking list. A system can undertake several responsibilities at the same time, or assign responsibilities to different modules.

A clear conversion between viewable and available is needed.

The video gives a possible future, but the robot also needs the coordinates, time, strength of the action and new observations after execution. Three-dimensional scenes can support roaming, and may not contain material parameters suitable for contact tasks. When connecting the results downstream, it should be clearly written what is retained, what is estimated, and which quantities do not exist at all.

For example, the generation scene is used for robot training, at least check the proportion, collision geometry, action interface and task result definition. The focus here is to check the training contract of the project itself, not to require each entertainment content to have the accuracy of engineering simulation.

What kind of gap does the data value come from?

Real videos can enrich the knowledge of appearance and events; the robot trajectory with action and results allows specific execution conditions to enter the data. DROID and OpenVLA show the specific path of real machine data, task adaptation and strategy learning, and cannot jump to "do housework after watching Internet videos".DROID、OpenVLA

When evaluating new data, I will compare the failure types on the same set of tasks: whether the object is not recognized, slips when touching, failure is not detected, or the next step is wrong. The scale of data can only be meaningful to the product if it falls on these differences.

The judgment reserved at present

Generation, simulation and planning may provide training signals to each other, but "possible integration" cannot be regarded as evidence of general action capabilities. The industrial discussion in the white paper is divided into three questions that can be verified separately: whether the results are controllable, whether the status is credible, and whether the actions are reliable. They also connect creative software, space tools and robots respectively.
