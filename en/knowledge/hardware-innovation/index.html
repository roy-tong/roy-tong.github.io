Hardware innovation: organizational decision-making according to irreversible input

Let value verification, system integration, supply chain and business commitment connect, and avoid the success of the prototype as a product.

Author's Analysis · Content Date2026.08.31 · Description of inclusion and citation

Intelligent hardware undertakes two types of changes at the same time: software can still be updated, but devices, structures, molds and inventory may have been locked. The organizational mechanism needs to be built around these different change costs.

The original article is developed according to the founder type, team stage and management prototype. The more general main line is reserved here: what kind of evidence should be supplemented before each expansion of irreversible input.

The stage gate should decide the input, not just check the progress.

The technical prototype proves that the key ability can be done, the user prototype helps to judge whether the results are important, whether the engineering verification checks the design can be realized, and the trial production and mass production verify the delivery conditions. Different products will be arranged at different stages, but the responsibilities of evidence should not be mixed together.

After the demonstration is successful, it is also necessary to ask what conditions it depends on: who prepares the environment, who intervenes when it fails, where the parts come from, and what is the cost of replacement and manufacturing. The hidden work of the demonstration often reappears at the time of delivery.

The three responsibilities must be borne by someone.

The product leader explains the value boundaries of the user and the current version; the system leader turns the goal into indicators and interfaces that can be met at the same time; the project leader deals with interdisciplinary dependencies, integration plans and risk escalations.

These are responsibilities, not stipulations that all small teams must immediately recruit three full-time positions. It's okay to work concurrently, but the key make must be made clearly by someone, and it can't be suspended between multiple departments.

The team size given in the original text is suitable as a planning example for a specific complexity, and should not be used as a general number of people in the industry. This page does not follow a set of fixed compilation.

Freeze the range while retaining the changed path.

Freezing is not to prohibit learning, but to explain what is the baseline of this round of delivery. The new requirements should show the impact on cost, cycle, quality and confirmed experience, and then decide to include in this round, deal with it in the next round or abandon it.

Software updates are not a free remedy. It may affect energy consumption, performance, safety and after-sales service; if the original hardware capacity is insufficient, the update cannot replenish the device or heat dissipation space out of thin air.

From one product to multiple products

The premise that the reuse platform is worth investing in is that different projects repeatedly need the same ability and can accept common boundaries. Too early unification will limit exploration, and too late unification will cause duplicate construction.

Business responsibilities also need to cover post-listing services, repairs, inventory and life cycles. Celebrating only when the press conference is completed will make the team underestimate the work that still has to be undertaken after being delivered to users.

I will use a question to check whether the organization is really mature: after the key person in charge leaves a meeting, can the team still deal with changes, expose risks and complete trade-of-malls according to the common baseline? If not, the organization may still rely on individual coordination to maintain it.
