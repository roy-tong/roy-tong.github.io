Dexterous operation: how hands, touch and data constrain each other

Evaluate the dexterity with the target task and the observable contact process, and avoid the jump between the degree of freedom, the number of data hours and the value of the platform.

Author's Analysis · Content Date2026.08.31 · Description of inclusion and citation

Dexterous operation simultaneously faces the shape, contact, friction, deformation and control errors of objects. One hand can pose a lot, which does not mean that it can operate the target object stably; a batch of videos looks rich, but it does not mean that it provides the action information needed for training.

My research focuses on the adaptation between hardware, perception, data and tasks. The non-public routes and parameters of specific companies do not enter this public framework.

Define the task space first, and then discuss the degree of freedom.

There are different requirements for grabbing rigid boxes, plugging and unplugging wire bundles, screwing lids and finishing fabrics. When evaluating, first write down the changes of the object, the conditions of success, the allowed time-consuming and the consequences of failure, and then compare the claws, the driver hands and the hands with a high degree of freedom.

Increasing the degree of freedom may expand the reachable action, and also increase the driving, control, fault point and learning space. Its benefits should be reflected in target task coverage or reliability improvement, and should not be proved only by appearance close to human hands.

What does the data need to record?

Data source What can be provided? What can't be made up directly?
Ordinary video Semantics, objects and action clues Actuator state, contact force, executable action
The first perspective of human beings Actual work situation and behavior changes The correspondence between human hands and robot actions
Real machine remote operation Observation, robot status, action and results Collection cost, coverage deviation, demonstration quality
Simulate Controllable conditions and a lot of trial and error Unmodeled materials, contacts and real differences
Actual deployment Target tasks, failures and manual intervention Automatically form a high-quality training set

DROIDPublicize the cross-environment operation data and the collection system together;OpenVLAProvide the research path of the visual language action model. Both show that data and models can be specifically checked, but it cannot be judged that a hand or a family task is available based on the scale of the data set alone.

The value of touch should fall on the problem of contact.

Vision mainly helps to locate and recognize, and force and touch can supplement the information after contact. However, what different sensors can measure, how loud the noise is, how to cababrated, and how long they can be used are not consistent.

The presence of tactile sensors does not mean that the strategy makes effective use of touch. When verifying, the visual baseline of the same task should be compared with the performance after adding tactile, and the conditions of sliding, occlusion, material change and sensor degradation should be retained. Additional maintenance and system costs should also be included.

When can data infrastructure become a platform?

If a certain hardware has a stable interface and the ability to supply and maintain in bulk, algorithms and data have the opportunity to accumulate around it. This platform also requires cross-customer reuse, independent developer adoption and continuous task effect to verify.

Selling many hands and forming a general skill ecology are separated by data protocols, annotations, evaluations and rights boundaries. The next step worth tracking is the effect of a skill change of hands, objects and venues, not just how many new data hours are added.
