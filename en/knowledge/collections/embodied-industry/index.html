Stage judgment

What can be confirmed now?

Demonstration, pilot and continuous delivery are three different pieces of evidence. Industry judgment should first explain who the robot completes and what tasks, and then compare the form, technical route and company location.

  • The same technology has different proof responsibilities in researching prototypes, pilot equipment and scale delivery.
  • The degree of freedom, model scale and data volume cannot exceed the success rate and delivery conditions of the task to directly prove the value of the product.
