Stage judgment

What can be confirmed now?

New equipment needs to meet three conditions at the same time: high-frequency tasks, physical acceptable and existing equipment is inconvenient to complete. The combination corpus can find scene clues and cannot replace real behavior and evidence.

  • The function list cannot replace the available moment; the input and output conditions and physical resistance determine whether the equipment can exist for a long time.
  • The product feedback subset of historical articles and the combination snapshot are not the same denominator, and the numbers cannot be directly interchanged.
