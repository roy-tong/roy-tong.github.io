What can be confirmed now?

Whether the family task is established depends on whether the labor saved is greater than the cost of deployment, maintenance and rescue. Platform discussion can expose task clues, but cannot directly prove the purchase demand.

  • The same family task should record the executor, beneficiary, takeover and maintainer at the same time.
  • The number of records is not the number of people, nor is it an independent demand; the concentration of sources must be disclosed together with the conclusion.
