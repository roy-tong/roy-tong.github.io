Local AI and personal computing power

What tasks really need to be run locally, and should you choose an existing computer, shared device or dedicated hardware?

Let's see first: Local AI and personal computing power · corpus summary Screen 5 pieces of materials in this file

Stage judgment

What can be confirmed now?

First prove that the task must continue to occur locally, and then compare the performance, permissions, maintenance and manual intervention. The existing computer is the default alternative, and the dedicated device should prove the incremental value of the complete workflow.

  • Running the model offline does not mean that the entire Agent workflow does not leave the device.
  • About 90% of the public corpus comes from Reddit, which is suitable for finding problems and does not represent the overall market distribution.
Existing materials

Local-AI and Agent-context notes, a 211,088-record summary, and a desensitized user study of private edge compute.

Evidence to be supplemented

Compare equipment by actual workload, and supplement the installation, maintenance, failure recovery and long-term use evidence of non-technical users.
