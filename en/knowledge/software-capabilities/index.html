Software restructuring and capability economy

Interface, callability, delivery assets and economic value are four different objects; judge who has mastered what respectively.

Author's Analysis · Content Date2026.08.31 · Description of inclusion and citation

When the user hands over the task to the Agent, the interface of the software may no longer be the entrance that must be used every time it is used. However, the use of the interface is reduced, and the software cannot be launched directly. It has no value. Databases, rendering, payment execution, proprietary data and on-site services can be used through different entrances.

My long-term judgment is that some software will be re-divisioned around the callable ability. When judging a company, it is necessary to look at how the ability is discovered and whether what it eventually delivers is scarce.

Take apart the four objects.

Object Example Questions to be answered
Entrance App, web page, dialogue, CLI Who receives the user's target?
Port API、MCP、SDK、Skill How to call the external system?
Deliver assets Data, execution system, permissions, customer processes Can it be copied by changing the interface?
Economic results Paid use, attributable income, performance cost Who paid and why did you renew the fee?

Skill can describe the operation method, MCP can expose tools, and CLI can facilitate determinism. They do not automatically generate exclusive data, customer trust or continuous income. Conversely, services with simple interfaces may also have irreplaceable delivery capabilities.

The basic status of the product should be read and modified.

In AI creation tools, only one exported file is usually not enough. It is necessary to continuously save the relationship between characters, materials, versions, authorization and modification. In the enterprise process, it is necessary to retain orders, approval status, participants and irreversible actions.

This makes "semantic objects and their history" an important product asset. People understand and revise through the interface, and Agent operates through the interface. Both should face the same state. You can't give Agent another set of background shortcuts without permission constraints and out of sync with the human interface.

Distribution, use and value verification separately

The installation instructions enter a certain environment; the display instructions enter the selection range; the selection indicates that a decision occurs; the call indicates the execution begins. After the task is completed, it is also necessary to judge whether the result is accepted and whether it is more cost-effective than the alternative.

Therefore,Competency catalogResponsible for discovering,Special topic of ability economyMaintain the argument,AgentMeasureResearch and measurement. Catalog inclusion cannot be used as usage, let alone constitute investment or procurement recommendation.

Counterexamples that need to be retained

Products with high-frequency interaction, precise control, multi-person collaboration and strong brand relationships may continue to have important human entrances. Some enterprises would rather let Agent work in the existing software than give the goal to another platform.

Therefore, "All Apps Will Disappear" is too radical. All software and chat boxes can adapt to changes" also lacks an explanation. Specific workflows should be studied one by one: which operations can be passed, and which statuses and responsibilities still need to be undertaken by the professional system.

The observation cycle should focus on real customer tasks. If there are no more accepted results of calling growth, or if the increase in revenue comes entirely from higher retry costs, the value hypothesis of the ability economy cannot be confirmed.
