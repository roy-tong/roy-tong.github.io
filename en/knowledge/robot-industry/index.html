Intelligent Industry Map: Tasks, Technology and Business Maturity

Separate the ontological form, technical route, company location and delivery evidence, and avoid answering different questions with the same list.

Author's Analysis · Content Date2026.08.31 · Description of inclusion and citation

"Humanoid", "VLA", "Dexterity", "Family Service" and "Financing" are often placed on the same industrial map, but they are body form, model method, component, scene and capital event respectively. When researching, we must distinguish the level before we can judge what has changed a piece of news.

This map reorganizes the definition and framework in the previous white paper. It serves product and business judgment, and does not rank the company without unified calibration verification.

An industrial map requires two perspectives.

The manufacturing perspective is developed along parts, ontology, integration and service, which is suitable for the study of cost, supply and responsibility. The perspective of the learning system tracks how experience is converted into ability:

Link Key target Facts that should be checked
Experience Remote operation, first perspective, simulation, deployment record There is no action, result, failure and usage permission
Model Perception, VLA, world model, strategy Whether the training and test conditions match the target task
Carry out Planning, control, hands and sensors Contact, real-time and abnormal stop
Product Ontology, interaction, maintenance plan Can you undertake the agreed tasks for a long time?
Lay out On-site process, delivery, after-sales Can new customers reuse their existing capabilities?

The two diagrams complement each other. The improvement of model performance does not mean the improvement of the whole machine delivery, and the increase in shipments does not guarantee that the new data can enter the training.

The body is a decision-making task.

For the mobile operation in the leveling site, the wheeled platform is worth comparing with the two-footed; the complex ground inspection needs to examine the passability of the four-leged; the fixed workstation operation needs to be compared with the fixed robotic arm and the special device first.

These are engineering choices, which cannot be written as "a certain body is always better". After the stairs, range of action, load, energy consumption, maintenance and personnel coexistence conditions change, the choice will change. The compatibility potential of humanoid tools also needs to be realized by specific tasks.

Business maturity requires observable progress.

Tracking along "controlled presentation - customer pilot - repeat delivery - continuous operation - repurchase expansion" is more explanatory than mixing papers, orders and revenue together. At each step, ask whether there are independent customers, how much manual support there is, and whether the acceptance conditions are met.

Platformization is no longer the highest level of this maturity ladder by me. A narrow task device without developer ecology may also have mature profits; an open SDK research platform may also have no duplicate customers. Platform structure and business maturity are two dimensions.

Write the degree of autonomy into the economic account.

The effective task cost of the unit should cover depreciation, deployment, manual takeover, maintenance, consumables and software, and the denominator should adopt the qualified tasks recognized by the customer. The statistical caliber must indicate the running time, task difficulty and failure handling.

"Autonomous effective working hours" is suitable for observing trends, but it is not a value indicator common to all tasks. One hour of slow completion and high-speed completion cannot be directly compared; for some tasks, the qualified number of pieces or abnormal disposal effect is more important.

The next research focuses on the evidence of long-term work in the same task and environment, and whether the delivery of new customers is becoming more and more labor-saving. In the absence of this evidence, the judgment of technical progress should be retained and the conclusion of business scale should be suspended.
