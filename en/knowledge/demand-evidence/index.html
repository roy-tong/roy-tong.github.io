Demand evidence: What decision can a feedback support?

Retain the connection between scenarios, alternatives, acceptance conditions and behaviors, and do not let the corpus scale pretend to be demand verification.

Author's Analysis · Content Date2026.08.31 · Description of inclusion and citation

The more information, the easier it is to mistakenly write "find a lot of relevant discussions" as "requirements have been verified". The key action of the research is to let each material only bear the judgment of its actual support.

SURE is the method and tool I use to sort out this matter. It distinguishes between scenarios, problems, alternative behaviors, program acceptance and business behavior, and requires evidence to return to the source. The software is responsible for checking the structure, and the researcher is responsible for explaining the semantics and making decisions.Project and agreement

The level of evidence is not the ladder of market maturity.

Rank Recorded content It can't be launched directly.
E0 Activities, characters, scene backgrounds There are unmet needs.
E1 Clarify tasks, difficulties or goals Willing to change a solution
E2 Current practice, detour, failure or switching cost Accept the new program in the research
E3 Acceptance or preference for the plan under given conditions Purchased or continuously used
E4+ / E4− Commercial intention / Rejection, cancellation, return, etc. Everyone has the same will.
E5 Paid holding, deployment, reuse and other behaviors that have occurred These behaviors are caused by a certain function of the product.

The problem chain, scheme chain and behavior chain in the same role, scene and task can be connected for judgment. Putting together A's complaint, B's payment and C's long-term use will create a "complete user" that does not exist at all.

First, compare how users can solve it now.

"I want a robot to help me" may just express the desire for results. How do users deal with this matter, how often, what to pay, and why the old plan is not enough, so that the new product has a comparison target.

The satisfactory alternative plan should be retained. It may indicate that the market has a good enough answer, or it may indicate that the new plan needs to cross the threshold too high. Only receiving complaints will turn the research into a library of materials to prove the established plan.

Automatic coding can only produce candidates to be reviewed.

The "return" in a public discussion may refer to the return or the return of the program; "sweeper bought for parents" cannot be directly turned into evidence of the purchase of a general care robot. The price may also be other products, services or unrelated expenses.

In this round, three groups of corpus recounted to verify the number of records, fields and source distribution, and did not confirm the machine labels one by one. See the statisticsData page. The CLI inspection pass and the research conclusion pass, which are two different acceptances.

Let the research decide the next action.

For a scenario to be developed, I hope to study and deliver four things: the strongest supporting evidence at present, the strongest counter-evidence, the threshold of judgment that has not been crossed, and the next verification that can change the investment decision.

If what is missing is the acceptance of the program, design an understandable prototype; if the missing is the delivery cost, make a pilot with complete support records. Continuing to expand the background corpus usually cannot make up for these two types of gaps.
