Talents and industry diffusion: After the resume label, it depends on how the ability is transferred.

Separate the entrepreneurial list, organizational experience and industry results, and avoid the reasons and success rates of a few successful companies pushing back the flow of talents.

Author's Analysis · Content Date2026.08.31 · Description of inclusion and citation

Adding the "department" of a large factory to the start-up company is convenient for dissemination, and it is also easy to press the complex experience into a label. Also from an enterprise, formal employment, internship, consultant, cooperation and spin-off are not the same relationship; founders and ordinary team members are not the same statistical subjects.

The most valuable improvement in this round of local list review is to separate these relationships first and then discuss how the capabilities may spread. The refining method here does not republish the resumes and business data that have not been verified one by one into a factual list.

First, make sure what you are counting.

Companies, brands, startup projects and founders may appear in the same table. A person who starts a business many times will generate multiple projects, and a company may operate multiple brands. The number of nodes on the same relationship diagram cannot be directly rewritten as "how many leavers".

The year of establishment is not equal to the year of leaving. The financing stage, cumulative financing, valuation, crowdfunding and income each answer different questions, and cannot be directly added up for a highly communicable total number.

The resume is just the starting point of research.

I am more concerned about which ability to migrate: system integration, supply chain organization, quality judgment, product definition, channel management, or teamwork. They need to be judged by actual products and delivery records, and cannot be confirmed by the name of the former employer alone.

After leaving the original organization, the new team may absorb the experience of other companies, academic institutions and partners. Attributing all the results to a former employer will miss this part of the accumulation.

Successful cases cannot give a success rate.

It is easier for the media to report on financing, shipments and growth, and it is more difficult for silent, stopped and shifted projects to be on the list. Only visible cases are collected, and there is no denominator to calculate the failure rate, exit rate or entrepreneurial success rate.

Even if many items on the list appear at a similar time, it is necessary to check whether the collection focuses on searching for recent news. The search window of researchers may create a seemingly obvious wave of entrepreneurship.

From industrial research to organizational design

If an ability can be repeatedly brought into different tasks, it is worth being used as an object of organizational construction. The next step should be to study how to train this ability, which roles to share, what supply chains and systems to rely on, and which parts will fail after leaving the original organization.

This question is more useful than copying the organization chart of a star enterprise. It also connects the hardware approach of this knowledge base: the migrable ability ultimately needs to be expressed in judging quality and repeated delivery, not the length of the talent list.

The follow-up article can be developed along "How to migrate engineering capabilities across categories". Citation cases still need to be checked one by one before public disclosure, and the caution of the method should not be offset by the certainty in the title.
