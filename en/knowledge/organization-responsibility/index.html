Organizational responsibility: repair the interface first, and then judge who is not suitable

The legitimacy of the organization is placed on the time of decision-making records, cross-team commitments and bad news, and does not simplify trust into satisfaction.

Author's Analysis · Content Date2026.08.31 · Description of inclusion and citation

The organization chart answers who reports to whom, and does not necessarily answer who is responsible for a cross-team result. If product commitments, system limitations, research and development schedules and customer expectations do not recognize each other, each department may complete the work according to its own goals, but the whole will continue to miss the appointment.

To deal with this kind of problem, we should first find the breakpoint of responsibility, and then judge how the structure or personnel need to be changed.

In addition to the appointment, the record of joint work is also required.

In the original article, I used "position legitimacy" and "organizational legitimacy" to distinguish the power brought by the appointment and the judgment trust formed by the team in the joint work. This is a management practice framework, not a new academic definition.

Judging trust does not require everyone to agree with the result. It requires the team to know the basis for the decision, clear the take-over, the error correction path and the review conditions, and also needs to see the promise fulfilled.

The new person in charge doesn't have to choose between observation and action.

The expanding explicit risk should be controlled immediately. Reversible and low-risk process improvements can also be done as soon as possible. The threshold of evidence should be raised for restructuring, key personnel and strategic shifts.

"Learn for three months first" or "change people immediately after take office" cannot constitute an independent method. The schedule should serve risks and evidence, not to perform a certain management style. The 90-day schedule in the original text is reserved here as an example and is not set as a universal deadline.

The four interfaces affect the results before the new organization chart.

Whether the goal describes the same result; who makes the decision in the end; whether the review allows the exposure of unresolved problems; and whether the cross-team commitment includes quality, time and change.

If the project manager only has the right to urge and does not have a path to escalate risks, adding meetings cannot solve the problem. If the product scope continues to change and no one bears the cost, a new person in charge of research and development may continue to be postponed.

Of course, the clear interface cannot cover up the real ability, integrity or will. Systematic analysis helps to make fairer judgments, which does not mean the indefinite postponement of necessary personnel decisions.

Check changes with behavioral signals

I will observe whether the bad news appears earlier, whether the objections enter the formal discussion with evidence, whether the general differences are no longer escalated to the top person in charge, and whether the team can continue to use the same set of standards when the person in charge is not present.

These signals themselves still need to be understood in combination with business results. Fewer upgrades may mean that the authorization is valid, or it may mean that the problem is suppressed. It is necessary to check risk exposure and delivery quality at the same time to avoid replacing one management slogan with another local indicator.

This page only sorts out the methods in the public management article, and does not include personnel information, internal evaluation or undisclosed decisions of specific organizations.
