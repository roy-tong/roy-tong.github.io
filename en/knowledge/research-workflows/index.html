Research Notes · Continuous Revision

Connect the research workflow to the inspectable product.

Use iRead to find changes, use SURE to organize evidence, and use knowledge pages to maintain judgment; tools and research conclusions are responsible for each.

Author's Analysis · Content Date2026.08.31 · Description of inclusion and citation

The research task is most likely to stop at "reading a lot of materials and writing a report". The next time the problem occurs, the source in the report is expired, the number can't be recalculated, and the new Agent doesn't know which judgments have been denied.

I prefer to organize the things left over from the research into source records, evidence tables, current judgments and verification products. Tools can be different, and these types of objects need to be able to locate each other.

What are the existing projects responsible for?

Project Input and product I'm not responsible for proving anything.
iRead Fields and audited sources; events and reports There are many reports that do not prove that the trend is valid.
SURE Decision-making issues and feedback evidence; study contracts, coding and audits CLI does not prove that the market demand is real.
Video transcription tool Media approved for processing; checkable verbatial manuscripts Transcription is not equal to the factual verification of the original author, nor does it grant the right to republish.
AgentMeasure / Lab Operation log or experimental design; caliber and results The success of the assembly line does not prove the real business benefits.
Open knowledge base Organized analysis, citation and data description Do not undertake the full backup of the private original

These projects now have independent entrances. What is described here is a collaborative method that does not claim that they have formed a complete platform that runs automatically and does not require review.

An update should change the clear object.

When new information comes in, first judge whether it is another report of the old event, new facts, contrary evidence, or a statement that needs to be further examined. Only the materials that change the research problem are worth updating the subject text.

Update a judgment record: what is the old conclusion, which premise has been changed by the new evidence, and which parts are still valid. The original text can be checked back, and the current knowledge page gives the maintained version. In this way, it can be admitted that the past judgment was limited without erasing the basis at that time.

The boundary of automation

Instructions may be included in documents and sources, and tools cannot be authorized to act just because they appear in research materials. Publishing, sending letters, deleting, paying and device control need to be authorized by the task itself.

Data de-weighting, format verification and indicator re-counting are suitable for automation. Whether the source is independent, which product is supported by the comment, and whether a concept is commercially meaningful, need to be reviewed semantics; research tools should expose these problems.

How to accept this workflow

Choose a topic that has an old conclusion, and follow the whole process with a new counter-evidence: whether to find the affected knowledge page, update the data caliber, keep the original link, and let the related article prompt readers to check the latest judgment.

If only the number of documents and abstracts is increased, and the retrieval and review costs required for the next judgment are not reduced, the research workflow has not completed its task.
