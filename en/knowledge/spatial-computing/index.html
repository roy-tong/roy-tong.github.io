Spatial interaction: the object pointed to needs to be operated continuously.

From "seeing, pointing, pinching, saying" extends to object identity, execution confirmation and cross-step status, space display is not equed with complete interaction.

Author's Analysis · Content Date2026.08.31 · Description of inclusion and citation

In the original article, I used "look, point, pinch, say" to describe a possible spatial interaction syntax. This is a product judgment, not a paraphrase of the existing platform standards. The most valuable part of it is to let people point directly to the object in the environment and then express what to do.

But choosing an object is just the beginning. If its identity, version and permissions cannot be extended in the next step, the spatial interaction is still just a brief display.

Let the subject survive in the mission first.

The same object may have visible forms, files, business records and operation permissions at the same time. Looking at the furniture on the screen does not mean authorizing the purchase of it; pointing to colleagues' documents does not mean having the right to edit.

Sustainable operations need to answer: which object is pointed to, what is the current state, what actions are allowed, and how the execution result is returned to the user. For high-effect actions, the confirmation interface must clearly display the scope, and gaze cannot be regarded as consent.

Quick interaction and long tasks need to be connected.

Actions such as moving, scaling and selecting require direct feedback; cross-application sorting, comparison and production may be borne by Agent. Two rhythms can coexist. Changing everything will slow down the direct operation; making all long tasks rely on gestures will increase the memory and physical burden.

I prefer to let the direct operation undertake the "designated object and local adjustment", and let the agent undertake the "understanding the goal and organizing multi-step tasks". There should be visible plans, progress, suspensions and resumptions in the middle, especially what status changes have occurred.

Spatial research cannot be limited by the headset category.

In the local space world research, the discussion involves different fields such as modeling, formatting, collaboration, engine, device connection, etc. They help to find work resistance, but they can't be called head display needs together. A file can't be opened between tools. First of all, there is a delivery link problem; whether space is displayed or not requires additional argumentation.

Here, the clues in the original report are converged into three research objects: cross-tool-maintained objects, interpretable operation states, and input and output combinations that can complete tasks. Do not use the demand level or willingness ratio that has not been manually checked.

Verification can start from the normal screen.

Choose a real workflow, such as reviewing and modifying a three-dimensional scheme. First, record the cost of positioning objects, understanding differences, expressing modifications and verification results in the ordinary interface, and then compare which link has been improved by spatial interaction.

If the benefit only comes from a larger display area, it should not be attributed to the new intelligent interaction. Conversely, if the same set of objects and operation protocols are valid on mobile phones, desktops and headsets, the value may be mainly at the system layer, not in a certain shape.
