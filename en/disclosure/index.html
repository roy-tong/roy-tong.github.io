Disclosure and editorial position

This site (roy-tong.github.io) is Roy Tong's personal research and writing file, which will contain the actual measurement and evaluation of the agent tool from October 2026. This page explains how these contents are produced, where the money comes from, and where the boundaries are. It is a prefix file for each evaluation, not an exemption decoration.

I. Sources of income

Current (2026-10):

  1. Not havePaid listing, evaluation fee, promotion rebate or any form of manufacturer-paid content;
  2. If there is a sponsor position or enterprise-entrusted research on the site in the future, it will be announced on this page and the homepage of this site, and:
    • The sponsorship position and the evaluation content are physically separated (different columns, different layouts, clear price, only display does not constitute recommendation);
    • The commissioned research shall be marked as "entrusted research" and the client at the beginning of the article, and the client shall not have the right of final judgment;
    • If the revenue of a single manufacturer does not exceed 20% of the total revenue, it will trigger a disclosure and cooling-off period.

The tested account and paid package are purchased at your own expense; the account and fee records are archived for checking. The test account provided by the manufacturer can be used, but "Sample provided by X" is marked one by one.

II. Evaluation red line (long-term validity)

No evaluation fee/listing fee; no sponsorship of "read the manuscript first and then release"; abandon comments or disclose abandonment of comments when there is an equity/consultant relationship with the evaluated object; do not notify the manufacturer before the release of negative conclusions; use a unified evaluation framework in the same category and disclose the methodology and version number; correct the record is made public; do not delete published evaluations (only additional corrections); annual release revenue structure ratio.

III. Evaluation date semantics

The evaluation date marked in the article is "the date of our verification and actual measurement", which is not the release date of the product, nor does it promise the state of the product after that.The product will change after evaluation; it may take a long time to retest, or even not retest. When the retest trigger conditions (large version release/competitive product pattern change/readers' credible feedback accumulation, three choose one) are met, the new evaluation will be added with a new article, and the old evaluation will not be deleted.

IV. Method and credibility grading

Actual measurement articles follow the public seven-element specifications: fixed task set (some held-out is not disclosed, anti-targeted optimization), each task runs ≥3 times to report "at least one success rate", horizontal evaluation control variables, unified conversion into each standard task cost, each conclusion with clickable original output or source, header annotation evaluation date and product version number, fixed limitation statement. Non-actual representation and judgment are graded according to credibility (actual measurement/snapshot/representation), and speculation is not written as a fact.

V. Description of AI participation

The research and writing process of this site is assisted by AI tools (collection, verification, drafting);The responsibility for verifying the numbers and conclusions lies with the author., the list of sources at the end of the article can be reviewed article by article. This site does not publish the finished content that has not been reviewed by anyone.
