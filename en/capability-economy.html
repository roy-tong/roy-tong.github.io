CURRENT CAMPAIGN

Agent Capability Economy

The long-term brand is "how AI becomes products, systems, and markets." The most important structural change right now: software consumers are shifting from humans to agents. That raises a new question — how software is discovered, chosen, invoked, metered, and paid for. This is the only campaign running at full intensity for the next 6–12 months.

THE THESIS

Why now, and why this question

When agents become software consumers, the basic unit of the software economy shifts from the App to the Capability: software is no longer only installed, opened, and browsed by humans, but discovered, chosen, invoked, metered, and paid for by agents.

The shift is already happening, but the industry conversation remains fragmented: some talk about protocols (MCP), some about tools (Skills), some about payments, some about marketplaces, some about observability. What's missing is one complete question connecting it all — how the entire software economy is reconstructed when agents become software consumers.

Within that question, measurement is the gap that must be solved first: without reliable measurement, there is no comparison, no pricing, and no settlement. AgentMeasure is not a product in search of a thesis; it is the productized validation of this judgment.

TERMINOLOGY

Measurement language

Terms are the smallest unit of citation. AgentMeasure is building an open measurement language — every word must be defined clearly first.

  • AgentA software consumer that pursues a user's goal: acquiring context, choosing capabilities, and invoking software to complete tasks.
  • CapabilityA single capability of software that an agent can invoke — not the whole app. The basic unit shifts from App to Capability.
  • ReachWhether a capability opportunity is discovered by agents (discovery layer).
  • ChoiceHow an agent chooses among candidate capabilities (selection layer).
  • UseWhether and how a capability is actually invoked and executed (invocation layer).
  • UtilityHow much the invocation contributed to the task (utility layer).
  • ValueHow utility converts into settlement-ready value (value layer).
  • MeteringReliable measurement of capability use — the prerequisite for pricing and settlement.
  • MarketplaceThe venue where capabilities are discovered, compared, and transacted.
  • CaaSCapability as a Service: software charged by capability rather than by seat or install.
