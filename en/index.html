Each project is being built as a discoverable, installable, and callable agent component: continuous monitoring, demand research, video transcription, and knowledge retrieval.

Open sourceIn active development

iRead

Give an agent one or more research domains, review the recommended sources and representative work, then receive continuously updated daily, weekly, and monthly reports.

  • Separate original evidence from second-hand reporting and duplicates
  • Keep data and reports on the local machine by default
  • Preserve human review instead of treating generated output as final judgment
