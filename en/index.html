WHAT I THINK

Four working theses

These are not industry slogans. They are working hypotheses I use to choose products, markets, and the next test. My writing records how evidence changes them.

01 · PHYSICAL AI

Agents complete tasks in information space; robots change the physical world.

They may share model capabilities, but their product constraints, failure costs, and value realization are fundamentally different.

02 · AI NATIVE

In AI-native products, the basic unit shifts from an interface to an auditable task loop.

Models, tools, memory, permissions, workflow, and human review determine whether work actually gets done.

03 · MARKET SEQUENCE

Geek, Professional, B2B, and Consumer are market roles for removing different uncertainties—not one funnel.

The next market should remove the largest current unknown, not obey a linear niche-to-mass narrative.

04 · EVIDENCE

Research is valuable when it changes a decision, not when it merely adds information.

Facts, inferences, and hypotheses must stay separate; counterevidence, stop rules, and unknowns belong in the output.

HOW I WORK

From a technical opening to a validated product, I repeat four moves.

  1. Define the taskWhich concrete workflow can the technology reliably change?
  2. Build the evidenceWhat is fact, inference, hypothesis, or a critical unknown?
  3. Choose the marketWhich users can remove the largest current uncertainty?
  4. Set the gatesWhat result permits continuation, return, or termination?
