Series B · Product Form

Agent and AI terminal

AI moves in two directions at the same time: becoming Agent in the information space, and becoming a new terminal and body in the physical world. This series studies the product form, interaction paradigm and economic structure of two ways.

The core judgment of this series

  1. 01

    After RAG, Agent needs Context Recommendation.

    What the complex Agent needs to solve is what context, tools, states and permissions should be injected in each round - this is a system capability independent of the retrieval.

  2. 02

    Agent to the left, body to the right

    The two routes of sharing model capabilities are gradually divided in terms of failure cost, evaluation, security, deployment and commercialization. They will become less and less like the same industry.

  3. 03

    Robots do not replace people, but give people a new body.

    Field of view, presence, movement and physical attributes - ability gain is closer to the user value than the substitution rate.

  4. 04

    The competition of AI wearables is not glasses to headphones.

    What really needs to be compared is the whole system of modality, control, body position and long-term wearing cost.

  5. 05

    AI interaction is waiting for its "touch moment"

    What is missing is not more gestures, but cross-application stable, combinable and revocable public interaction syntax.
