Series C · Organization and Management

Product management from software to hardware

Software iteration is counted in days, and hardware changes are counted in stages. When the product contains both at the same time, the difficulty is not the respective methods, but putting them into the same system. This series records my judgment, organization method and management review from software to software and hardware integration.

The core judgment of this series

  1. 01

    Intelligent hardware is the superposition of three rhythms.

    Hardware stage gate + software continuous delivery + system engineering. The difficulty of management comes from the difference in change economy: changing a line of code and changing a mold are not the same cost.

  2. 02

    The organization allocates people according to three types of risks

    Value risk, technical/system risk, delivery/business risk. Early 6-10 key roles can be concurrent, but the Owner cannot be ambiguous.

  3. 03

    Title is not equal to legitimacy.

    Titles provide decision-making power, and the record of solving problems together forms the legitimacy of the organization. The first task of airborne managers is to stabilize and diagnose, not to prove themselves.

  4. 04

    Family is not a scene.

    The changing environment, multi-person relationships, multi-task long-tail and multi-payment mechanism superposition make the technology more difficult and the economy more rich - a single-task ROI is not equal to poor economy.
