Series A · Judgment Method

Technology → Product Translation

There is no shortage of interpretation and translation in new technologies. What kind of product a technical opportunity becomes, who to serve first, and why it is established - this series disassembles the translation process into a reusable judgment method.

The core judgment of this series

  1. 01

    The market is not a ladder, but an environment of evidence.

    Geek, Professional, field B-end and mass consumers are not maturity levels, but four evidence environments, each eliminating different unknowns. When choosing the next stop, choose the most suitable evidence field first, not the largest market.

  2. 02

    Comparing the workflow of competitive products, not comparing the parameter table

    Software is better than workflow, migration and continuous change; hardware is better than physical system, mass production reliability and service. There is no insight in the parameter table, only coordinates.

  3. 03

    The sample size is not equal to evidence.

    200,000 user feedbacks may be full of noise. Demand judgment requires evidence grading, scenario restoration and counterexample testing - first define what is evidence, and then talk about the sample.

  4. 04

    After AI Native, the basic unit of the product has changed.

    Pages and files will not disappear, but will degenerate into different views of the same semantics and task status. Product definition should start from the object model, not the interface.
