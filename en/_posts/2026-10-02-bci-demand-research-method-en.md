    <h1>How to study the demand of consumer-level brain-computer interfaces: dissipation, clustering and paid evidence</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">In the new product category where users have no product mind, the questionnaire test is imagination. Evidence stage × direction two-dimensional grading, three-level verification chain.</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-10-02T16:00:00+08:00">2026-10-02</time>
      <span>8 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/bci-demand-research-method/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>In the new category where users have not yet stabilized the product mind, the questionnaire and focus group are easy to measure imagination, not behavior: it is difficult for the questionnaire to ask users what they have not experienced, and the focus group has gained most of the imagination of science fiction. The consumer-level brain-computer interface belongs to the new product category of &quot;users have not used it, but the relevant pain and alternative behavior already exist&quot;. The evidence needs to be found where the user has left traces. I have done a round of complete implementation of this set of research. The following is the design model after review and standardization. There are seven links, and each link has workpieces that can be audited.</p>

<p>The method and action are easy to talk about, and the whole text runs through with a fabricated schematic clue--&quot;The user said, hoping to know himself better when he sleeps.&quot; It does not correspond to any real research, and only demonstrates the action in three places: storage cleaning, evidence grading, and downgrade when samples are insufficient.</p>

<h2 id="先立契约写下这项研究要改变哪个决定">Make a contract first: write down which decision to change this research</h2>

<p>Before the collection begins, a research contract is signed: what is the decision-making, what are the options, what is the minimum evidence, and which inferences are clearly prohibited. The ban should be written specifically, such as &quot;don&#x27;t mention functions as demand&quot;, &quot;don&#x27;t regard crowdfunding amount as retention&quot;, &quot;don&#x27;t infer market share based on convenient samples&quot;. At the same time, the contract equips each hypothesis with falsifying conditions - any evidence will overturn it, such as &quot;users who generally say that the existing alternative is sufficient&quot; appear on a large scale, and the pain point hypothesis is overturned on the spot. Finally, write the rules of suspension and restart: key issues can be closed when they are first-hand or cross-sourced; reopen when new facts such as new product release, the first batch of retained data, and disclosure of financing terms appear. Without this contract, three months later, you are likely to get a corpus summary that can prove anything.</p>

<h2 id="三层采集场景痛点需求不能混在一个桶里">Three-layer collection: scenes, pain points and needs cannot be mixed in one bucket.</h2>

<p>The original record is layered according to &quot;scenario-pain point-demand&quot;, and each record is reduced to a requirement unit: user role × scenario/trigger × task/result × existing substitution × friction/cost × consequence × evidence level.</p>

<p>For example, if you get the sentence &quot;know yourself better when you want to sleep&quot;, the cleaning is to ask out the seven fields one by one: who, in which scene mention sleeping, &quot;know more&quot; what results to get, what to use now (sleep bracelet, diary, do nothing, all are candidates), where is the alternative plan to make him dissatisfied, what will happen if it is not solved? Leave the fields that cannot be asked blank, generate tasks to be collected, and do not allow researchers to answer on their behalf - the records that can withstand this round of questions are counted into the layer.</p>

<p>In the collection design, the community routing is divided into barrels according to &quot;platform × community × month&quot; to prevent the time window skewed and regard the recent heat as a demand trend; each route runs a trial collection first, records the output and deviation, and the zero-output route is redesigned before releasing the volume. The corpus is quota according to the role of evidence: direct feedback of the research plan, open scenarios without product language, rejecters, post-purchase support (reliability, deployment, maintenance), and mainstream comparison. No matter how many routes in the same platform are, they are only the internal coverage of the same source family, and cross-platform is an independent source - this rule prevents &quot;30 communities&quot; from being misread as &quot;three-way independent verification&quot;.</p>

<p>The bucket must contain a control group: mainstream behavior, silent users who do not consume, and those who reject &quot;the existing alternatives are enough&quot;. The sample selection also needs to prevent Zhang Guan and Li Dai - I stepped on a pit: a community route named after the product name. After the trial collection, it was found that it was a fan community of the band of the same name. It was corrected, retired, made up with the same window, and left a formal error record. If such errors are not written in the file, they will pollute the downstream conclusions along the corpus.</p>

<h2 id="多框架消融防止叙事框架替你做结论">Multi-frame disintegration: prevent the narrative framework from making conclusions for you</h2>

<p>The biggest source of pollution in new product research is narrative. The team read the same evidence with different presets of &quot;this is a sleeping device&quot; or &quot;this is a competitive training device&quot;, which may lead to a completely different conclusion. The general form of melting is<strong>Multi-frame</strong>: The same batch of user evidence is reassessed under multiple scenario frameworks. The framework is taken from the narrative of competition in the category - the same evidence, put into the &quot;sleep improvement&quot; framework to ask if there are any observable sleep problems and costs behind it, and put into the &quot;self-quantification&quot; framework to ask whether such users have been observing themselves in other ways. Four groups were actually used in my round of projects; the number of groups is the landing choice of that round, not part of the method. Each framework pre-registers its own adjudication conditions, and cross-contamination shall not be allowed before adjudication. The meaning of pre-registration is to set the definition of &quot;established&quot; before reading the evidence - supporters of framework A cannot modify the standard after reading the data. There are only three types of outlets for the ruling: support, overturn, and insufficient samples. The third is the same official conclusion as the first two. Before all the frameworks are cut out, any statement that &quot;this new sensor brings incremental value&quot; will be treated as a hypothesis and cannot enter the conclusion area.</p>

<h2 id="候选方案--用户簇交叉矩阵比人物小传有用">Candidate scheme × user cluster: cross matrix is more useful than character biography</h2>

<p>User clusters grow out of data clusters, and it is forbidden to invent out of thin air. The general form of the method is the cross matrix of &quot;candidate scheme × user cluster&quot;: each station of the candidate scheme is a column, the user cluster occupies a row, and the evidence strength is filled in the grid. In a recent round of research, 2 candidate schemes × 6 user clusters were actually formed - numbers are the landing products of that round and should not be regarded as part of the method. The output of this table is not a few biograms, but a structural discovery - which cluster of users only recognize a certain plan, which cluster stays in the complaint layer for all candidates, and which grid is completely empty. Spaces and conflicting grids are the task list for the next round of research. The naming of user clusters also comes from the corpus: describe themselves with their own complaints and alternative behaviors, and use less demographic labels - a name that grows from a cluster that the team can directly use to speak to users.</p>

<h2 id="付费证据单独成轨态度和钱包分开放">Paid evidence is tracked separately: attitude and wallet are separated</h2>

<p>The statement of &quot;willingness to buy&quot; and the real payment behavior are divided into two track management. The payment track is organized according to four questions: who pays, how much, why, and how long to pay - corresponding to the portrait of the payment subject, price anchor, payment motivation, repurchase and retention, each has its own source of evidence. No matter how enthusiastic the words in the attitude track are, they should not be carried into the paid track; on the other hand, the weight of a return or abandonment record is more important than ten praises. The price anchor gives priority to the real transaction and the selling price, and the attitude number (&quot;I&#x27;m willing to spend a thousand yuan&quot;) is only weak evidence of the purchase intention. When no payment precedent can be found, the honest output is that &quot;the payment evidence of the category is empty&quot;, rather than moving the price of the neighboring category to charge - the neighboring market can only provide boundary reference.</p>

<h2 id="证据定级阶段一条轴方向另一条轴">Evidence grading: one axis of stage and another axis of direction</h2>

<p>This section first explains a design revision. The grading caliber of this round of research is an E0–E5 mixed axis: E4+ record the price anchor and purchase intention, and E4− record the rejection, return, and abandonment - in the same layer, both &quot;where did the behavior go&quot; and &quot;it supports or counter-provement&quot;. When I reviewed it, I found that there would be practical problems if these two dimensions were pressed together: a statement of &quot;I won&#x27;t buy&quot; and a record of &quot;spending 3,000 yuan to return the goods after two weeks&quot;, which were regarded as the positive and negative ends of the same layer on the old axis; but the former mostly did not even have real interaction, and the latter was the complete behavior of returning after the transaction. Therefore, the following directly gives the disassembled version - this is the design revision proposed in this article, and the hybrid axis was still executed in the original project.</p>

<p>Dimension A is the Evidence Stage, which only answers one question: where the behavioral evidence has come.</p>

<p>Dimension B is Direction, with only three values: + support, − refutation, 0 neutral. Two dimensions are combined: E1+ is a clear pain point; E2− is that the existing alternative is sufficient and constitutes a counter-proof; E3− is a rejection after trial; E4− is a refund after purchase; E5+ is a continuous use and repurchase; E5− is a long-term abandonment after a period of use. There is also a chain adjustment of the disassembly axis: the purchase intention statement is moved out of E4 - the intention is not a transaction, but the attitude track; the price anchor is originally market data, not the grading of user evidence.</p>

<p>For example, when there is only the sentence &quot;I hope to know more about myself when I sleep&quot;, remember E1+: there is a clear expected result, and the direction is support. If this user has also written &quot;wearing a bracelet to sleep, only see a score in the morning&quot;, substitute behavior and friction have appeared, remember E2+. On the other hand, if he writes &quot;I have worn the bracelet for half a year, and I know everything I need to know&quot;, the stage is also E2, but the direction is −: the existing replacement has satisfied him, and this evidence is to prove the demand hypothesis. If the context is just talking about sleep and there is no pain, stop at E0.</p>

<p>There are two iron rules. First, it is not allowed to infer the high stage from the low stage: No matter how dense E1+ and E2+ are, it cannot prove that the user will accept a specific plan, and at most give the judgment of &quot;hypothesis is valid and to be verified&quot;; upgrading can only come from new evidence, not from quantity. Second, the direction does not automatically turn positive with the stage: the high stage is not equal to good news. E4− is a counter-evidence returned after the transaction, and it should not be counted as positive evidence because it reaches the high stage; E2− (satisfactory substitute), pain-free scene and other counter-evidence are evaluated with the positive evidence.</p>

<p>The verification chain is also changed to the third level with the removal of the axis. The old caliber requires a problem chain (E1/E2), an acceptance chain (E3) and a business chain (E4+/E5) of &quot;verified&quot; conclusion to be established at the same time; this time it is revised together, because the requirements are too strict - verify a pain point without paying evidence:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Problem validation (Problem validated)</strong>: Under the same role, scene and task, the positive evidence of E1/E2 is sufficient, and there is no large-scale E2− counter-evidence;</li>
  <li><strong>Solution validated</strong>: Real interaction and selection of candidate schemes appear in the E3 layer;</li>
  <li><strong>Commercial validation (Commercial validated)</strong>: Real transactions, retention and repurchases appear on the E4/E5 layer.</li>
</ul>

<p>The three levels are established and invalidated separately. The problem verification is valid and the commercial verification is empty, which is a legal intermediate output - it shows which level of evidence should be supplemented next, not the failure of the study.</p>

<h2 id="审计件与诚实降级">Audit and honest downgrade</h2>

<p>The credibility of the research is supported by two types of workpieces. First, pipeline fingerprint: the input and output hash of each step of analysis are written into the audit directory, and any conclusion can be traced back to the specific batch of data. The second is calibration: layered sampling and negative samples to make a calibration table, two independent encoders blindly code the same batch of samples, separate the stage and direction of coding, calculate the consistency coefficient, and enter the manual review of inconsistencies. Semantic insights and determinism are stored in separate files, and the conclusion is only allowed to cite the numbered records in the evidence ledger.</p>

<p>The most important form of output is<strong>Honesty downgrade</strong>: When the sample cannot support a certain judgment, write &quot;the sample is not supported&quot; in the official deliverable, and attach the failure check item, the impact on decision-making, and the next step to allow the remedy - do not quietly lower the standard to continue output. Access control failure itself is a research state, and the delivery is a status report with a failure banner. For example, if the cluster of &quot;know yourself better&quot; is all E1+ wishes, and there is no E2 alternative behavior. In the report, this grid is written &quot;the sample is not supported&quot;, indicating that the next step is to supplement the alternative behavior - not to be written as &quot;the user is strongly eager to understand oneself&quot;, which is to regard the density of the desire as the intensity of the demand. A new product that has not yet been launched, a map that marks the stage and direction of the evidence, is much more valuable than an optimistic report.</p>
