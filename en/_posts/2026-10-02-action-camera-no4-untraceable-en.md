    <h1>I checked 13 research institutions, but I still couldn&#x27;t find the world&#x27;s 4th sports camera brand.</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">IDC only gave the top three, and the verification of 13 institutions was fruitless. The SEO report was 3.3 times different from the same caliber - the market figures were first of all the caliber problem.</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-10-02T12:00:00+08:00">2026-10-02</time>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>IDC said that the global shipment of handheld smart cameras in 2025 will be 16.65 million units, an increase of 83% year-on-year (IDC official website press release, 2026-03-21). The top three are clearly given: DJI 10.40 million units, 62.4%, Yingshi 3.4 million units, 20.4%, GoPro 1.80 million units, 10.8% (IT Home quoted IDC chart, 2026-03-21). So who is the fourth? I checked the public channels, 13 institutions, five languages - at least in the public channels, IDC did not give an answer.</p>

<h2 id="先钉死口径1665-万台量的不是运动相机">First nail the aperture: 16.65 million units are not sports cameras.</h2>

<p>IDC&#x27;s definition of &quot;handheld smart camera&quot;: a consumer-grade portable imaging device with computing processing power, electronic or optical anti-shake, resolution of 2K or above, and can be used by hand, including sports cameras, panoramic cameras, and gimbal cameras (original definition on IDC official website). Among them, the sub-category of sports cameras will ship more than 8 million units in 2025, +61% year-on-year; more than 2.5 million panoramic cameras; and the number of gimbal cameras will increase by more than 100% year-on-year.</p>

<p>Taking 16.65 million units as the &quot;scale of the sports camera market&quot; to benchmark the English report is the first source of the accident. The benchmark sports camera should use this gear of 8 million units.</p>

<h2 id="第一层失效idc-的榜单到第-3-名就停了">The first layer is invalid: IDC&#x27;s list stops when it reaches the third place.</h2>

<p>The top three totaled 15.6 million units, 93.7%; the remaining 1.05 million units, about 6.3%. IDC&#x27;s expression is &quot;other brands&quot; and no one is named. The quarterly reports of IDC 2026Q1 and 2026Q2 also only named these three companies (Securities Times 2026-06-25; IT Home 2026-09-23).</p>

<p>The top three eat 93.7%, which means that the fourth place in the world can only exist in the remaining 6.3%. Further evidence comes from the Chinese market. Luotu Technology 2026H1 reported that brands other than Xinjiang and Yingshi accounted for about 10% of the sales, but only about 2% of the sales (2026-08-07). It can&#x27;t tell us who is the fourth in the world, but it means that the top three are likely to have entered the low-priced and fragmented long tail.</p>

<h2 id="第二层失效候选厂商自己也不披露">The second layer is invalid: the candidate manufacturers themselves do not disclose</h2>

<p>These are historical data and single-channel data: the shipment volume in 2025 cannot be inferred from the &quot;cumulative 6 million units in 15 years&quot;, and the global ranking cannot be inferred from the &quot;Amazon single monthly sales&quot;. The two main candidates are unlisted companies: no annual report, no prospectus, and no verified third-party shares.</p>

<p>The scope of verification is the 13 companies in the above table, plus the search in Chinese, English, Japanese, French and Portuguese and more than 50 page crawls: no 4 brands that have been released in the world and their individual shares have been found.</p>

<p>In addition, the other seven commonly cited institutions - Counterpoint, CINNO, Airi, TrendForce, Canalys/Omdia, Sadie, Yole - were verified in multiple rounds, and no public data of this category was found. At least in the public information that I can check, I can&#x27;t find Counterpoint&#x27;s data for this category. If it is cited, the specific report name, date or original page should be given at the same time, otherwise it cannot be verified.</p>

<p>Among the 13 institutions I checked, only Aowei Cloud Network ranked 5th, but it only covers China&#x27;s caliber and cannot be extrapherted to the global - although it notes by the way: GoPro fell to 5th in its former second largest market.</p>

<p>If you have to give a global answer: SJCAM and AKASO are the most evidenced candidate combinations. But this is an inference, not data.</p>

<h2 id="seo-型市场研究报告那些美元数字是怎么生产出来的">SEO-type market research report: How are those dollar figures produced?</h2>

<p>After the failure of the regular channel, the front row of the search engine is another type of source: SEO market research reports from publishers such as QYResearch, Bosson and Global Info Research. The numbers on the free page have these problems, and most of them can be verified on the spot:</p>

<ul>
  <li>The same publisher, the same caliber, released in the same year, the scale difference is 3.32 times: QYResearch&#x27;s &quot;Action Cameras&quot; reported $2.292 billion in 2024 (https://www.qyresearch.com/reports/5039949/action-cameras), and &quot;Action and Sports Cameras&quot; reported 7.611 billion US dollars in 2024 (https://www.qyresearch.com/reports/4633808/action-and-sports-cameras).</li>
  <li>The template placeholder will be published without filling in: &quot;North American market ... was valued at $ million in 2024&quot; &quot;the global five largest players hold a share approximately %&quot; - the front of the percent sign is empty (4633808 page original text).</li>
  <li>The regional share reported by Bosson sisters is 25% + 40% + 40% = 105%, more than 100%; there are also Nokia and Panono, which have long withdrawn from the list of key manufacturers (https://pdf.marketpublishers.com/bosson_research/global-360-degree-panoramic-camera-market-research-report-2022status-n-outlook.pdf).</li>
  <li>The GVR report attribute table says &quot;Base year for estimation = 2024&quot;, but the scale value given is 7.272 million US dollars in 2025 (the previous version of the attribute table is reprinted: https://chosareport.com/gvr-action-camera/; the latest version of the value: https://www.giiresearch.com/report/grvi1941953-action-camera-market-size-share-trends-analysis.html).</li>
  <li>Sullivin&#x27;s English official website mistranslated &quot;42.80 billion yuan&quot; into &quot;428 billion yuan&quot;, which is exaggerated by 10 times (English page: https://www.frostchina.com/en/content/insight/detail/69044bff735ca7f8a65e61f7, Chinese page: https://www.frostchina.com/content/insight/detail/69044bff735ca7f8a65e61f7, web archive: https://web.archive.org/web/20261002141432/https://www.frostchina.com/en/content/insight/detail/69044bff735ca7f8a65e61f7).</li>
</ul>

<p>The nature of these numbers is modeling output, not actual measurement. It can be seen by doing arithmetic with their own numbers: GVR said that the scale of &quot;sports cameras only&quot; in 2025 will be 7.272 billion US dollars, while the sales of the entire handheld smart camera category (16.65 million units) measured by IDC was 46.1 billion yuan, about 6.4 billion US dollars at the 7.1–7.2 exchange rate (our conversion) - the amount of a single subcategory exceeds the whole category, and the two numbers cannot be valid at the same time. In terms of implicit unit price, GVR is about $909/unit, Mordor is about $886/unit, while GoPro&#x27;s self-reported average selling price in 2025 in 10-K is $357 (+8.2%).</p>

<h2 id="口径考古沙利文和-idc-差-56-倍先别急着判断谁错">Caliber archaeology: Sullivin and IDC are 5.6 times different. Don&#x27;t rush to judge who is wrong.</h2>

<p>44.61 million sports cameras will be shipped globally in 2023 under the Sullivin caliber; more than 8 million sports cameras will be shipped in 2025 under the IDC caliber. The year is two years earlier, but the number of units is 5.6 times that of the latter (calculated according to the figures of the two companies). However, the amount is right: Sullivin&#x27;s 36.4 billion yuan in 2023 (transferred by China Gold), and IDC 46.1 billion yuan in 2025, which is reasonable in terms of magnitude and direction. The difference lies in the implicit average price: one is less than 1,000 yuan, and one is about 2,769 yuan/unit (IDC full category 46.1 billion yuan ÷ 16.65 million units).</p>

<p>The root cause can be spelled out from the two materials. The research report of China Gold Corporation on 2025-07-12 provided raw materials: the definition of IDC requires a resolution of 2K and above; there are many white-licensed manufacturers in the entry-level market, and the price is as low as about 500 yuan. Putting these two together, the conclusion is that IDC systematically excluded the 1080p white-label machine, and Sullivin counted these, and the two quantities were two batches of products (the comprehensive inference is from the verification of this article, not the original sentence of Zhongjin). A caliber difference also explains the superficial &quot;contradictions&quot; of the three groups: the difference in the number of units is 5 times; Sullivin&#x27;s caliber growth rate is 12-14%, while IDC reports +83%; Sullivin said that CR5 in 2023 is about 74%, while IDC said that CR3 in 2025 is 93.7%. It is more useful to check the category definition first when checking market figures than to check the numbers themselves first.</p>

<p>Exceptions should also be noted: Sullivin and Jiuqian&#x27;s difference of 75% to 49% on the global panoramic camera share in 2025Q3 is a substantial conflict, and the Sullivin report, which has been removed from the shelves twice and cannot be downloaded from the official website (reported by Global Network 2025-11-07, quoted from Titanium Media: https://www.tmtpost.com/7772651.html), there is still arithmetic evidence - according to its Xinjiang share, the single-season panoramic revenue of Yingshi will exceed the total revenue of Yingshi in the season. This kind of number should be discarded as a whole.</p>

<h2 id="引用市场数字前的三步核查">Three-step verification before quoting market figures</h2>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Return one hand.</strong>IDC press releases, company annual reports, 10-K and prospectuses are given priority; media citations should be returned to the original source. Example 1: The accurate decimal point of the manufacturer split (62.4%/20.4%/10.8%) comes from the IDC chart quoted by IT Home. The text of the IDC official website is only 62% after rounding. Both are true and must be marked separately. Example 2: Yingshi&#x27;s revenue in 2025 is 9.858 billion and 9.741 billion. The former is the unaudited performance report on 2026-02-13, and the latter is the audited annual report on 2026-04-28. The annual report has been modified - the quotation is subject to the annual report.</li>
  <li><strong>Align the caliber.</strong>Category boundaries (whether there is a 2K threshold), geographical scope (global or Chinese online), channels (traditional e-commerce or omni-channel), year (whether the base year and the value are the same year). Sullivin&#x27;s answer to IDC is a textbook case: the amount is right, the number of units is 5.6 times different, and the answer is in the category definition.</li>
  <li><strong>Do arithmetic again.</strong>Number of units × average price ≈ amount, the three numbers bite each other to pass the test. Bosson&#x27;s $680 million can be self-calculated with its self-described 1.51 million × $450/unit - but the recalculation is the factory caliber of the manufacturing end, which cannot be directly compared with the retail caliber. In addition to verification, arithmetic can also tell you what the number is.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="查不到本身就是结构信息">&quot;Can&#x27;t find&quot; itself is structural information.</h2>

<p>The world&#x27;s fourth sports camera brand, there is no answer in the public channel, and &quot;can&#x27;t find&quot; itself is the portrait of this market: the top three eat 93.7%, and the remaining 6.3% are shared by dozens of brands, none of which is named by IDC separately. No. 4 is not the most important information in this market; the real information is that outside the top three, the market has quickly fallen into a highly fragmented long tail. When doing competitive analysis and writing industry reports, this structure is more useful than any fabricated No. 4.</p>

<p>(This verification was completed on 2026-09-23; the quoted quarterly data is up to date with the IDC 2026Q2 report, released on 2026-09-23.)</p>
