    <h1>Agent to the left, body to the right: the fork of AI in information space and the physical world</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">They share models and planning capabilities, but are subject to the reversibility of the digital world and the irreversible consequences of the physical world respectively.</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-08-13T21:00:00+08:00">2026-08-13</time>
      <span>10 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/agent-left-embodied-right/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>
      
        <span class="post-tags">

  <div class="prose">
    <p>If a piece of code is written incorrectly, it can be rolled back; if an email is sent incorrectly, it can be supplemented; if the database is mistakenly modified, the log and backup can be saved. If the robotic arm hits an object, the loss has occurred; the mobile robot hits a person and cannot be recovered by &quot;retry&quot;; the equipment is exhausted and stopped in a dangerous position. The problem is not only the failure of the mission, but also the introduction of new risks.</p>

<p>This line is the dividing line between Agent and body intelligence: whether the system directly bears the irreversible consequences in the real world. Agent mainly changes the state of the information world; the body system directly changes the state of the physical world. The status is different, whether the error can be revoked, how the system is evaluated, how the safety responsibility is divided, how the product is deployed, and how the company is scaled, all of which are divided.</p>

<p>This dividing line is particularly easy to erase today, because there is a popular story that stitches the two together: the model is getting stronger and stronger, and AI begins to understand the environment, planning steps, and calling tools - so in-body intelligence is the next stop of the agent, and the robot is to put a body on the agent.</p>

<p>The first half of this story is true, and the second half is wrong.</p>

<h2 id="它们确实有一段相似的过去">They do have a similar past.</h2>

<p>In the information world, automation begins with scripts and fixed rules: RPA clicks on the interface according to the preconfigured steps, and traditional software processes input according to a clear process. After the model ability is up, Copilot participates in the partial work. One step further, Agent understands the goal, selects tools, performs multi-step operations and continues to adjust according to the results.</p>

<p>In the physical world, automation has also relied on fixed programs and structured environments: industrial robots repeat actions within certain positions, rhythms and safety boundaries. Later, visual perception, learning control and strategy models gradually improved the ability of the system to process changes - RT-2 put vision, language and robot actions into the same model, Open X-Embodiment tried to improve strategy migration with cross-robot data, and Google DeepMind&#x27;s Gemini Robotics also listed generalization, interaction and dexterity as the key capabilities of robot models.</p>

<p>The common changes of the two routes do exist: the environment no longer needs to be fully predefined; the system begins to understand natural language and multimodal context; the system not only gives suggestions, but also selects and executes actions; after the action, it will read the feedback and decides the next step. From the perspective of the model&#x27;s ability, the Agent calls the API, and the robot calls the motion controller; the Agent reads the web page and database, and the robot reads the camera, depth information and joint status.</p>

<p>However, the common model ability is not equal to the common product law.</p>

<h2 id="分岔发生在哪里">Where did the fork happen?</h2>

<p>Agent usually handles documents, codes, messages, schedules, databases, accounts and business processes; the body system handles the security relationship between location, posture, object, space, energy and people and equipment.</p>

<p>Of course, the digital world also has irreversible operations - transfer, public release, data deletion - but software can usually isolate high-risk actions separately through permissions, sandboxes, versions, logs and manual approvals. The errors of the physical world are more difficult to abstract in this way, and a model that performs well on Benchmark is not automatically equivalent to a robot product that can be delivered continuously: the model is only a layer in the physical closed loop, and sensors, state estimation, control, structure, actuators, energy consumption, safety, maintenance and abnormal recovery must be established at the same time.</p>

<p>Along this dividing line, the two routes will go further and further in eight dimensions:</p>

  <thead>
    <tr>

    </tr>

    <tr>

    <tr>

    </tr>
    <tr>

    <tr>

    </tr>

</table>

<p>This table does not mean that Agent must be simple and robots must be slow - Agent in the core systems of finance, medical care and enterprises may also face extremely high error costs; highly convergent robot products such as sweepers have been commercialized on a large scale. What it means is that you can&#x27;t use the speed and success criteria of one route to infer another.</p>

<h2 id="agent-这边竞争落到任务交付">Agent this side: competition falls on task delivery</h2>

<p>When the basic model gradually becomes a replaceable public capacity, the difference between Agent products no longer comes from &quot;smarter models&quot;, but from whether it is really connected to a task system. A set of available Agents should solve at least five things.</p>

<p><strong>It understands the task, not a prompt.</strong>&quot;Generating a report&quot; is not a complete task. It is also necessary to explain who the report is to, what decisions are supported, what materials are used, what results are completed, and how to deal with failure. A longer Prompt can&#x27;t solve these problems. What Agent needs is a clear result contract.</p>

<p><strong>Its permission boundary is a part of the product.</strong>The more tools, the stronger the ability. Once Agent accesses mail, code base, payment, customer data and internal system, &quot;what can be accessed and what cannot be accessed&quot; must become an explicit design: low-risk operations are automatically executed, and high-risk operations need to be confirmed; read-only, suggested, drafting, submitted and formal execution should be of different authority levels.</p>

<p><strong>The process should leave checkable state changes.</strong>Reliable Agent should not only give the final answer, but also let users know what it has read, based on what judgment, what objects have been changed, which steps have failed, and where manual intervention is needed - this is also the reason why the current Agent development framework has begun to emphasize tools, Guardrails, Tracing and observability: product management is the whole task execution chain, not a dialogue.</p>

<p><strong>Errors should be found, rolled back and compensated.</strong>The digital world can establish version, diff, log, sandbox and approval mechanism. Products should actively use these features instead of letting the model directly operate all systems.</p>

<p><strong>It should be clearly written when the person will take over.</strong>Human-in-the-loop is not a safety slogan: what confidence to continue, what action must be confirmed, what abnormality must be stopped immediately, how users can quickly understand the scene and take over - all of these must have clear answers.</p>

<p>Therefore, the long-term competition of Agent is more like the competition of the &quot;task delivery system&quot;: who has a more complete context, more reasonable tools and permissions, a clearer result contract, and a lower cost of abnormal handling.</p>

<h2 id="具身这边竞争发生在持续可靠的物理闭环">On this side: competition occurs in a continuous and reliable physical closed loop.</h2>

<p>Emmodied intelligence also needs a better model, but the model ability must enter a longer and more constrained system link. To complete the task, a robot needs to continuously process the perception of the environment, judge the state, understand the intention, plan the action, control the execution, check the results, and handle the anomalies - any link is unstable, and the final task may fail.</p>

<p>And it faces a world of continuous change: objects will slide, people will suddenly enter, light will change, ground friction will be different, sensors will be obscured, actuators will heat up, and parts will wear out. This is also the reason why robot safety naturally needs to be layered: high-level models can understand tasks and semantic risks, and the bottom still need collision avoidance, contact force limitation, dynamic stability and security control for specific ontologies - Google DeepMind clearly separates low-level security control from high-level semantic understanding in the public description of Gemini Robotics.</p>

<p>For embod products, the success of a demo is far from enough. The meaningful indicators are: how many times to complete the task in a clear environment; what changes will fail and whether the failure can be restored; how long manual intervention is needed; the time, energy consumption and service cost of a task; whether the equipment is still reliable after weeks and months of use; and whether the user and service team can quickly locate and repair in case of failure.</p>

<p>Therefore, the early stage of body intelligence is more suitable for growing out of narrow tasks with clear boundaries: the environment is relatively clear, the task is repetitive, the value is high enough, and the failure can be handled safely. First, a physical task is stable, and then the environment and task boundaries are gradually expanded, which is usually closer to the product reality than the pursuit of &quot;universal robots&quot; at the beginning.</p>

<h2 id="商业化速度不能互相套用">The speed of commercialization cannot be used against each other.</h2>

<p>Agent products can be distributed by software: a capability update serves a large number of users at the same time, new tools are accessed through API, and task data is continuously generated in use. Every time the robot expands its income, it often needs to produce, deliver and maintain more equipment. The growth of scale also brings pressure on manufacturing, supply chain, on-site deployment, maintenance and customer service. Even if the model can be upgraded online, the body is still limited by existing sensors, computing power, structure and actuators.</p>

<p>The verification order is also different: Agent is more likely to verify &quot;whether is willing to hand over the task to it&quot; first; the robot must verify that &quot;the task is valuable, the system can do it, and it can be delivered for a long time&quot; at the same time. Therefore, it cannot be inferred that general-purpose robots will enter homes and enterprises at the same speed just because Agent enters a large number of workflows in a few months; nor can its long-term value be considered lower just because the commercialization of robots is slower.</p>

<h2 id="会合流但不会变回一条路">It will converge, but it will not turn back to the same road.</h2>

<p>In the future, many complete tasks will include both information actions and physical actions. For example, a set of on-site inspection system: Agent reads work orders, understands equipment history, plans tasks, dispatches robots, sorts out abnormal evidence and generates reports; the robot is responsible for moving, observing, measuring and on-site operation.</p>

<p>Whether the two can work together depends on a clear task agreement: what the goal is, what information and physical actions are allowed, how success, failure and anomalies are defined, what conditions must be stopped, when to request manual takeover, and what evidence is left in the end. Agent and robots will collaborate, but are still supported by two different sets of engineering, products and operating systems - the former is responsible for understanding, coordinating and managing the state of information, and the latter is responsible for changing the physical state safely and reliably.</p>

<h2 id="这个区分怎么用于产品判断">How can this distinction be used for product judgment?</h2>

<p>To judge an Agent product, first ask: which real work does it complete for users? What data, tools and permissions do you need to access? What results are completed and how to evaluate them? Which actions can be rolled back and which must be approved? Where does manual intervention take place and how much does it cost?</p>

<p>Judge a body product and continue to ask: what kind of environment does it face, what object does it face, and what high-frequency tasks does it complete? Can the current ontology and model form a continuous closed loop instead of just completing a demonstration? What are the most dangerous, common and most expensive failures? How to charge, maintain, restore and upgrade the device? Is the unit economy established including manufacturing, deployment and service?</p>

<p>If you skip this distinction, the judgment will often be wrong: the migration of model capabilities is seen too fast, and the system capabilities that still need to be replenished by the product are seen too lightly.</p>

<p>Agent and embodied intelligence share models, natural language, multimodality and planning capabilities, which will also be connected to each other in more and more tasks, but will not converge into the same product. Agent to the left to enter a deeper information context, tool permissions and organizational workflow; to the right to enter a more complex physical environment, reliability, security and delivery system. When judging them, we should first look at whether they can continue to deliver tasks, rather than putting every model progress into the same &quot;AI next stop&quot; story.</p>
