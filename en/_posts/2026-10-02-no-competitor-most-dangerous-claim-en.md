    <h1>&quot;No competitive products&quot; is the most dangerous competitive analysis conclusion.</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">Negative research cannot prove that it does not exist; the core of the method is to draw an auditable boundary for &quot;not found for the time being&quot;.</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-10-02T15:00:00+08:00">2026-10-02</time>
      <span>6 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/no-competitor-most-dangerous-claim/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>The conclusion of the first competition report is that no one occupies this category. The second report overturned it--<a href="https://tarecount.com">TareCount</a>Just do what I want to do, the same anchor case, the same set of local operation plans, and a complete audit of a clear price of $2,500. I haven&#x27;t deleted the two reports: they are more valuable together than any one alone. The first one records how I was exhausted in the laid search surface, and the last one demonstrates how this search surface is leaked, how an overwritten conclusion is caught, and how to write it back.</p>

<p>The most dangerous conclusion in competitive analysis is that &quot;there is no competitive product&quot;. The danger is that it sounds like a certificate - the person who gets it will arrange the product route and write financing materials according to it. And negative research can never prove that &quot;it does not exist&quot;. There is only one thing that can be proved: it was not found within the defined search space and time point. The original conclusion of the first report is that &quot;the category is empty&quot;, which jumps the distance between these two sentences one step further, and the car is overturned on this jump.</p>

<p>The background is my own open source project.<a href="https://github.com/roy-tong/AgentMeasure">AgentMeasure</a>Do V2 competition research. V2 Question to be answered: AI customer service has been charged according to the &quot;number of resolved conversations&quot;, and the &quot;resolution&quot; is determined by the seller himself, and whether the buyer can independently verify the bill. Competitive analysis therefore faces an extreme problem - is there anyone else in this category? There is no direct answer to this question. The best thing that can be delivered is a record of exhaustion with boundaries. &quot; There is no way to confirm that the category is empty. There is only one problem that really needs to be solved: how to set an auditable boundary for &quot;I haven&#x27;t found it for the time being&quot;.</p>

<h2 id="一搜索面铺三层代码机构市场">I. Search the three layers of noodles: code, institution, market</h2>

<p>I divided the search surface into three layers: code, institution and market, and each layer takes evidence separately.</p>

<p>The market layer added three more numbers to illustrate the pain level: a buyer manually audited 40 work orders, the bill caliber 40% resolution rate, 12.5% after checking one by one (<a href="https://community.intercom.com/fin-product-feedback-member-group-49/fin-s-flawed-resolution-assumption-10516">Intercom Community Post 10516</a>); Another buyer reported a confirmed resolution rate of 6–7%, and assumed a resolution rate of nearly 60% (<a href="https://community.intercom.com/analyze-fin-93/how-do-you-improve-your-confirmed-resolution-rate-8268">Community Post 8268</a>); In the sample of Supportman, 256 dialogues without customer replies and citation articles were all recorded as solved (<a href="https://supportman.io/fin-audit/">supportman.io</a>). The three layers together are my exhausted records in the defined search surface: not found in the code, not found in the institution, the market is running wildly and the audit is missing. This record says that nothing was caught on the Internet in these three, not that there were no fish in the sea - the first report also wrote the second half of the sentence in the conclusion, which was the starting point of the overturn.</p>

<h2 id="二给每个没找到划一条可审计的边界">II. Draw an auditable boundary for each &quot;not found&quot;</h2>

<p>A 0 result that only writes &quot;not found&quot;, readers can&#x27;t tell whether there is really nothing in the world or I didn&#x27;t find the right place - this indistinguishable is the dangerous place of the conclusion of &quot;no competitive products&quot;. Therefore, three things should be nailed next to each 0 result:<strong>Query words, time, search surface</strong>。</p>

<p>The first report was about 35 independent searches, and the negative results were recorded in this format:<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">"outcome audit" AI agent vendor pricing startup</code>(No product),<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">"resolution verification as a service"</code>Exact phrase (no result),<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">AI agent invoice audit</code>And<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">verify AI agent outcome billing</code>(No result),<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">ISO standard AI agent evaluation SC 42</code>And<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">"confirmed resolution" definition standard AI support</code>(No standard). If it were anyone&#x27;s hand, the same word, the same face, should get the same &quot;no&quot; - it&#x27;s different, which means that it&#x27;s time to update the conclusion.</p>

<p>The boundary of the search surface should also be explained. My record fixed a section of &quot;Access Restrictions&quot;: Zendesk Help Center crawls and returns an empty shell (later uses its JSON API to read the full text), cio.com and lexology.com returns 403 to my IP, and the Intercom community is a single-page application rendered by JS, and only 1 of the 21 candidate posts crawled gets the full text. These contents deline the scope of application of the conclusion: I am only responsible for &quot;no&quot; within the specified boundary, and I will not say a word outside the boundary.</p>

<h2 id="三最像的对手在同结构里不在同品类里">III. The most similar opponent is in the same structure, not in the same category.</h2>

<p>After laying out three layers, the search surface still owes one piece: companies with the same structure and different categories. They are not in your competition today. Turning over a product line is.</p>

<p>The biggest one is<a href="https://www.vaudit.com/">Vaudit</a>, do AI token/API bill audit: seller expenditure of more than 4 billion US dollars has been independently verified, more than 100 million US dollars have been recovered, and more than 57 corporate customers; in March-June 2026, about 1.7 million US dollars of billing errors were found from the 34 million US dollars of AI expenditure of 60 companies, about 5% (<a href="https://www.vaudit.com/post/ai-billing-reconciliation-how-vaudits-tokenaudit-found-1-7m-in-billing-errors">Case page</a>); The charge is 1% of the audit quota; Salesforce is already in the platform list. The expression of Deepak Kumar, the person in charge of its product, is almost the same as my product definition: each seller is scoring his own homework, and the buyer can only accept the score; any seller controls the expenditure category of the meter, and this problem will grow.</p>

<p>There are also the same structure.<a href="https://github.com/inferock/inferock-bench">Inferock</a>——Local agents verified LLM billing one by call. 141 star and Product Hunt ranked first on the same day, and also released their own open standards. Zoom in again: freight bill has Freehand, recovery audit has PRGX, telecommunications has Araxxe, legal e-billing has independent audit, and advertising verification is<a href="https://www.isara.ai/blog/the-doubleverify-for-ai/">Isara</a>It is already a market of $4.82 billion when cited as a precedent. In the mature expenditure audit category I compared in this round, the &quot;resolution volume&quot; of AI customer service has not at least formed an equally mature independent audit layer.</p>

<p>There is also a reverse signal to be recorded: at least five independent parties have publicly talked about the same argument - Clelp&#x27;s blog (July 2026), 3LI&#x27;s article (August 2026), Vaudit&#x27;s product manager, Inferock&#x27;s README, QEval&#x27;s VERDICT method, plus two arXiv papers in September 2026 (including<a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.07680">One article</a>Experiment: The accountability layer of the self-reported report of the only read intelligence body, the probability of finding the real fault source is 4.1%, which is lower than the random baseline of 20%). The gap independently expressed by many parties is a known gap, and insight does not constitute a moat. This line must be written in the conclusion of competition, otherwise the conclusion is self-deceiving.</p>

<h2 id="四转折点搜索面本身会漏">IV. Turning point: the search surface itself will leak</h2>

<p>The original conclusion of the first report is: &quot;No company sells &quot;result audits billed by results&quot;, and the category is empty.&quot; It is not nothing - it found Supportman (buyer-side QA of Intercom single-platform, which is labeled as the largest strategic risk), 3LI, which provides manual reconciliation services, and Glacis, who claims to only record and not prove honesty. What it missed was TareCount.</p>

<p>The reason for the leak is now clear: TareCount is a manual service, free spot check plus a complete audit of $2,500, and the entire network footprint is a landing page and a 1 star warehouse. There is no financing news, no catalog entry, no search ranking - I searched for words, but I didn&#x27;t find it.</p>

<p>The second report changed the entrance and found it from the demand site: pull down all 10,364 post URLs in the community.intercom.com site map, filter out 71 candidates and crawl 21 keywords. The result is in the comment section of the anchor complaint post - the founder of TareCount posted two comments on August 2 and 4, 2026, selling free random checks to the buyers who complained. In the early days, they stood directly in the pond with the most fish.</p>

<p>This step is the key turning point of the whole method. Three layers of evidence, 35 queries, each 0 nailed the query word and time, but none of them could cover TareCount - it was not on those search surfaces at all. The three-piece set solves that 0 can be re-run, which cannot solve the blind area of the search surface itself: if the category search is not found, it does not mean that it does not exist. If you are poor, you can only do the known side, but you can&#x27;t make the unknown side; the unknown part needs to be found on the spot at another entrance.</p>

<p>Later, I looked through the old notes and found that the name TareCount had appeared in a line earlier in a final draft - &quot;already exists, not counted&quot;. The clue came and was released by me as a footnote.</p>

<h2 id="五把纠错写进方法">V. Write the error correction into the method</h2>

<p>The handling of the second report is the part I want to stay most in this matter. It did not quietly change the first one, but wrote in the method note that the conclusion of the competition report that &quot;the category is empty&quot; was wrong. It omitted the TareCount, and the competition chapter of this report shall prevail. Keep the first one as it is, and keep the two copies side by side--<strong>The error path itself is data.</strong>。</p>

<p>There is another step after correcting the error: use the same set of doubts to audit the newly found competitors. TareCount claims to have released the open standard ARS-1 (CC BY 4.0 draft, August 27, 2026). I went to verify its public footprint: the only cited carrier is an inaccessible LinkedIn Pulse article, and the only public repository is the 1 star landing page - no reconciliation engine, no standard text, no license. ARS-1 was therefore downgraded to &quot;unverified&quot; in my record. Finding a competition is not the end. It just changes the object of the method of negative evidence.</p>

<p>Now I will write a conclusion such as &quot;no competitive product found&quot;. The report is fixed with three things: a negative evidence registration form (what was checked, where to check, when, what was not found), a section of access restrictions, and a special question - if it exists but there is no search footprint, which scene will it appear at? &quot; &quot;Found&quot; is an observation with a time stamp, which will expire at any time; it is more accurate to write it according to what will expire than to write it by attribute.</p>

<p>This set of methods can&#x27;t keep &quot;no competitive products&quot;, and I don&#x27;t plan to keep it. What it keeps is that every &quot;unfound&quot; has a boundary, a date, and can be run again. In the future, who will give you a report that says &quot;There are no competitors in this market&quot;? First, ask three questions: what is the query word, when was it checked, and where did the search surface go? If the third question and answer comes up, it is an observation record with a shelf life; if it can&#x27;t be answered, it just writes &quot;I didn&#x27;t find it&quot; as &quot;non-existent&quot;.</p>
