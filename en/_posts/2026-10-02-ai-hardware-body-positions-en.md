<p>I compressed the spatial interaction into four original words: look at the establishment of attention, refer to the selected object, pinch to make a promise, and say to express the goal.</p>

<p>This set of grammar is not suspended in the software. It should grow somewhere in the body. &quot;Look&quot; occurs near the eyes, &quot;pinch&quot; occurs on the hand, and &quot;say&quot; occurs near the mouth. AI hardware competes for the head, ears and wrists, and competes for the landing points of these original words on the body, and how much context each landing point can get.</p>

<h2 id="手机为什么不够">Why is the mobile phone not enough?</h2>

<p>The mobile phone is usually in the pocket or desktop. To get the context, users need to pull out, unlock, aim and input.</p>

<p>The value of wearable devices is to put sensors and feedback channels closer to senses, actions and intentions, and eliminate the start-up cost. But the context that can be obtained from different body areas is not the same.</p>

<h2 id="同一类传感器换个位置会发生什么">What will happen to the same kind of sensor in another position?</h2>

<p><strong>Near the eyes and face.</strong>It is possible to obtain the first-person image, head orientation or eye movement information; ordinary cameras do not automatically measure the direction of gaze. It is suitable for display, audio and touch, and also bears the cost of optics, weight, heat, appearance, prescription adaptation and shooting privacy.</p>

<p><strong>Near the ear.</strong>It can get ambient sound, dialogue and head movement, which is suitable for private audio, touch and head movement. The price is long-term blockage, hearing occupation, hygiene and endurance.</p>

<p><strong>Near the mouth and throat.</strong>It can obtain voice, breathing and subtle voice, which is suitable for voice input and silent voice potential. The price is social visibility, noise, privacy and skin comfort.</p>

<p><strong>Wrists and fingers.</strong>It can collect hand movement, physiological and activity signals. It is suitable for gestures, tactile and discrete commands. It is the most natural home for &quot;pinching&quot; and &quot;fingering&quot;. The price is weak understanding of the scene, accidental touch and occupation of hands.</p>

<p><strong>Neck and chest.</strong>It can get a more stable orientation, sound and movement, with a larger carrying space, and can also remove weight, camera and battery from the face. The price is swinging, clothing cover, oppression and appearance acceptance.</p>

<p>The context density depends on whether a location can continue to be close to what the user is watching, hearing, saying, doing and deciding, rather than the number of sensors. The same sensor, installed on the leg of the mirror and lying in the pocket, gets two kinds of context. The gap is not in the pixels, but in the distance from the intention.</p>

<h2 id="控制器和主设备可以分开">The controller and the main device can be separated.</h2>

<p>The controller does not have to grow on the main device. A kind of spatial computing platform uses eye pointing and gesture confirmation; a kind of display glasses uses wristbands as a controller; a kind of smart ring uses two-finger pinching to control shooting or alarm clocks.</p>

<p>Putting these examples together is a &quot;position-original language&quot; table: the eyes are responsible for seeing and fingering, the wrist is responsible for silent instructions, and the fingers are responsible for pinching. The combination of these original words does not require each body position to be equipped with a device.</p>

<p>But the physical position is not a permanent territory, but a continuous license. The device has a context only when the user is willing to wear it, allows perception, and can be used in real situations.</p>

<p>The strongest competitors are often still mobile phones. The mobile phone startup friction is greater, but it has mature display, input, computing power, connection and application ecology. Many wearable devices eventually become mobile phone accessories, and it is this part of the cost that is not equal.</p>

<h2 id="怎样验证">How to verify</h2>

<p>Don&#x27;t rely on the parameter table. Record the wearing behavior of the whole day: when to put on, take off, mute, switch to mobile phone, forget to charge; at the same time, record the number of times you take off, effective wearing time, battery life coverage and bystander reaction.</p>

<p>Write down the overturning conditions in advance: if it shows that the task completion rate has been improved, but the wearing time is significantly shortened, the scheme should be contracted instead of continuing to increase the parameters.</p>

<p>Therefore, the competition for wearable devices focuses on four things: which inputs can obtain the context required for the task, which outputs can deliver results with minimal disturbance, which control methods can allow users to express their intentions reliably and privately, and which position can be accepted for a long time.</p>

<p>The device with the most sensors, the strongest display or the largest model may not win. The winner is more likely to be a product that combines modality, interaction, body position and wearing cost into a low-friction process.</p>
