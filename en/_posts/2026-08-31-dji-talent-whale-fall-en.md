    <h1>One frontier falls, and all things are born.</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">Xinjiang has not collapsed, but the people it has trained are growing into new companies elsewhere. A survey of 63 samples.</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-08-31T00:00:00+08:00">2026-08-31</time>
      <span>10 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/dji-talent-whale-fall/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>
      
        <span class="post-tags">
          <a href="/en/tags/#%E7%A1%AC%E4%BB%B6">#Hardware</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E5%A4%A7%E7%96%86">#Dajiang</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E5%88%9B%E4%B8%9A%E7%94%9F%E6%80%81">#Entrepreneurship Ecology</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E7%BB%84%E7%BB%87">#Organization</a>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>On August 19, 2026, Yushu Technology was listed on the Science and Technology Innovation Board, with a closing market value of 341.7 billion yuan on the first day.</p>

<p>Wang Xingxing&#x27;s intersection with it is often mentioned. After graduating with a master&#x27;s degree in 2016, he joined Xinjiang and left to start a business two months later. This detail is easy to remember, but two months is too short to explain Yushu&#x27;s success or Xinjiang&#x27;s gains and losses.</p>

<p>What is really worth seeing is another group of people. They stayed in Xinjiang for several years to ten years, responsible for batteries, gimbals, lidars or the whole product line, and later put their new business outside the company. In 2017, Wang Lei, who had done the research and development of Xinjiang battery, founded Zhenghao; in 2020, Taoye founded Tuozhu. The initial five people covered the consumer-grade UAV business department, system engineering, FPV, gimbal and software. By 2025, the annual revenue of Tuozhu will exceed 10 billion yuan.</p>

<p>·</p>

<p>Recently, I picked up four rounds of entrepreneurial clues from the background of the former Xinjiang that I could find on the whole network, and got 90. After checking the identity relationship one by one, 63 core samples were left, that is, the named founding members have recognizable official experience in Xinjiang. The other 27 separate columns include the official spin-off of Zhuoyu and two E-bike brands, the consultant and cooperation relationship, internship experience, and pending clues. A single list is not a negation, but to avoid mixing stories of different natures.</p>

<p>The distribution of samples is more informative than the total amount.</p>

<p>On the track, there are 17 consumer hardware, 13 robot bodies, and only 8 drones and low-altitude vehicles on the track. Most of the people who left a drone company no longer make drones. The capabilities of battery, perception, control, software and hardware can be recombined in many products. The two founding members of the Infinite Workshop were previously responsible for the perception algorithm of DJI sweepers and the route planning of agricultural drones respectively, and now they do desktop five-axis CNC.</p>

<p>In terms of qualifications, nearly half of the founding members of at least 30 samples have clear experience in Xinjiang departments, teams or business leaders.</p>

<p>In terms of time, 27 of the 58 articles that can be attributed to the group have been established since 2024. Of these 27 articles, the disclosure financing stage of 23 articles is still stopped at Seed, Angel or Pre-A. That is to say, Tuozhu and Zhenghao are the only two mature business cases at present. Recently, most of these companies have just started. This is a map of entrepreneurial opportunities, not a list of successful enterprises.</p>

<p>·</p>

<p>For Xinjiang, the pressure is not on this map, but on the product lines.</p>

<p>Image. Yingshi&#x27;s revenue in 2025 was 9.741 billion yuan, and 762 million yuan was invested in new business and customized chips that year, including drones, gimbal cameras and wireless collar microphones. By the end of 2025, panoramic drones have been launched. Vivo will set up a Vlog camera at the end of 2025, and OPPO&#x27;s gimbal camera project code-named &quot;Fuyao&quot; was reported by the media in April 2026.</p>

<p>Clean. Xinjiang&#x27;s ROMO will be listed in August 2025, making it the sixth in the industry in eight months, followed by Cloud Whale, Stone, Chase and Covos.</p>

<p>Energy storage. When Dajiang launched the Power series in 2024, Wang Lei&#x27;s Zhenghao had been in business for seven years. In this business line, the first to establish product and customer relationships are the people who go out of Xinjiang.</p>

<p>3D printing. Dajiang invested in Tuozhu&#x27;s intellectual school. The co-founder of intelligent school said in an interview that he hoped to use Xinjiang&#x27;s experience to supplement its product capabilities and catch up with Tuozhu. The same group of talents stand on different sides of capital.</p>

<p>As for the most valued physical intelligence, Shi Zhihang on the top of the tower was founded by the former chief engineer of machine vision of Xinjiang. In the interview, Wang Tao&#x27;s position on entering the humanoid robot is that the technology and stage are mature.</p>

<p>·</p>

<p>Why did these people leave? No list can answer the motives for all the leavers, but two things happened at the same time.</p>

<p>One is inside. Xinjiang has been promoting organizational change since 2018. In a late interview in April 2026, Wang Tao reviewed the process of only paying attention to products in the early years and later making up for management, and confirmed that he would re-invest in interviews and large-scale recruitment in the second half of 2025. Companies need to measure resources together, and the original degree of freedom and return structure of individuals may not be retained.</p>

<p>The other one is outside. Capital is actively looking for people. Some institutions are squatting near Dajiang&#x27;s headquarters, and some teams get high valuations before the product is completed. It Shizhihang completed the $455 million Pre-A round in April 2026, which is the most eye-catching one.</p>

<p>The most quoted sentence in the interview is that I can recruit faster than dig. Another sentence that is also worth noting is that if you talk about incentives without money, you are all PUA. This sentence admits that the problem is related to the distribution, but it needs to be said more accurately. Not going public does not mean that it can&#x27;t give long-term returns. Huawei is also not listed, and it has retained a large number of business leaders by long-term employee shareholding. The real problem is not the button of the capital market, but the distribution and authorization. What a person can decide, what kind of team can be built, how to share the value created, and whether these arrangements can be sustainable.</p>

<p>For a person who has led a product line, the external choice is to hold the equity of a company and determine the direction of the product. What can be given internally, if it is just the next promotion and salary adjustment, the balance will be tilted. When the external choice of the position is the starting point of becoming a company, motivation is no longer a salary issue.</p>

<p>·</p>

<p>The cost of the organization for this is easily covered by the financing figures.</p>

<p>In the past, Xinjiang relied on a few highly capable individuals to promote product iteration. The price of this method is that knowledge grows on people. The accumulation of a person in charge is not just about drawings and codes. Which plan has failed, which abnormality is worth stopping and checking, how to verify the indicators promised by the supplier, some of these can be written into the document, and the other part should be delivered in the process of working together. People leave, and these things follow. Newcomers take over not only the job responsibilities, but also the cost of rebuilding the collaboration method.</p>

<p>The most dangerous stage of organizational adjustment is here. The old tacit understanding is invalid, and the new ability is not stable, but the product has to be delivered as usual.</p>

<p>It is also necessary to distinguish the time. Zhenghao was founded in 2017 and Tuozhu was founded in 2020, both of which are earlier than the latest round of adjustment. The year of establishment is not equal to the year of leaving. The outflow of talents is a long-term phenomenon, but the wave has been higher in the past two years.</p>

<p>·</p>

<p>Once the movement forms a scale, it will change the environment itself that retains people.</p>

<p>At least 14 of the 63 samples have a clear middle stop. After leaving Xinjiang, the founding members have been to other companies, research institutions, or started a business. Lin Maojiang&#x27;s path is Dajiang, Tuozhu, Zhenghao, and then to the desktop CNC startup team. When Yang Shuo was preparing to start a business at the end of 2024, he sent an invitation to Li Haonan, who was still in Xinjiang. Li Haonan joined after leaving the company in April 2025, and the two are jointly responsible for research and development in Miaodong.</p>

<p>Companies founded by former employees are becoming a new platform for future generations to join. For Xinjiang, when some people leave, they already know who to cooperate with, where the money comes from, and who to ask for mass production. What Renren has to face is no longer just the high salary of other companies, but a ready-made entrepreneurial network.</p>

<p>·</p>

<p>Xinjiang is also adjusting its relationship with these new companies. It invested in Chilong Power, which was founded by the former head of E-bike research and development. Zhuoyu, Amflow and Avinox are independent by spin-off. After the employees leave, the two sides can still keep in touch through capital, technology and supply chain.</p>

<p>However, the Xinjiang Department is not an organization chart that Xinjiang can still command. These companies are not all successful, and they don&#x27;t just belong to the story of Xinjiang. They belong to the new partners, investors, and the experience accumulated by the founder after DJI.</p>

<p>Biologists call the whale landing an oasis in the deep sea, and the sinking of a whale can feed a set of ecosystems for decades. There are many similar examples in business history. The people of Xerok PARC made a graphical interface, the disbandors of Bell Labs scattered throughout Silicon Valley, and Nokia directly funded retired employees to start a business with the Bridge program. The ceiling of the organization is often the soil of the industry.</p>

<p>Xinjiang has not sunk yet. Its sales are still growing, and it completed a $1 billion financing last year. However, the group of companies that grew around it have begun to participate in deciding where the next round of hardware innovation will happen and who will keep the profits.</p>

<p>The birth of all things does not mean that all things return to the frontier.</p>
