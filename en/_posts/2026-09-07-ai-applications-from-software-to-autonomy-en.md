    <h1>The next stop of AI application is not more Agent.</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">As models become more and more common, the value of applications is migrating from abilities and tools to the autonomous closed loop of work, results, transactions and the real world.</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-09-07T11:30:00+08:00">2026-09-07</time>
      <span>20 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/ai-applications-from-software-to-autonomy/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>Recently, I talked to some investors about AI applications, and the feedback I got was a bit of a fight with each other.</p>

<p>On the one hand, the story of the underlying Infra is getting more and more difficult to tell. Agent has not appeared enough killer applications. Payment, identity, Eval, Memory, Observability and other infrastructure, even if the direction is correct, it will be asked a very realistic question: how much real workload is there downstream? Alipay can be very important, but the value of Alipay does not grow out of thin air. It is followed by Taobao and transactions.</p>

<p>On the other hand, capital has begun to look at AI applications again, especially those intensive industries. Logic is no longer a smarter tool, but directly takes over some people&#x27;s work and even delivers the final business results. In the past, it sold SaaS, and in the future, it may sell Result-as-a-Service.</p>

<p>At the same time, there is another problem that cannot be avoided: the basic model has been improving. I encountered the same kind of dilemma when I started my own business last year and later worked as a Video Agent in Xinjiang. Many things that seemed to require a special agent to be done at that time, as general agents such as Claude Code and Codex become stronger, the space of exclusive products will be quickly compressed.</p>

<p>On the other side, more and more top AI researchers and entrepreneurs are entering the world model, body intelligence, robot and brain-computer interface. It seems that after the digital world is rolled to a certain extent, AI is looking for a new way into the real world.</p>

<p>These views are reasonable on their own, but it is easy to get several contradictory conclusions when put together:</p>

<ul>
  <li>AI applications will flourish again;</li>
  <li>General Agent will swallow a large number of AI applications;</li>
  <li>AI Infra is of great value, but it is not easy to establish now;</li>
  <li>Result-as-a-Service is the next wave of opportunities;</li>
  <li>The real long-term opportunity may be in Physical AI;</li>
  <li>The model is getting stronger and stronger. Is it helping the application or eliminating the application?</li>
</ul>

<p>I&#x27;m more inclined to ask another question now.</p>

<p><strong>Don&#x27;t ask &quot;what is the AI application&quot; anymore, but ask: what kind of responsibility is the machine taking over?</strong></p>

<p>The movement of this responsibility boundary can explain the next stage of industrial changes better than the category words Agent, Infra and Robot.</p>

<h2 id="一ai-的真正演化轴从生成能力到承担责任">I. The real evolution axis of AI: from generating ability to taking responsibility</h2>

<p>In the past three years, AI products have roughly gone through five levels.</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Levels and ranks</th>
      <th>What does AI do?</th>
      <th>What do people undertake?</th>
      <th>What does the customer really buy?</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Capability</td>
      <td>Generate text, pictures, codes and videos</td>
      <td>Judge, organize and execute</td>
      <td>An ability</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Copilot</td>
      <td>Assist in the work of a position</td>
      <td>Decision-making and final execution</td>
      <td>Efficiency improvement</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Agent</td>
      <td>Complete a task independently</td>
      <td>Targeting, abnormal approval</td>
      <td>Complete the task</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Operator / Service</td>
      <td>Take over a complete workflow</td>
      <td>Manage exceptions and responsibilities boundaries</td>
      <td>Business results</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Autonomous System</td>
      <td>Continuous perception, decision-making, action and self-correction</td>
      <td>Define goals and boundaries</td>
      <td>The production capacity of continuous operation</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>ChatGPT&#x27;s initial solution was &quot;Give me an answer&quot;.</p>

<p>Copilot&#x27;s solution is &quot;help me do this quickly&quot;.</p>

<p>Agent began to become &quot;You do this for me&quot;.</p>

<p>Result-as-a-Service takes a step forward: &quot;I don&#x27;t care what you do, I only buy results.&quot;</p>

<p>Robots, autonomous driving, and industrial autonomous systems continue to expand their responsibilities to the physical world: machines not only modify files and databases, but also continuously change the real state and bear the reliability requirements for these state changes.</p>

<p>Therefore, the lower evolution is not:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Chatbot → Agent → Robot</p>
</blockquote>

<p>It is:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>Information → Decision → Action → Outcome → Autonomy</strong></p>
</blockquote>

<p>Every time AI takes a step forward, it transfers some of the responsibilities that people had to bear in the past to machines.</p>

<p>This line can explain why many AI products disappear quickly, and it can also explain why other companies are getting bigger and bigger because of the stronger model.</p>

<h2 id="二应用层重新变重要不代表-wrapper-又有机会了">II. The application layer becomes important again, which does not mean that Wrapper has another chance.</h2>

<p>In 2025, U.S. companies will spend about 37 billion US dollars on generative AI, of which about 19 billion US dollars will be spent on application layers, which is more than half. Coding&#x27;s single category is about $4 billion, accounting for 55% of department-level AI spending; Vertical AI is about $3.5 billion, of which about $1.5 billion is medical. Menlo Ventures called Coding the first killer use case that really ran out.<a href="https://menlovc.com/perspective/2025-the-state-of-generative-ai-in-the-enterprise/">Menlo Ventures, 2025 State of Generative AI in the Enterprise</a></p>

<p>This set of figures shows that the application layer is indeed regaining value, but it cannot be understood as &quot;the App on the model is safe again&quot;.</p>

<p>The real change is that the application is being divided into two species.</p>

<p>The first one, I call it<strong>Ability-based application</strong>(Capability Application). What it sells is what AI itself can do: writing, summarizing, translating, generating PPT, cutting videos, searching, drawing, and writing code. The problem with this kind of product is that its core value is often the ability that the basic model has not fully provided at present. Once the model is complete, this difference will disappear.</p>

<p>The second one is<strong>Responsible application</strong>(Responsibility Application). What it sells is not &quot;what AI can do&quot;, but &quot;whether this matter has been completed in the end&quot;.</p>

<p>For example:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Has the customer&#x27;s complaint been resolved?</li>
  <li>Has the claim been closed?</li>
  <li>Has the arrears been recovered?</li>
  <li>Whether the contract has been reviewed and signed;</li>
  <li>Have the medical records, codes and payment links been processed?</li>
  <li>Whether the code has passed the test and successfully deployed.</li>
</ul>

<p>The core value of the latter does not depend on the model to maintain defects. On the contrary, the stronger the model, the greater the scope of responsibility it can assume.</p>

<p>This is the most essential difference between the two types of applications.</p>

<h2 id="三判断一个-ai-项目先做模型十倍测试">III. To judge an AI project, do the &quot;model ten-fold test&quot; first.</h2>

<p>Assuming that the capacity of the basic model is increased by ten times tomorrow and the cost is reduced by another order of magnitude, what will happen?</p>

<p>If the result is:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Users can use the basic model directly and do not need this product.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>This is a negative model gradient - the stronger the model, the closer the product is to being replaced.</p>

<p>If the result is:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>The product can automatically complete more work, reduce more labor, enter more complex scenarios, and improve the gross margin.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>This is a positive model gradient - the stronger the model, the more responsibility the product can take over. It can be called &quot;Base Model Acceleration Test&quot;.</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>After the model ability is improved by 10 times</th>
      <th>Project status</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>The core functions of the product are natively covered by the model.</td>
      <td>High-risk</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>The experience is better, but the differentiation is shrinking rapidly.</td>
      <td>Biased risk</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>The automation rate increased from 30% to 60%</td>
      <td>Good</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Artificial Exception decreased significantly</td>
      <td>Very good</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>It can be expanded from a single task to a complete Workflow.</td>
      <td>Very good</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>You can start charging Output directly.</td>
      <td>Better</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Be able to enter transactions, funds or asset flows</td>
      <td>The strongest</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>This is also the way I re-understand Video Agent now.</p>

<p>If the value chain of a Video Agent is only:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Understand the intention → Plan the clip → Call the video tool → Generate the film</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Then the general model and the general Agent become stronger and will continue to cover it downwards. Planning, tool use and multimodal understanding, which had to be done by yourself in the past, will gradually become public capabilities.</p>

<p>But if you continue to go down:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Content production → delivery → audience testing → iterative materials → conversion optimization → responsible for GMV or ROI</p>
</blockquote>

<p>It is no longer just a Video Agent, but begins to become a business Operator. The stronger the model, the lower the production cost of its unit content, the more ideas that can be tried, and the faster the closed loop.</p>

<p>This is the real boundary between &quot;eating model dividends&quot; and &quot;being replaced by models&quot;.</p>

<p>A good application should not be based on the model that can never do something, but on the more the model can do things, the more responsibilities I can take on.</p>

<h2 id="四为什么-coding-是目前最值得迁移的成功范式">IV. Why is Coding the most valuable successful paradigm to migrate at present?</h2>

<p>If you want to find rules from the fields that have been run, Coding is more worth studying than the vast majority of AI entrepreneurship cases.</p>

<p>The reason is not that programmers are more willing to try new things, but that the code world is almost naturally suitable for Agent.</p>

<p>It meets several conditions at the same time:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Input is highly digital: code, Issue, document, Git History are all naturally machine-readable;</li>
  <li>Action Space is clear: edit files, run commands, call tools;</li>
  <li>The results can be verified: Compile, Test, Benchmark, Diff;</li>
  <li>The environment can be Reset: Git can be rolled back, and Sandbox can be rebuilt;</li>
  <li>High labor cost: the same automation rate corresponds to high economic value;</li>
  <li>Model progress directly expands the boundaries of the task: from completing a line of code, to modifying a file, to handling Repository-level Task.</li>
</ol>

<p>Therefore, the product evolution of Coding is not:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>The model can write code → Coding products disappear</p>
</blockquote>

<p>It is:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Autocomplete → Chat → Edit → Agent → Repository Task → PR → SDLC</p>
</blockquote>

<p>The stronger the model, the longer the Workflow taken over by the application.</p>

<p>This is the &quot;success paradigm&quot; that should be migrated. It is not to copy the product form of a Coding Agent, but to find the same in other industries.<strong>Digital input, clear action, verifiable results, expensive labor, low-cost retry</strong>The work of.</p>

<p>On the other hand, &quot;manpower-intensive&quot; itself is not enough.</p>

<p>Many human work is not suitable for AI: input in the human mind, the environment is highly unstructured, the result is only known after half a year, a mistake causes huge losses, or a lot of value comes from relationships and responsibility endorsements rather than task execution.</p>

<p>Therefore, the more accurate screening formula should be:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>Digitality × Repeatability × Verifiability × Actionability × Economic Density</strong></p>
</blockquote>

<p>The five items are high at the same time, which is closer to the fertile soil of the next batch of AI Native Service.</p>

<h2 id="五result-as-a-service-不是换一种收费而是软件开始吞服务业">V. Result-as-a-Service is not a new charge, but software begins to swallow the service industry.</h2>

<p>Bessemer defined this trend as<strong>Owning the Outcome</strong>: In the past, software helped professional service companies work. Now AI can enter the service delivery itself and directly deliver the completed contract, claim, report or business results.<a href="https://www.bvp.com/atlas/owning-the-outcome-bessemers-ai-native-services-evaluation-framework">Bessemer Venture Partners, Owning the Outcome</a></p>

<p>This matter is important not because SaaS has been changed from Seat Pricing to Outcome Pricing.</p>

<p>The real change is that the definition of TAM has changed.</p>

<p>Traditional SaaS competes for Software Budget:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>A company has 100 employees and costs $50 per person per month.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>AI Native Service competes for Labor / Outsourcing / Professional Service Budget:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>You originally spent 10 million a year to hire and outsource to complete this business, and I spent 3 million to complete 70% of it.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Customers get a drop in costs, but the software revenue obtained by AI companies can be much higher than the traditional Seat model.</p>

<p>This is not a product upgrade of SaaS, but the first time that the software directly enters the production function of the service industry.</p>

<p>There has been a clear first signal in the customer service. Intercom&#x27;s Fin charges by Outcome, and a successful Resolution is billed; Salesforce&#x27;s Help Agent launched in 2026 also uses Pay-per-Resolution, and Unresolved or upgraded Sessions are not counted as a successful result.<a href="https://www.intercom.com/help/en/articles/8205718-fin-ai-agent-outcomes">Intercom: Fin AI Agent Outcomes</a>；<a href="https://help.salesforce.com/s/articleView?id=ai.usage_flex_credits.htm&amp;language=en_US&amp;type=5">Salesforce: Help Agent Resolutions</a></p>

<p>The reason why the charging method is worth seeing is that it reflects the change of product responsibility: from &quot;I&#x27;ll give you a set of tools&quot; to &quot;I&#x27;m responsible for the completion&quot;.</p>

<p>In the future, it is likely to see more companies that are not like software companies on the surface, but like law firms, accounting companies, customer service outsourcing, recruitment companies, insurance TPA, and medical operation service providers. The only difference is that their internal production mode has changed: most of the standard work is done by Agent, and people handle abnormal and high-responsibility scenarios.</p>

<p>The products of such companies are not just an Agent interface, but a whole set of AI Native Organization.</p>

<h2 id="六真正的-ai-native-公司核心不是-agent而是异常率曲线">VI. The core of the real AI Native company is not Agent, but the &quot;abnormality rate curve&quot;</h2>

<p>Result-as-a-Service can easily be said as a nice word, but what really determines the business model is another indicator:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>How many manual interventions are needed for every 100 tasks to be completed?</strong></p>
</blockquote>

<p>The human Exception Rate goes down, and then there is a story of human effect. If every task needs to be reviewed, AI only improves employee efficiency, and the company is still a traditional service industry. If 100 tasks only need people to handle 30 anomalies, it has begun to change human efficiency. If the model continues to become stronger and becomes 10, 3, 1, the company&#x27;s cost structure will change qualitatively.</p>

<p>Therefore, the most important business curve of AI Native Service is not DAU, not just Token Cost, but the continuous decline in manual intervention rate, as well as the resulting synchronous expansion of automation rate, gross profit and workflow:</p>

<p>This is also why many AI companies have to do Eval, Tracing, Feedback, Human Review and data closed loop by themselves in the end. These things that look like Infra are no longer subsidiary tools, but production systems in a company that really delivers business results.</p>

<h2 id="七这也解释了为什么-infra-的故事现在变难了">VII. This also explains why the story of Infra has become difficult now.</h2>

<p>Infra has not become less important.</p>

<p>On the contrary, the more responsible AI is, the more it needs:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Eval；</li>
  <li>Observability；</li>
  <li>Identity；</li>
  <li>Permission；</li>
  <li>Memory；</li>
  <li>Payment；</li>
  <li>Security；</li>
  <li>Audit；</li>
  <li>Settlement。</li>
</ul>

<p>The problem is just the order in which the value appears.</p>

<p>When the workload is still very small, it is easy to make an &quot;Agent payment agreement&quot;, &quot;Agent identity network&quot; and &quot;Agent settlement layer&quot; first, and it is easy to fall into the problem of waiting for the market.</p>

<p>The path of truly strong infrastructure is often:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>First, there is a large amount of real business → internal infrastructure grows → infrastructure forms industry standards → and then independent platformization.</strong></p>
</blockquote>

<p>Instead of:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Build an infrastructure first → wait for all future agents to call.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>The analogy of Taobao and Alipay is here. Of course, it is important to pay for infrastructure, but its early value comes from real transaction density, not &quot;paying in this direction must be important in the long run&quot;.</p>

<p>Therefore, in addition to technical problems, we must also answer a business question for AI Infra today:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>Which Agent workload am I attached to is growing rapidly?</strong></p>
</blockquote>

<p>Without downstream workload, Infra is an option; with high-frequency business flow, it becomes a control point.</p>

<p>A more realistic entrepreneurial path may be<strong>Application → Internal Infra → Platform</strong>, not the other way around.</p>

<h2 id="八通用-agent-会吞掉大量-app但未必吞掉-business">VIII. General Agent will swallow a large number of Apps, but not necessarily Business.</h2>

<p>Another extreme judgment is that general agents such as Codex, Claude, Doubao and Qianwen are getting stronger and stronger, and all vertical agents will disappear in the future.</p>

<p>I agree with the first half of the sentence and disagree with the second half.</p>

<p>The easiest general Agent to swallow is:</p>

<ul>
  <li>GUI；</li>
  <li>Fixed Workflow;</li>
  <li>Prompt engineering;</li>
  <li>General Planning;</li>
  <li>Ordinary Tool Routing;</li>
  <li>Lack of single-point ability of proprietary Context.</li>
</ul>

<p>That is to say, it will quickly compress a large amount of space for &quot;AI product shells&quot;.</p>

<p>But Business has several other layers of things:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Context、Permission、Domain Data、Operations、Compliance、Evaluation、Liability、Distribution、Transaction。</p>
</blockquote>

<p>For example, in the future, users may no longer open an insurance claim App and only say to the general agent:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Help me deal with this accident.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>General Agent calls the claim operator behind it again.</p>

<p>So the UI was swallowed, but the claim business did not disappear.</p>

<p>The software changed from Human-facing App to Agent-callable Capability, and even further to Agent-callable Business.</p>

<p>Therefore, the future structure may not be a super agent that swallows the entire software industry, but:</p>

<div class="language-text highlighter-rouge"><div class="highlight"><pre class="highlight"><code>Foundation Intelligence
        ↓
General Agent / Runtime
        ↓
Identity + Context + Permission
        ↓
Vertical Operator / Service
        ↓
Outcome
        ↓
Transaction
</code></pre></div></div>

<p>The top entrance may be highly concentrated, and the economic activities below may not be concentrated.</p>

<p>Agent will redo the distribution, which does not mean that it will have all the industrial profits.</p>

<h2 id="九模型越强护城河反而越往模型之外迁移">IX. The stronger the model, the more the moat moves outside the model.</h2>

<p>When the model itself continues to be commercialized, the moat of the application will not disappear, but will migrate.</p>

<p>In The Past, People Liked To Talk About Proprietary Model.</p>

<p>The next stage may be more worth seeing:</p>

<h3 id="1-context">1. Context</h3>

<p>Enterprise data, user history, business status, long-term memory.</p>

<h3 id="2-permission">2. Permission</h3>

<p>How far does the system have the right to execute, who approves and approves, which actions can be reversed, and which actions must be transferred manually.</p>

<h3 id="3-workflow-ownership">3. Workflow Ownership</h3>

<p>It&#x27;s not that I know how to do it, but that I&#x27;m really in the business link.</p>

<h3 id="4-eval--verification">4. Eval / Verification</h3>

<p>Whether the result has been completed or not, whether it has been completed correctly, and how to pursue responsibility.</p>

<h3 id="5-operations">5. Operations</h3>

<p>Abnormal handling, manual bottoming, customer delivery, complex long tail.</p>

<h3 id="6-distribution">6. Distribution</h3>

<p>Who can continue to get real tasks instead of experiencing traffic.</p>

<h3 id="7-regulation--liability">7. Regulation / Liability</h3>

<p>Who can bear compliance and responsibility in finance, medical care, law, industry and other fields?</p>

<h3 id="8-transaction-network">8. Transaction Network</h3>

<p>Once the product enters the capital, order, resource allocation and matching, the value capture will take another level.</p>

<p>These things have a common feature: the stronger the model, the more useful they are, not the more useless they are.</p>

<p>A good company should be Model-leveraged, not Model-dependent: the former expands the scope that can take over by the progress of the model, while the existence of the latter depends on the imperfection of the model.</p>

<h2 id="十abridge-给了另一种很值得看的演化从-wedge-到-workflow">X. Abridge gives another evolution worth watching: from Wedge to Workflow</h2>

<p>Medical Ambient Scribe looks like a function that is easily eaten by models at first: listen to the conversation between doctors and patients, and then generate medical records.</p>

<p>If the product is always stuck in &quot;voice to structured text&quot;, it is really dangerous in the long run.</p>

<p>But the evolution of Abridge is not to make Scribe more fancy, but to continue to enter the medical workflow. In June 2026, it released a new clinician intelligence platform, further connecting clinical dialogue to care delivery, payment and evidence-based treatment; in August, it announced that its context-aware clinical intelligence had been adopted by more than 300 medical systems.<a href="https://www.abridge.com/press-release/patient-centered-clinician-intelligence-platform-keynote">Abridge, June 2026</a>；<a href="https://www.abridge.com/press-release/context-aware-clinical-intelligence-extended-to-all-partners">Abridge, August 2026</a></p>

<p>This path is very typical:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>Point Capability → Workflow → System of Action → Business Outcome</strong></p>
</blockquote>

<p>Many successful AI applications today are really worth migrating, not the first version of the product, but the process of going down.</p>

<p>A Narrow Wedge is just an entrance to real scenes, Context, data and distribution. The real value of the company comes from whether it can continue to expand its responsibilities along the business chain.</p>

<h2 id="十一physical-ai-不是-agent-的下一站而是另一条并行的责任扩张">XI. Physical AI is not the &quot;next stop&quot; of Agent, but another parallel responsibility expansion.</h2>

<p>Let&#x27;s look at world models, embodied intelligence and robots.</p>

<p>It is easy to form a linear narrative:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>LLM → Agent → World Model → Robot → BCI</p>
</blockquote>

<p>I think this line is too neat, and it will even mislead the judgment of starting a business.</p>

<p>The common point between Digital Agent and Physical AI is not that &quot;one is a virtual actuator and the other is a real actuator&quot;, but they both form a closed loop:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>Observe → Understand → Predict → Plan → Act → Observe Again</strong></p>
</blockquote>

<p>In the digital world, this closed loop faces files, databases, browsers, software and APIs.</p>

<p>In the physical world, it faces the boundaries of space, objects, friction, force, occlusion, energy and human security.</p>

<p>So a more accurate statement is:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Agent is Digital Agency; Robot is Physical Agency.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>The two are two parallel branches of AI autonomy.</p>

<p>But the robot body cannot be understood as a simple and replaceable &quot;actuator&quot;.</p>

<p>Gemini Robotics ER 2 released by Google DeepMind in 2026 illustrates this problem: the high-level embodied reasoning model is responsible for understanding the physical world, multi-step planning, collaboration and task status tracking, and then entrusts the specific movement execution to the lower-level VLA. High-level Reasoning can be more and more common, and low-level Motor Control is still strongly related to specific embodiment.<a href="https://deepmind.google/models/gemini-robotics/embodied-reasoning/">Google DeepMind, Gemini Robotics ER 2</a></p>

<p>In other words, the brain may become more and more common, and the cerebellal and the body will not be so common.</p>

<p>Sensors, degrees of freedom, actuators, structure, delay, dynamics and security restrictions will in turn determine how intelligence is implemented.</p>

<p>Soft and hard combination is not &quot;adding a shell to AI&quot;, but the common optimization of Model × Data × Control × Sensor × Actuator × Mechanical Design.</p>

<h2 id="十二为什么顶尖-ai-人才去做-world-model不等于所有创业者都该去做机器人">12. Why do top AI talents do World Model? It doesn&#x27;t mean that all entrepreneurs should do robots.</h2>

<p>Now many front-line researchers are turning to Spatial Intelligence, World Model and Physical AI, which is certainly an important signal, but there is also a clear selection bias.</p>

<p>There are at least three different reasons.</p>

<p>First, the Frontier of pure Language Intelligence is extremely crowded. Those who can continue to do cutting-edge research will naturally look for problems that have not been solved on a large scale: 3D, Dynamics, Physics, Manipulation, Long-horizon Planning.</p>

<p>Second, the TAM of the physical world is indeed greater. Digital Agent is mainly in Cognitive Labor, and Physical AI has the opportunity to enter Physical Labor. A large amount of costs in manufacturing, logistics, agriculture, construction and service industries are not in the software budget, but in people, equipment and real operations.</p>

<p>Thirdly, It Is Also The Most Easily Ignored: The Commercialization Of Physical AI Is Also Much More Difficult.</p>

<p>The code world can be Git Reset, and there is no Ctrl+Z in the physical world.</p>

<p>The failure of the digital agent can be retry, rolled back, sandbox isolation; a robot error may lead to collision, damage, line stoppage and even personal risk. Real data collection is expensive, and the environment is longer. For each additional execution unit, manufacturing, deployment, maintenance and after-sales service are also needed.</p>

<p>Therefore, the longer-term TAM is greater, which does not mean that the recent start-up return is better.</p>

<p>These two judgments can be valid at the same time: Physical AI may be the largest long-term Frontier of AI; Digital Agent / AI Native Service is still a more certain commercialization path in the next 3–5 years.</p>

<h2 id="十三世界模型的价值也不应该被简化成机器人终于有大脑了">XIII. The value of the world model should not be simplified to &quot;robots finally have brains&quot;</h2>

<p>World Labs will divide World Model into Renderer, Simulator and Planner in 2026. The key link it really emphasizes is to enable Agent to act, learn and be evaluated in the world, rather than just generating a &quot;real-looking&quot; video.</p>

<p>The Real-to-Sim-to-Real system released in July is to convert the real environment into a simulated environment and then use it for robot strategy training and evaluation.<a href="https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/real-to-sim-to-real">World Labs, Building Worlds That Train Robots</a></p>

<p>On September 1, World Labs released Atlas again, putting text, images, videos and 3D into a unified spatial context, and clearly taking robotics simulation as one of its uses.<a href="https://www.worldlabs.ai/blog/atlas">World Labs, Atlas</a></p>

<p>The most noteworthy thing here is not the &quot;generative world&quot; itself, but the opportunity for Simulation to become the training environment and Eval environment of Physical AI.</p>

<p>Why is Coding easy to run? Because the code naturally has Compile, Test and Sandbox.</p>

<p>What robots have always lacked is a set of equally cheap, repeatable and large-scale training and verification environment.</p>

<p>If World Model really brings value, it is likely not because it is more generated than video models, but because it reduces the cost of Physical Agent to acquire training data, test strategies and discover Failure Region.</p>

<p>This goes back to the same industrial logic: what is really valuable is not the ability display, but whether it can enter the closed loop of production.</p>

<h2 id="十四bci-应该单独看它更像-biological-io而不是另一种机器人">14. BCI should be looked at separately. It is more like Biological I/O than another robot.</h2>

<p>Brain-computer interface is often placed in the same story of &quot;AI enters the real world&quot; with Physical AI, but the industry logic is not the same.</p>

<p>Most of today&#x27;s BCI mainly solves the input of Human → Machine: movement intention, Cursor Control, Speech Decoding; part of the closed-loop neuroregulation includes Machine → Human stimulation.</p>

<p>It is closer to a<strong>Biological I/O Layer</strong>, instead of a new Physical Actuator.</p>

<p>In 2026, Nature Reviews Bioengineering is still discussing the evolution from speech BCI to language BCI; at the same time, the long-term stability, inter-patient generalization, clinical scalability and neural data protection of implanted BCI are still core issues.<a href="https://www.nature.com/articles/s44222-026-00460-4">Nature Reviews Bioengineering, 2026</a></p>

<p>Therefore, robots, World Model and BCI can share the big narrative of &quot;AI is coming out of the pure text world&quot;, but they cannot be judged by the same set of schedules and business models.</p>

<h2 id="十五把这些方向放在一起ai-正在进入三个世界">Fifteen. Putting these directions together, AI is entering three worlds.</h2>

<p>At present, I prefer to divide the future AI application into three parallel routes.</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>World</th>
      <th>The form of AI</th>
      <th>The main target of action</th>
      <th>Main value pool</th>
      <th>Current bottleneck</th>
      <th>Recent business certainty</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Digital World</td>
      <td>Agent / Operator</td>
      <td>Software, documents, accounts, business processes</td>
      <td>Cognitive Labor / Service / Transaction</td>
      <td>Context, authority, reliability, organizational adoption</td>
      <td>Highest</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Physical World</td>
      <td>Robot / Autonomous Machine</td>
      <td>Space, objects, equipment, real tasks</td>
      <td>Physical Labor / Asset Utilization</td>
      <td>Data, control, hardware, reliability, cost</td>
      <td>Medium</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Biological World</td>
      <td>BCI / Human Augmentation</td>
      <td>The boundary between the nervous system and human ability</td>
      <td>Healthcare / Augmentation</td>
      <td>Clinical, stability, supervision, ethics</td>
      <td>Lower</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>In the end, the three lines are doing the same thing: changing the machine from &quot;giving advice&quot; to &quot;continuous action on an environment&quot;.</p>

<p>However, the environment is different, and the product rules are completely different.</p>

<h2 id="十六ai-应用的价值池会沿着责任梯度不断扩大">16. The value pool of AI application will continue to expand along the responsibility gradient.</h2>

<p>If we look at the economic value, we can also see another very important migration. The earliest AI products competed for the Software Budget; Agent began to enter the Labor Budget; AI Native Service further entered the outsourcing and professional service budget; after truly completing the business results, it continued to enter the Transaction Budget; Physical AI expanded to the physical labor and asset operation budget.</p>

<p>It can be written as:</p>

<div class="language-text highlighter-rouge"><div class="highlight"><pre class="highlight"><code>Capability
    ↓
Software
    ↓
Labor
    ↓
Service
    ↓
Outcome
    ↓
Transaction
    ↓
Autonomous Operation
</code></pre></div></div>

<p>The lower you go, the bigger the TAM.</p>

<p>But at the same time, the responsibility is heavier: stronger reliability, Eval, authority, insurance, compliance, capital and operational capabilities are required.</p>

<p>That&#x27;s why it&#x27;s getting easier and easier to &quot;make an AI function&quot;, while it&#x27;s not getting easier to &quot;be a really big AI company&quot;.</p>

<h2 id="十七未来最重要的公司可能根本不像ai-应用公司">17. The most important company in the future may not be like an &quot;AI application company&quot; at all.</h2>

<p>If the above judgment is valid, the largest AI company in the future may not grow into the Chatbot or Agent App that is familiar today.</p>

<p>It may look like:</p>

<ul>
  <li>A law firm;</li>
  <li>An accounting service company;</li>
  <li>An insurance claims company;</li>
  <li>A customer service operator;</li>
  <li>A recruitment service company;</li>
  <li>A logistics operator;</li>
  <li>A factory automation service provider.</li>
</ul>

<p>It&#x27;s just that its production function is completely different.</p>

<p>Traditional companies:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Software + 1000 Human Workers → Service</p>
</blockquote>

<p>AI Native Company：</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Models + Agents + Workflow + Eval + 100 Exception Handlers → Service</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Physical AI may further become:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Models + Robots + Remote Ops + Maintenance → Physical Outcome</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Therefore, the boundary between &quot;AI application&quot; and &quot;AI company&quot; will become more and more blurred in the future.</p>

<p>The company itself is an application.</p>

<h2 id="十八下一批机会我会优先看六种形态">18. In the next batch of opportunities, I will give priority to six forms.</h2>

<p>If I scan the future based on this framework, instead of chasing the VC trend in a certain quarter, I will give priority to six types of companies.</p>

<h3 id="1-digital-outcome-company">1. Digital Outcome Company</h3>

<p>Cut in from a digital task and ultimately be responsible for the complete business results.</p>

<p>For example, claims settlement, accounts receivable, compliance, customer service, taxation, procurement, recruitment and operation.</p>

<h3 id="2-ai-native-service">2. AI Native Service</h3>

<p>In the past, it was mainly delivered by professional manpower, but now people can be turned into Exception Layer through AI.</p>

<p>The key is not whether there is an Agent, but whether the Human Exception Rate can continue to decline.</p>

<h3 id="3-vertical-transaction-network">3. Vertical Transaction Network</h3>

<p>Not only to complete the work, but also to enter orders, funds, matching and resource allocation.</p>

<p>This is a step from Workflow to the real industrial profit pool.</p>

<h3 id="4-physical-operator">4. Physical Operator</h3>

<p>Don&#x27;t sell the robot itself, but sell every handling, every square meter of cleaning, every inspection, and every qualified part.</p>

<p>Physical Result-as-a-Service may be closer to AI&#x27;s value capture than simple hardware sales.</p>

<h3 id="5-human-augmentation-device">5. Human Augmentation Device</h3>

<p>If smart hardware is just &quot;putting ChatGPT into another shell&quot;, the long-term value is limited.</p>

<p>What is really meaningful is what new Observe/Act Channel it adds to AI: first-person vision, Always-on Audio, physiological signals, spatial positioning, movement, grasping, and identity confirmation.</p>

<h3 id="6-workload-born-infrastructure">6. Workload-born Infrastructure</h3>

<p>First, solve Eval, payment, permission, Memory, Security and other problems in the real application, and wait until the workload is large enough before platformization.</p>

<p>This is more stable than betting directly on a future standard out of business.</p>

<h2 id="十九相反我会对五类项目保持高度警惕">19. On the contrary, I will be highly vigilant about the five types of projects.</h2>

<p>First,<strong>Ability Wrapper</strong>: The main value comes from the basic model and will not be done for the time being.</p>

<p>Second,<strong>Small replica of General Agent</strong>: There is no unique Context, Distribution or business responsibility.</p>

<p>Third,<strong>Infra two floors in advance</strong>: The direction is correct for a long time, but the real workload does not exist yet.</p>

<p>Fourth,<strong>AI hardware without new Observe / Act capability</strong>: It&#x27;s just a device shell.</p>

<p>Fifth,<strong>Use long-term Frontier to replace recent products.</strong>: World models, general robots and BCI are all important, but &quot;important&quot; and &quot;suitable for starting a business now&quot; are not the same thing.</p>

<h2 id="二十最后我会用十个问题判断一个-ai-创业机会">20. Finally, I will judge an AI entrepreneurial opportunity with ten questions.</h2>

<p>Looking at an AI project in the future, I&#x27;d rather ask the following questions first than watch Demo first.</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Question</th>
      <th>Good answer</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Why does the customer really pay?</td>
      <td>To complete the work or results, not the AI function</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Where is the money spent now?</td>
      <td>Labor / Service / Transaction, not just Software</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>What about after the model ability is improved tenfold?</td>
      <td>The automation rate is rising, not the disappearance of products.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Is the work digitalized?</td>
      <td>Input, status and action can be accessed by most machines.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Can the result be verified?</td>
      <td>Have clear completion conditions and Eval</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Can Agent act directly?</td>
      <td>It can not only make suggestions, but also change the business status.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Does each execution generate feedback?</td>
      <td>Fail / Correction / Reward data can be formed</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Will manual intervention continue to decline?</td>
      <td>Human Exception Rate has a clear downward path</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Can it be expanded from Point to Workflow?</td>
      <td>There is a longer chain of responsibility behind Wedge.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Can you finally enter Outcome / Transaction?</td>
      <td>You can get a bigger value pool.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>If it is hardware, add two more questions:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>What perception channels does it add to AI that they didn&#x27;t have before?</p>
</blockquote>

<blockquote>
  <p>What action space does it add to AI that it didn&#x27;t have before?</p>
</blockquote>

<p>If you can&#x27;t answer, it&#x27;s usually not AI Native Hardware.</p>

<h2 id="结语未来真正稀缺的不是智能而是可靠地替人负责">Conclusion: What is really scarce in the future is not intelligence, but &quot;reliably responsible for others&quot;</h2>

<p>In the past three years, everyone has been discussing whether Intelligence will become Commodity.</p>

<p>Now I think this problem is no longer the most important thing.</p>

<p>It is almost certain that the model capacity will continue to rise. More reasoning, generation, planning and Tool Use will be absorbed by Foundation Model and General Agent, and a large number of AI products that seem independent today will disappear.</p>

<p>But this does not mean that the application layer is worthless.</p>

<p>On the contrary,<strong>The cheaper the intelligence is, the more the really scarce things will migrate from &quot;have intelligence&quot; to &quot;let intelligence enter the real world and take responsibility&quot;.</strong></p>

<p>In the digital world, this means Context, Permission, Workflow, Eval, Operations and Outcome.</p>

<p>In the physical world, it means Sensor, Embodiment, Control, World Model, Safety and Reliability.</p>

<p>In the biological world, this means long-term stable Neural I/O, clinical effect and responsibility boundary.</p>

<p>Therefore, the next stop of AI application is not to do more agents.</p>

<p>It is to let AI gradually become a real producer from a model that can speak, think and call tools: complete work, deliver results, enter into transactions, and finally continue to operate independently in the digital and physical worlds.</p>

<p>If I have to summarize my current judgment on the future of AI application in one sentence:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p><strong>The industrial value of AI is migrating from &quot;capability premium&quot; to &quot;responsibility premium&quot;. The model is responsible for making the machine smarter and smarter, and the real big company is responsible for making the machine more and more entrusted.</strong></p>
</blockquote>
