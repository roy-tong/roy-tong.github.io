    <h1>After WAIC: The AI industry began to pay for &quot;making things done&quot;</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">From Coding, Agent, RSI to body intelligence, competitive units in 2026 are changing from models to systems.</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-07-23T08:00:00+08:00">2026-07-23</time>
      <span>12 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/waic-from-models-to-systems/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>
      
        <span class="post-tags">
          <a href="/en/tags/#ai">#AI</a><a href="/en/tags/#waic">#WAIC</a><a href="/en/tags/#agent">#Agent</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E5%85%B7%E8%BA%AB%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD">#Body intelligence</a>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>In the past six months, I have used iRead to continuously track AI applications, body intelligence and AI hardware. By July 21, 23,930 articles from 112 sources had been accumulated in the local database. In four days of WAIC, there were 166 articles directly related to the conference.</p>

<p>These materials repeatedly point to the same change: the way the industry judges an AI product is changing.</p>

<p>Model running score, robot action and hardware parameters are still important, but customers have begun to ask more: how is the performance of long-term operation, who will take over when it fails, and how much does a successful task cost? The industry has not yet entered the harvest period of scale, but it has begun to seriously pay for &quot;making things done&quot;.</p>

<h2 id="waic-改变的是评价尺度">What WAIC has changed is the evaluation scale.</h2>

<p>This year&#x27;s exhibitor structure is very explanatory.</p>

<p>Depend on<a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/kmIponLZfw6Vm5PzJ9rXGA">Statistics of the General Assembly of Jiazi Light Year</a>, WAIC 2026 has more than 1,100 Chinese and foreign enterprises and more than 4,000 exhibits. There are 242 enterprises related to robots and intelligent hardware, and 206 enterprises related to computing power, chips and infrastructure. Among the large model and generative AI exhibitors, 83 companies use Agent or intelligent application as the main label, and only 18 basic large models.</p>

<p>The basic model is still important. It is becoming a means of production for the whole industry. After an enterprise accesses a strong model, it also needs to deal with data, permissions, processes, evaluation, costs and organizational transformation, so that the model ability can become a result.</p>

<p>Three very specific changes can be seen on WAIC:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Chip companies began to discuss the output, energy efficiency and system stability of effective Token;</li>
  <li>Agent The company is no longer satisfied with answering questions and begins to be responsible for the results of the task;</li>
  <li>When robots and AI hardware leave the chat box, they must face space, power consumption, security and real users.</li>
</ul>

<p>I prefer to regard 2026 as the year when the system delivery begins to be tested. Competing units are changing from a single model, a single chip and a single robot to a system that can complete tasks stably.</p>

<h2 id="coding-为什么站在这轮变化的最前面">Why is Coding at the forefront of this round of change?</h2>

<p>Coding is the AI application closest to &quot;task economy&quot; at present. The reason is very simple: the code can be compiled, the test can be run, and whether the requirements are completed can be verified by machines and people.</p>

<p>With verifiable feedback, Agent can work continuously for a longer time. It can read code, modify files, run tests, observe errors, and then continue to correct. Of course, AI can also be used for design, documents and video creation, but &quot;whether the work can be delivered&quot; often depends on people&#x27;s aesthetic and business judgment, and the feedback is far from clear as the code.</p>

<p><a href="https://www.bilibili.com/video/BV1Mhgz6QEHA/">Let&#x27;s talk about LateTalk&#x27;s 2026 Q2 quarterly report later</a>Explain another meaning of Coding: it is not only the clearest revenue entrance at present, but also may become the research and development infrastructure of the next generation of AI.</p>

<p>When the long-range Agent can read papers, make hypotheses, write code, run experiments and analyze the results, Coding not only helps programmers improve efficiency, but also begins to enter Auto Research. It is even trying to improve the model training itself.</p>

<p>This path is often summarized as:</p>

<p><code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">Coding + 长程 Agent → Auto Research → Recursive Self-Improvement</code></p>

<p>RSI, that is, recursive self-improvement, is a concept that can easily be declared successful in advance. The current public evidence is closer to research process automation and local self-improvement: AI can optimize code, experimental tools, training formulas or GPU Kernel, and people are still choosing the research direction, judging whether the conclusions are novel, and dealing with failures that are not covered by the evaluation.</p>

<p>Brushing a Benchmark higher can only prove one local optimization. What can better explain the question is whether the same set of systems can continue to become stronger on different tasks. At that point, the advantage of the leading company may change from &quot;one position ahead&quot; to &quot;having faster acceleration&quot;.</p>

<p>Today, there is not enough evidence to prove that the general RSI has appeared. But Coding is letting AI participate in the production of the next generation of AI, which is worth following for a long time.</p>

<h2 id="agent-的难题已经进入组织内部">Agent&#x27;s problems have entered the organization.</h2>

<p>When an individual uses Agent, it usually only takes a few more minutes to fail once. After enterprises put Agent into procurement, finance, customer service or research and development processes, errors may mean data leakage, overreach, wrong payment or production interruption.</p>

<p>Therefore, enterprise agents need three types of context: public knowledge, enterprise private knowledge and organizational operation rules. The traditional knowledge base only covers a part of it. Information limitation, access rights, approval relationship, and suspension and rollback after task failure are the troubles after entering the core process.</p>

<p>A group of abilities that used to be less conspicuous began to become important:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Context: Organize scattered documents, business objects and implicit experiences into callable information;</li>
  <li>Runtime: Provide sandboxes, browsers, tools and resource scheduling for long-term tasks;</li>
  <li>Evals: Continuously check the quality of the results after going online;</li>
  <li>Governance: control authority, budget, audit and manual takeover.</li>
</ul>

<p>On WAIC, PPIO disclosed that the business scale of Agent sandbox increased 123 times a year after it was launched. This figure comes from the company&#x27;s caliber and still needs to be verified by the customer;<a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/gH7pvWzd4mlYWWEKJSS2hQ">The evaluation method proposed in the interview</a>More complete: Don&#x27;t just look at how cheap a model call is, but also count the context, tool call, failure retry and manual check as a successful task.</p>

<p>This also explains why the demand for computing power does not necessarily decrease after the unit price of inference falls. Agent will increase the call round and context length. A Token has become cheaper, but a task consumes more Token.</p>

<p>Next, Agent products should give priority to the delivery rate, manual modification, takeover rate and unit successful task cost, rather than the number of generations.</p>

<h2 id="机器人开始谈工作但还没有证明规模经济">Robots began to talk about &quot;work&quot;, but the economy of scale has not yet been proven.</h2>

<p>WAIC The most easily spread robot content in the past was dancing, running and somersaults. In this year&#x27;s booths, there are obviously more pharmacies, warehousing, automotive wiring harnesses, optical module testing, retail shelves and laundry rooms.</p>

<p><a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/3Ld3rm5x9YjDJFzKPUGiHA">AI Technology Review visits more than 100 robot booths</a>After that, the change is summarized as &quot;application display replaces functional performance&quot;. The display of several companies is also beginning to approach the production problem:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Galaxy General extends the single task to 3 to 5 minutes;</li>
  <li>It Shi Zhihang shows the fine connection of flexible wire harness;</li>
  <li>Leju discloses that the standard industrial scenario can be implemented within two weeks;</li>
  <li>Jizhijia allows the robot to cooperate with the original warehousing system;</li>
  <li>Su Du tested the online response with target movement, visual interference and tasks outside the question bank.</li>
</ul>

<p>These demonstrations are more valuable than simple performances, but the landing of the booth and the landing of the factory are still two things.</p>

<p><a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/qG2V7AkZ6Np0QefsiePMkQ">A demonstration of making tea recorded by Hufu</a>It took about six and a half minutes, during which there were capture failure, repeated alignment, and back tilt during operation, and the assistance of engineers was also needed. It does not show that the robot &quot;can&#x27;t do it&quot;, but it clearly shows the distance between Demo and the product.</p>

<p>Next, the robot company should disclose another set of indicators: the number of paid deployments, continuous operating hours, average failure-free time, manual takeover rate, beat, number of deployments, maintenance costs and customer expansion points.</p>

<p>A set of systems succeeded 99 times and failed 1 time, which sounds good. But if it has to complete tens of thousands of movements every day, the remaining 1% will still become a lot of abnormalities. The single success rate is far from production availability, and there are two doors of continuous operation and failure recovery.</p>

<h2 id="具身智能需要什么样的数据">What kind of data is needed for embedied intelligence?</h2>

<p>This year&#x27;s body forum repeatedly discusses the data. Real machine data is the most direct, but slow and expensive; human first-person data is easier to expand, but it needs to solve the difference between human and robot action space; the number of Internet videos is huge, lacking power, touch and failure tags; simulation data is cheap and controllable, and Sim-to-Real cannot be avoided.</p>

<p>I don&#x27;t think a certain data will win alone. I am more optimistic about companies that can unify real machines, human demonstrations, simulations and online failures into training and evaluation: they record why the robot fails, how to recover, and whether the next version is really better.</p>

<p>This has a similar structure to Auto Research in the digital world: perform tasks, get feedback, form new data, and then update the system. The difference is that the code world can be rolled back quickly, and the failure of the physical world will damage equipment, affect production, and even hurt people.</p>

<p>Robots may become another type of carrier of RSI, but autonomous trial and error must be constrained by safety boundaries, simulation, real machine evaluation and manual takeover. Otherwise, the so-called data flywheel is just creating accidents faster.</p>

<p>Richard Sutton made this clear when talking about the &quot;age of experience&quot; in WAIC: it is not enough to learn the knowledge that human beings already have. The intelligence of continuous learning requires first-person experience through action, observation and reward. At the same time, he also reminded the audience that today&#x27;s AI is still unreliable and will cause hallucinations.<a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/D2_cPST9ZtQaxTDlhh6TnQ">The arrangement of this speech</a>It is worth watching with the lively robot demonstration.</p>

<h2 id="ai-硬件争夺的是行动权">AI hardware competes for the right to act.</h2>

<p>AI mobile phones, glasses, headphones, rings and companion hardware are all competing for the entrance of continuous perception. The closer the device is to the user, the more continuous the context is obtained, and the more tasks can be completed.</p>

<p>But a system can read albums, messages and locations, and can call maps, orders and payments, which means that it also has the right to make big mistakes.</p>

<p>AI hardware needs to obtain the long-term authorization of the user before the model capability has the opportunity to be transformed into product value. Low-risk tasks can be completed automatically, and high-risk operations should be returned to the user for confirmation; sensitive data should be processed locally as much as possible, and complex reasoning should be entered into the cloud; each key operation should be audited and rolled back in a limited way.</p>

<p><a href="https://mp.weixin.qq.com/s/2HERNh-u9A8NxWk0UynJJw">The Agentic OS displayed by Jieyue in WAIC</a>Adopt a similar idea. It has not yet proven large-scale mass production and long-term retention, but it has raised the problem of trust other than cross-app operation: the operational ability needs to be built on user authorization.</p>

<p>This competition will also redistribute the service entrance. If the system Agent can only open the App, the option is still in the hands of the Super App; if it can filter, order and pay for users, the mobile phone system and model company will regain the distribution right. Whether AI hardware can be established may depend on whether the model, system, supply chain, channel and service ecology can be organized into a complete product.</p>

<h2 id="强者愈强只在一些条件下成立">&quot;The stronger the strong&quot; is only valid under some conditions.</h2>

<p>Leading model companies do have a set of compounding structures: stronger models bring more users, more uses produce more failure samples and preference data, income supports computing power and talents, and research and development tools help develop the next generation of models. Coding is very special in this structure, because it not only generates income but also improves R&amp;D efficiency.</p>

<p>But the model competition has been alternately leading in the past three years. Talents will flow, and the cost and controllability of open source models will enter the enterprise. Vertical companies that master special data, field evaluation and customer processes still have the opportunity to establish a stronger system locally, while enterprise sales, authority governance, hardware mass production and after-sales service need another set of long-term accumulation. Therefore, my judgment of &quot;the stronger the stronger&quot; is that revenue, distribution and R&amp;D efficiency will be concentrated first, and technological leadership may not be permanently concentrated; only when a company takes the lead in achieving a movable and sustainable RSI can the existing balance be truly broken.</p>

<h2 id="未来半年我会盯着这些数字">In the next six months, I will keep an eye on these numbers.</h2>

<p>There is no shortage of new concepts in the industry, but what is lacking is indicators that can be compared across quarters. In the next 3 to 6 months, I will pay most attention to the following groups of numbers:</p>

<p><strong>AI application and Agent</strong></p>

<ul>
  <li>The total cost of a successful task;</li>
  <li>The proportion of results directly into production;</li>
  <li>At what stage does manual takeover take place, and whether it will decline with use;</li>
  <li>The actual usage depth of renewal and paid users.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Body intelligence</strong></p>

<ul>
  <li>The success rate after hundreds of hours of continuous operation;</li>
  <li>Can it recover independently after grabbing, covering, and moving the target;</li>
  <li>How much data, engineers and commissioning time do you need for a new workstat?</li>
  <li>Whether the customer will expand from a single pilot to a multi-unit and multi-workstat.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>AI hardware</strong></p>

<ul>
  <li>Retained for 30 days and 90 days;</li>
  <li>The monthly cloud reasoning cost of each device;</li>
  <li>The confirmation rate of high-risk operation, misoperation and privacy events;</li>
  <li>Battery life, heat, return rate and mass production yield.</li>
</ul>

<p><strong>Auto Research and RSI</strong></p>

<ul>
  <li>How much labor is needed for research direction, experimental design and final judgment;</li>
  <li>Whether the same system can repeatedly improve on different tasks;</li>
  <li>Whether the results can be reproduced by the independent team;</li>
  <li>Whether to complete the safety regression test again after the ability is improved.</li>
</ul>

<p>These indicators may not be as good as the press conference video, but they are closer to industrial progress.</p>

<h2 id="2026交付开始受检验收获尚早">2026: Delivery begins to be inspected, and the harvest is still early.</h2>

<p>Since this year, in my database, the title clearly discusses that the proportion of articles about Agent has increased from about 3.5% in January to about 6.8% in June; body intelligence and robots have remained at about 12% to 14% for a long time; financing, valuation and IPO discussions have increased significantly in May and June.</p>

<p>These figures can only reflect attention, not replace revenue and customer repurchase. Capital heat has been ahead of business maturity, and a large number of products are still in the exhibition, pilot and small batch deployment stages, and the public indicators mainly come from the company itself.</p>

<p>After WAIC 2026, Agents, robots, AI hardware and computing power began to undergo stricter delivery inspections. Companies that can stay will continue to record failures and use these data to make delivery more stable and cheaper. Which systems can do things well for a long time and stably are more worth tracking than what AI can do.</p>
