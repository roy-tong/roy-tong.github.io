    <h1>From AI application to the real world: my turn</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">Why stop starting a software business and start a new understanding of hardware, physical intelligence and the next generation of AI products?</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-07-19T12:00:00+08:00">2026-07-19</time>
      <span>9 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/from-ai-software-to-physical-world/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>
      
        <span class="post-tags">
          <a href="/en/tags/#ai">#AI</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E5%85%B7%E8%BA%AB%E6%99%BA%E8%83%BD">#Body intelligence</a><a href="/en/tags/#ai%E7%A1%AC%E4%BB%B6">#AI hardware</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E5%88%9B%E4%B8%9A">#Start a business</a>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>In August 2025, I stopped my previous AI application entrepreneurship; then I entered DJI, working in image software, AI Agent and software and hardware integration products; in the past six months, I have invested a lot of time in the research of in-body intelligence and AI hardware.</p>

<p>Several turns revolve around the same group of questions: When AI capabilities are getting easier and easier to obtain, what else does the product lack? Where will the competitive threshold of the next-generation company be established?</p>

<p>This article records my current answer. When new facts appear in the future, I will continue to correct them.</p>

<h2 id="为什么我在-2025-年-8-月停下了-ai-应用创业">Why did I stop AI application entrepreneurship in August 2025?</h2>

<p>Around 2025, the development threshold of AI applications fell faster than I expected.</p>

<p>Products that used to require a complete team can be made available in a few days or weeks by a few people or even one person. Models, development frameworks, cloud services and open source components are becoming more and more accessible, and the time required from idea to launch is greatly shortened.</p>

<p>Development becomes easier, and new products emerge at a rate that far exceeds the growth of user demand. Users are faced with a large number of AI tools with similar functions every day, the switching cost is getting lower and lower, and the attention is quickly distracted. Even if a product is well done, it may only get a short freshness and soon be covered by the next batch of products.</p>

<p>What is more scarce in entrepreneurship is gradually from &quot;making products&quot; to &quot;let users see, remember and continue to use&quot;. The weight of content, channel, brand, growth and community management has also increased: in many cases, entrepreneurs have to let the product be seen before they have a chance to prove their product judgment.</p>

<p>I don&#x27;t think software entrepreneurship has lost its value. Products that can enter business processes, master unique data, form network effects, or complete complex tasks for a long time can still establish a high threshold. Just looking back at the direction I made at that time, I didn&#x27;t see a strong and lasting enough structural advantage.</p>

<p>So I chose to stop.</p>

<p>This suspension made me realize that model capabilities can be purchased, functions can be copied, and interfaces can also be imitated.</p>

<h2 id="在-dji-的大半年我重新理解了软件与硬件">In more than half a year of DJI, I re-understood software and hardware.</h2>

<p>After entering DJI, it was the first time that I observed how a mature hardware company faced new competition so closely.</p>

<p>The threshold of hardware is very specific. Optics, structure, chips, sensors, engineering design, supply chain, mass production, quality control and after-sales service, any of them need to be accumulated for a long time. Unlike software prototypes, it can be copied quickly in a few weeks.</p>

<p>But hardware leadership is not automatically equal to user experience leadership.</p>

<p>In categories such as panoramic images, a large part of the pressure brought by companies like Insta360 comes from software. Shooting is only the starting point of the whole experience, followed by material management, editing, mirror operation, template, sharing and cross-device collaboration. Users often want a piece of content that is easier to get and more willing to share; a set of beautiful hardware parameters itself is not enough for users to stay.</p>

<p>After the software lowers the creative threshold, it will also change the user&#x27;s judgment on the value of the hardware. Hardware determines the upper limit of creative ability, and software determines whether most people can reach this upper limit.</p>

<p>This experience has left two understandings.</p>

<p>For hardware companies, software has been directly involved in product competition. It transforms the sensor, computing power and structural design into results that users can perceive, and also determines whether the product can continue to be updated after purchase.</p>

<p>Software companies are facing another situation. When the threshold of model, development and distribution continues to decline, if advantages cannot be established in the base model, proprietary data, core workflow, content ecology or social relationships, hardware may become a way to build a long-term threshold. It can bring exclusive sensors, continuous data, stable access to use, and stronger control over the complete experience.</p>

<p>Of course, hardware is never a shortcut. Supply chain, inventory, mass production, reliability and after-sales service will increase the difficulty of starting a business by an order of magnitude. It is difficult to do without creating value. Models, software, hardware, data and services must form a complete system.</p>

<p>I believe more and more that it is difficult for excellent AI products to win in the next stage by relying on only one function. Competition will gradually move from single-point capability to systematic capability.</p>

<h2 id="下一步我为什么首先看具身智能">In the next step, why do I look at body intelligence first?</h2>

<p>Continuing along this line, I began to put physical intelligence at the forefront.</p>

<p>What I mean by body intelligence is wider than that of humanoid robots. Automobiles, drones, quadruped robots, robotic arms, cleaning robots, intelligent household appliances and various autonomous devices can be included in the broad framework of &quot;intelligence into the physical world&quot;.</p>

<p>In the long run, I tend to believe that the market size of pan-body may exceed that of mobile phones and cars. It corresponds to the intelligence of a large number of devices, tools and spaces in the real world: movement, operation, transportation, manufacturing, care, companionship and service may gradually be involved by intelligent devices.</p>

<p>This long-term judgment does not mean that it is mature in the short term.</p>

<p>Today&#x27;s physical intelligence is still at a very early stage. The model&#x27;s understanding of the real world is still unstable, high-quality data is scarce, the ontology and key parts are still iterating rapidly, and the supply chain has not been fixed. A successful Demo is far from continuous, safe and low-cost work in real scenarios, as well as productization, engineering and commercialization.</p>

<p>Funds and entrepreneurs have entered in advance. Although there are differences in different statistical calibers, there are already hundreds of domestic related enterprises. According to the data cited by the media by IT Juzi, from July 2025 to June 2026, there were 503 financings in the domestic primary market of physical intelligence, with a total amount of more than 96 billion yuan; the financing in the first five months of 2026 was highly concentrated in the early stages of the angel round to the A+ round. The valuation of many companies has entered the range of 10 billion yuan, and the market valuation of individual head companies has reached tens of billions of yuan. There is an obvious time gap between the popularity of capital and the maturity of the industry.<sup id="fnref:1" role="doc-noteref"><a href="#fn:1" class="footnote" rel="footnote">1</a></sup><sup id="fnref:2" role="doc-noteref"><a href="#fn:2" class="footnote" rel="footnote">2</a></sup></p>

<p>Therefore, I judge that in the next one to three years, physical intelligence will undergo a large round of industry cleaning.</p>

<p>&quot;90% of companies will die&quot; is not accurate statistics, but my directional judgment of the intensity of competition. If all the teams with body labels today are included, only a small part of the companies that can eventually survive this round of adjustment independently may be counted.</p>

<p>The reason is not complicated. What capital can win is only research and development time, and mass production, delivery and after-sales service still need to be completed by the company itself; technical stories can attract attention, why customers buy, how much they are willing to pay, and how long it will take to return the cost, these are up to the product itself.</p>

<p>This round of elimination is not necessarily a bad thing: talents, capital, supply chain and customer resources will be reconcentrated, and the product direction and technical route will gradually be clarified. A large number of parallel and even contradictory attempts in the industry today will be screened by real delivery results. After this round of screening, it is possible for embedied intelligence to enter the growth period jointly driven by products and markets from the capital-driven exploration period.</p>

<p>If you must find a reference, I think it is somewhat similar to the early evolution of China&#x27;s new energy vehicle industry. The tightening of subsidies and fraudulent rectification have eliminated a number of enterprises that lack real product capabilities. The surviving companies continue to polish batteries, supply chains, vehicles and infrastructure, and finally ushered in an acceleration of sales and penetration rates around 2020.</p>

<p>This analogy is not rigorous. The maturity, cost structure and infrastructure of the demand for robots and cars are completely different. But it reminds me that the acceleration of industrial sales and penetration rates usually occurs after the first large-scale liquidation, not when the number of companies and the amount of financing are the most lively.</p>

<h2 id="具身之外我也在看各种-ai-硬件">In addition to the body, I am also looking at all kinds of AI hardware.</h2>

<p>The landing cycle of physical intelligence is long, so in the past two years, I have also continued to pay attention to a wider range of AI hardware: glasses, headphones, wearable devices, cameras, recording devices, home terminals, and various new products that cannot be accurately classified.</p>

<p>The product form has not been fixed yet, and there are many variables.</p>

<p>Should AI be deployed locally, in the cloud, or use end-to-cloud collaboration? Does the product only handle a single mode such as voice or image, or does it continuously understand sound, vision, position and environment? Should users operate through voice, buttons, screens and gestures, or let the device actively judge when to provide services?</p>

<p>Each choice will continue to affect power consumption, heat dissipation, battery life, privacy, cost and appearance. There is no standard answer for all products between them, so the forms will be diverse, among which a large number of attempts seem novel and the experience is not complete.</p>

<p>At present, a lot of AI hardware is still stuck at two extremes: one is just adding chat ability to traditional hardware, and the other is good at showing technology, but does not form a complete experience worth using repeatedly. There is also a natural contradiction between the uncertainty of AI output and the need for hardware to be stable, timely and predictable.</p>

<p>Products that users are willing to use for a long time still need to answer some simple questions: does it solve high-frequency and clear needs? Is it more trouble-free than taking out a mobile phone? Can the key tasks be completed steadily? How is data used and privacy protected? What do the local and the cloud bear?</p>

<p>These questions are not novel, but they determine whether an AI hardware can enter the user&#x27;s life from demonstration.</p>

<p>I hope to see such a product in the next two years: it does not necessarily have the most functions, and it does not need to write AI in every line of propaganda, but it can connect models, interactions, hardware and services into a whole in a clear scenario.</p>

<h2 id="从信息世界走向真实世界">From the information world to the real world</h2>

<p>Looking back on the past year, my attention has not completely shifted from software to hardware. The change is that I began to care more about how AI can get a continuous context, enter real tasks, and turn a model call into a deliverable result.</p>

<p>Body intelligence and AI hardware are the problems on this line. They let AI leave the chat box and face the real life of space, time, energy, cost, safety and people. The problem is more difficult, the cycle is longer, and the failure is more expensive, but once the whole system is stable, the threshold of value and competition is more solid.</p>

<p>Next, I will continue to record my observations on AI, products, physical intelligence and entrepreneurship here. It not only records what I believe, but also records how these judgments are overturned and rebuilt by facts.</p>
