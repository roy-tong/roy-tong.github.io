    <h1>From 90 clues to 63 core samples: How do I make a business map?</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">90 clues to 63 core samples: inclusion in the standard, single column not deleted, the number of refusal to calculate, two self-tests.</p>

    <p>The first caliber trap is in the unit: strip, or home. I sent it on August 31st.<a href="https://roy-tong.github.io/notes/dji-talent-whale-fall/">&quot;One frontier falls, all things are born&quot;</a>, talk about the entrepreneurship map of former employees of Xinjiang; the statistical unit of that article is &quot;bar&quot; from beginning to end, and one can be a company, brand or entrepreneurship project, so this article says 63 core samples, not 63 companies. The part that was not talked about will be supplemented in this article: how did 90 come, why was it cut to 63, which numbers I counted, and which I refused to count.</p>

<p>The draft of the map is a screenable wide table (<a href="https://roy-tong.github.io/dji-map.html">Online version</a>), 90 original clues. The first problem exposed before the statistics: the page title of the original HTML is written as &quot;72&quot;, the data array read by the program is 90, and the text description is also 90. Before releasing any total number, confirm what exactly is in the data.</p>

<h2 id="一线索从哪来三路渠道四轮扒完">I. Where do the clues come from: three channels, four rounds of picking</h2>

<p>The method of research and archiving is the intersection of three ways: science and technology and financial media reports (36 Krypton, Leifeng.com, Huhu, Interface, Cailian News Agency, etc.); investment institution channels (official accounts and official websites of Sequoia, Shunwei, Gaoling, Jingwei, Qiji, Linear, Wuyuan, Lanchi, Li Zexiang Fund&#x27;s official account and official website PR); character dimension (the whereabouts of successive executives, product lines and technical leaders of Xinjiang are tracked one by one). Before posting social media, I supplemented the two sources of the company&#x27;s official website and recruitment information. The clues are divided into four rounds, and each one has a source link and credibility annotation (confirmation/doubt).</p>

<p>The channel itself decides who the sample is biased towards, and the last section will be talked about.</p>

<p>Whether there is a threshold for length of service: No, the standard only depends on the nature of the relationship - formal employment, consultant, internship, and spin-off; the only quantitative processing of the whole table is the two months of Yushu. Do you want to believe the layered label of the original table: incomplete. R-Zero, Hangar and Fairmart in T5 (the weakest layer of evidence) found the supplementary materials of the named founding identity and entered the core sample as usual; there are also consultants and unnamed entries in T1-T4. Sort the labels, and audit the conclusions article by article. All 27 articles are kept in the table, and only the practice of &quot;mixing in one number&quot; is removed.</p>

<h2 id="三算出来的数和它们的编码规则">III. The calculated numbers and their coding rules</h2>

<p>Four statistical modules: track distribution (consumer hardware 17, robot/body 13, drone/low-altitude 8, software/AI 8, travel vehicle 7, manufacturing tools 5, sensor 3, image optics 2); background of the person in charge (30/63 with clear experience in Xinjiang department, team or business leader, 47.6%); founding time (27 out of 58 groupable groups were founded since 2024, 46.6%); the financing stage is divided into two calibers (36 of all 63 core samples are in seeds, angels or Pre-A, accounting for 57.1%; 23 out of 27 units established since 2024, accounting for 85.2%).</p>

<p>There is a coding rule behind each number, otherwise the same word will count two results. &quot; The rule of &quot;experience of the person in charge&quot;: only the person in charge of the department, team or business of Xinjiang is recognized; only the title of engineer, only the product manager, and only the person in charge of the subsequent employer is not included; the founder of Yingwei only records &quot;one of the creators of the chip team&quot; and the management responsibilities are unknown, and is not included. Therefore, it is written that &quot;at least 30 articles are clearly recorded&quot; - 30 is the lower limit of the number of items; Wang Mingyu corresponds to two of Ruyuan and Morpho, which is wrong to &quot;30 executives leave&quot;. The rules of time grouping: When the year of establishment is an interval or a double year, all candidate years fall into the same period before they are grouping; Songling/Kuqi crosses the segment, and one year is not forced to be selected.</p>

<p>The verification of the resume does not rely on the original form for self-certification. For example, the source of the supplementary investigation is: used by the founder of Fengjiang<a href="https://www.eeo.com.cn/2026/0610/910442.shtml">The prospectus report of the Economic Observer</a>Check; Colin Guinn found<a href="https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-114hhrg93879/pdf/CHRG-114hhrg93879.pdf">U.S. Congressional Hearing Records</a>; Jan Gasparic&#x27;s job use<a href="https://www.dji.com/media-center/announcements/skycatch-and-dji-announce-global-agreement-to-deliver-high-precision-custom-drones-for-komatsu">Xinjiang&#x27;s official announcement</a>Check; R-Zero is used<a href="https://www.forbes.com/sites/amyfeldman/2022/08/19/this-startup-wants-to-bring-disinfecting-uv-light-into-every-physical-space/">Forbes Character Report</a>; Fairmart uses<a href="https://www.fairmart.app/about-us">Official website team page</a>; Use at the end of this book<a href="https://pitchhub.36kr.com/project/1678503148975113">36 Krypton database</a>。</p>

<h2 id="四拒绝算的数融资估值和三种率">IV. The number of refusals to calculate: financing, valuation and three types of rates</h2>

<p>The financing amount and valuation have not been added throughout the whole process. The audit record lists seven types of problems: Morpho entry is mixed with Ruyuan&#x27;s financing, and Ruyuan is listed in another line, which will be repeated directly; Yushu&#x27;s IPO fundraising, Dnsys&#x27; crowdfunding, and Tashi&#x27;s cumulative financing are not similar transactions; Zhuo Yu contains an unfinished Pre-IPO plan; Miaodong and Chilong have conflicting calibers, and large numbers cannot be taken as facts; Ruochuang&#x27;s valuation field is filled in the cumulative financing amount; a large number of &quot;tens of millions&quot; and &quot;near 100 million yuan&quot; have no available fixed value; the caliber before and after investment, currency type and time point are different.</p>

<p>There are also three rates that refuse to be calculated: the turnover rate, the success rate of entrepreneurship, and the year-on-year growth rate of the &quot;entrepreneurship wave&quot;. Article 90 is a list of public information. The original research intended to focus on the collection of projects in recent years. In addition to the deviation of financing disclosure and the deviation of media coverage, these ratios are methodologically untenable. Only distribution and cases are written in the article: Tuozhu&#x27;s revenue exceeded 10 billion yuan in 2025 (according to the report confirmed by the interface to Tuozhu) and Zhenghao&#x27;s establishment in 2017, which are all single facts and do not include any total.</p>

<h2 id="五两道自检对照样本与敏感分项">V. Two self-inspection: control sample and sensitive sub-item</h2>

<p>First, change the sample to see if the conclusion is stable. In 63 articles, only 52 recalculations of the original table marked &quot;confirmation&quot; are retained: the proportion of drones accounts for 12.7% to 13.5%, the proportion of the person in charge is 47.6% to 50.0%, and the number of establishment since 2024 is 46.6% to 46.0%; the denominator of the seed to Pre-A should be written clearly. It is 36, 57.1% for all 63 core samples, and 29 to 55.8% of the 52 confirmed subsamples, and the above 85.2% group (the denominator is 27 established since 2024) is two calibers, each established. The four directions have not changed, and the main conclusion is not sensitive to the sample caliber.</p>

<p>The second is to find sub-items that are sensitive to single sources. Software/AI fell from 8 to 2 - this sub-item is supported by weak evidence, so &quot;software entrepreneurship outbreak&quot; is not written in the text. The total numbers are good-looking, but the sensitivity of sub-items is ugly. Two things should be seen together.</p>

<p>The public release version has also been reviewed for fact attribution and wording compliance, which does not affect the statistical caliber of this article.</p>

<h2 id="六这张地图看不见谁">VI. I can&#x27;t see anyone on this map.</h2>

<p>The founders who can be reported by the media and seen by social media have passed three rounds of screening: someone is willing to write, has financing to disclose, and lives to be seen. If you make products quietly, don&#x27;t get financing, and fail to leave the market, you can&#x27;t see the whole map. A considerable proportion of 90 projects have only one public report. The text repeatedly states the boundaries: this is the description and statistics of public information, not the industry census; the year of establishment is not equal to the year of separation; the financing stage is not equal to the maturity of the product; these numbers cannot be used to calculate the turnover rate or entrepreneurial success rate of any company.</p>

<h2 id="附复核材料">Attachment: Review materials</h2>

<p>Audit CSV item by item (90, each contains the reason for inclusion/single column, the code of the person in charge, the time grouping, the source link), the statistical results JSON (including SHA-256 of the source file), and the re-run script that only parses the literal amount. The original data of the three documents is not changed, and the original atlas and research archives are kept as they are, and the caliber can be rerun and mistakes can be picked.</p>

<p>The next time you see any map of &quot;XX start-up company N&quot;, ask three questions first: is the unit a company, a brand or a project; how many spin-offs, consultants and internships are mixed in N; and whether it has announced the numbers it refuses to calculate.</p>
