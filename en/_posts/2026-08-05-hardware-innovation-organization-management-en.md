    <h1>The organizations of Dajiang, Yingshi and Anke are almost opposite, but they are all established.</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">There is no single solution to hardware organization: under what conditions each road is established and what price is paid.</p>
    <div class="post-meta">

    <p>Xinjiang, Yingshi and Anke have all achieved consumer hardware in the global market, but their organization methods are almost opposite: Xinjiang relies on core technology and the founder to deeply participate in products and engineering; Yingshi defines products around the user&#x27;s complete shooting task chain, and the founder personally participates in the supply chain meeting; Anke uses the minimized business unit of the person in charge of the product line to do multi-category operation. The establishment of all three companies shows that there is no single solution to the hardware organization - it is not necessary to see who is right and who is wrong, but under what conditions each road is established and what price to pay.</p>

<p>This article sorts out my observations of domestic hardware innovation organizations into a clue: why hardware organizations can&#x27;t learn the Internet, how founders shape early organizations, how teams evolve with the product stage, and what working mechanisms are needed from insight to mass production.</p>

<h2 id="一硬件组织为什么不能照搬互联网">I. Why can&#x27;t hardware organizations copy the Internet?</h2>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>What determines the way of organization is how the product is delivered, how long it can be modified, how customer relationships are established, and how high the cost of failure is. First of all, correct a common misunderstanding: B-end software is not necessarily a sales system, and Internet companies may not only do free services - it is meaningless to label according to the product form, and it should be classified according to the delivery method and change cost.</th>
      <th>Product form</th>
      <th>Main delivery methods</th>
      <th>Change the rhythm and cost</th>

    </tr>

<p>The real dividing line is the cost of change. Software can rely on automated testing, continuous integration and small-batch release for quick trial and error - DORA regards &quot;keeping ready for deployment, automated testing, and small batch changes&quot; as the core capability of efficient software delivery. Hardware is irreversible or high-cost decisions everywhere: chip and sensor selection, structure and mold, material technology, certification, long-cycle materials, stock inventory, production line and test equipment. Once it is decided, the cost of changing later is in months and millions.</p>

<p>The most uncomfortable thing about intelligent hardware is that two sets of rhythms exist at the same time: hardware cycle from half a year to several years, key schemes are frozen in advance, and the cost of error correction is high; software and algorithms are iterated weekly, continuously updated after listing, and can reduce the risk. The team should retain the stage management of traditional hardware and the continuous iteration of the Internet team at the same time, and then unify the architecture by system engineering. NASA&#x27;s system engineering system positions the person in charge of the system as a key role in connecting user expectations, architecture, demand allocation, interface management and verification and confirmation - this mechanism is especially important for complex software and hardware products such as robots, cars and drones. Without, no one can move the two sets of software and hardware rhythms.</p>

<h2 id="二创始人如何塑造早期组织">II. How did the founder shape the early organization?</h2>

<p>In the first two or three years of the start-up company, the formal process and job system had not been established. The management mechanism actually adopted by the company came from the founder&#x27;s personal working style: what the founder believed, what problems would personally intervene, how to view speed, quality and cost, and whether to rely on data or personal judgment in disputes. The management style of the early company is often an extension of the founder&#x27;s working style in the team.</p>

<p>Analyzing the founder, the path that is more useful than the labels of &quot;strong&quot; and &quot;technical&quot; is: what are the core advantages, what organizational ability is grown, what blind spots are left behind, and what roles need to be made up. There are roughly four typical forms of domestic hardware companies.</p>

<p><strong>Technical invention type</strong>, starting from the underlying technology, have a strong judgment of the technical path and product performance, and believe more in Demo and engineering results. Xinjiang had obvious characteristics in the early days: Wang Tao has long emphasized product quality, engineering capabilities and investment in key technologies; with the expansion of the organization, he also began to reflect in public interviews that relying on products and personal judgment alone could not solve the problems of supervision, culture and organizational governance. The advantages of this kind of organization are that it has centralized technical judgment, quick attack, and can attract high-level engineering talents. The price is that it is easy to overestimate the user value of technology, insufficient user and market verification, and the founder himself becomes the bottleneck of all decisions. What needs to be made up as soon as possible is the strong Product Owner, the person in charge of user insight and the Program Owner - in addition to &quot;can it be done&quot;, the company also needs to answer &quot;is it worth doing&quot; and &quot;can it be delivered stably&quot;.</p>

<p><strong>Product mission type</strong>, have a clear obsession with what problems the company solves, the product form can be changed, and the core value is stable. The &quot;car and home&quot; proposed by Li Xiang, the founder of Ideal Automobile, directly expresses this product concept. The direction is easy to be consistent, the choice is continuous, and it is not biased by short-term competition, which is the strengths of this kind of organization. The risk is that the founder&#x27;s product narrative may replace the real needs of users. The team understands the vision but does not know what exactly this version will deliver. The corresponding mechanism is to disassemble the product assumptions of the mission component stage, clarify that each stage only verifies limited core problems, and write down the stop conditions.</p>

<p><strong>User scenario type</strong>, highly sensitive to specific usage scenarios, and define products from user workflows rather than parameter tables. Yingshi Liu Jingkang has publicly expressed that the focus of the product is on a broader real scene. It was evaluated by the supplier that he will personally participate in the supply chain meeting, and his product evolution has long revolved around the complete task chain of shooting, editing and sharing. Such organizations find problems early, products are easy to form a complete workflow, and iterate quickly; however, the personal experience of the founder is limited in representativeness, and high-frequency user feedback is easy to expand the scope, and the founder&#x27;s personal appearance will also become a bottleneck. What is needed is user stratification, refutation research, and precipitation of the founder&#x27;s scenario judgment into a reusable research method, instead of relying on his personal experience all the time.</p>

<p><strong>Management and organization type</strong>, emphasize business models and resource allocation, and be good at designing business units. Huawei&#x27;s IPD emphasizes that from opportunity to commercial realization, it requires product line management to cover research and development, production, delivery, service and life cycle, rather than equsing product lines with research and development departments; Anke openly adopts the minimized business unit led by the person in charge of the product line, and Yang Meng&#x27;s public review also shows that his focus has gradually shifted from solving product problems in person to strategy, organization, talent and capacity building. Such organizations are prone to large-scale operation and clear responsibilities; the risk is to judge innovative projects prematurely with the gross profit standard of the income of mature business and compress long-term technical investment. Therefore, it requires a forward-looking technical budget and a project assessment method that is different from that of mature business.</p>

<p>There is a principle that runs through the four types: the founder&#x27;s personal ability should not be the behavior template of all employees. It should be transformed into product principles, division of responsibilities, evaluation and data mechanisms, and the organizational ability that does not depend on the presence of the founder. In the internal innovation of mature companies, it may be the head of the business department or the business sponsor who plays a similar role - for example, internal new businesses such as the Daotong AI business department. The organizational style depends more on how the executives in charge define strategy, resources and product responsibility.</p>

<h2 id="三创新项目的最小核心团队">III. The smallest core team of the innovation project</h2>

<p>There Are Two Most Common Mistakes In The Innovation Team: There Are Too Few People, They Can Only Do Technical Demo, And The Product Cannot Be Verified; There Are Too Many People, And The Complete Department Is Built In Advance Before The Product Is Clear.</p>

<p>The minimum team should cover three types of risks: product value risk (whether users really need and are willing to change their behavior), technical and system risks (can performance, power consumption, size and cost be established at the same time), delivery and commercial risks (whether production can be produced, whether the selling channel is established). The three types of risks cannot be all on the R&amp;D personnel.</p>

<p>In my experience, the core team reference range of medium complexity intelligent hardware is 6-10 key roles - one person can play multiple roles at the same time, which is not equivalent to recruiting ten people at the beginning:</p>

<p>Four principles: allocate people according to risks, not according to departments - the core risk is to recruit algorithm experts first, and to make up for that direction in optics or supply chain; there can only be one Owner in a key field, and the participation of many people does not mean that many people are jointly responsible; the core team must be able to make prototypes directly, and cannot rely mainly on reporting, outsourcing management and meeting operation; the founder can also serve as multiple Owners in the early stage, but before entering engineering development, the three responsibilities of products, technology and projects must be separated. ## IV. The organization evolves with the product stage</p>

  <thead>
    <tr>

      <th>Main output</th>

  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>0—1</td>
      <td>Whether the product is established</td>

    <tr>
      <td>Multi-product and scale</td>

<p>The first stage proves that the product is worth doing and can be basically done: the founder directly participates in the product and technology, the product, design and technology make prototypes together, and the supply chain is mainly trial and verification. The most common mistake at this stage is to regard technology Demo as product verification, pursue complete functions from the beginning, have no stop conditions, and make all decisions to wait for the founder.</p>

<p>In the second stage, from &quot;making prototypes&quot; to &quot;making products that can be mass-produced&quot;, independent Product Owner, System Owner, full-time Program Owner, and module leaders such as structure, supply chain, test quality, must be added. There have been four changes in the management mode: from people-to-people to Owner responsibility; from verbal judgment to product and system baseline with clear version, architecture, indicators and verification methods; from rapid change to gradual freezing - freezing user value, product scope, system architecture, industrial design and production plan in turn; from outsourcing execution to management supply chain, the company itself masters the product definition, system architecture, key technology and quality standards, and the core judgment cannot be outsourced to ODM.</p>

<p>The third stage only needs to be entered when the company is facing mass production, next-generation products, software operation and multiple projects at the same time: the product line is responsible for the combination, revenue and life cycle, professional function precipitation design, system engineering, algorithm, quality standards and talents, and the platform team accumulates general modules, software platforms and testing capabilities. The role of the founder then regressed from judging the direction by himself to selecting and authorizing the key Owner, and then to deciding the strategy, organization and platform investment.</p>

<p>Scale is not the goal. I have seen too many teams draw organizational charts according to the third stage before the problems in the second stage are solved.</p>

<h2 id="五从洞察到量产的工作机制">V. Working mechanism from insight to mass production</h2>

<p><strong>Insight link</strong>, the accuracy of the prediction cannot be guaranteed, and a variety of evidence can only be used to reduce misjudgment: market and industry evidence, real user behavior, interview and scenario observation, prototype testing, price or pre-sale evidence, post-listing use and retention data. The user strategy should be specific to who, in what scenario, what problems are encountered, what alternatives are used now, and why it will be changed; the market strategy should be specific to the category, region, price band, channel and listing rhythm.</p>

<p><strong>Translation mechanism</strong>When the long-term connection between the product and research and development is not smooth, in addition to checking the communication, it is also necessary to check whether the System Owner is missing. A complete conversion path is to implement user needs, product requirements, system requirements, subsystem specifications, and verification standards layer by layer. Each key requirement clearly describes the source, scenario, indicator, priority, implementation Owner and verification method. The requirements for aesthetics are the same: the product manager should not only say &quot;advanced, concise and technological sense&quot;, but also translate the design intention according to the user and the environment, brand sentiment, three to five design principles, morphological ratio, CMF and interaction, and translate it all the way to the gap, segment difference, damping, color difference and other engineering standards and Golden Sample acceptance. Different products match different design capabilities: consumer electronics look at the form and daily experience, robots and cars look at the system layout and human-machine engineering, medical equipment look at regulations and misoperation protection, and industrial hardware look at reliability and maintainability.</p>

<p><strong>Project mechanism</strong>The project manager manages system integration, not just speeding the progress: integrating the main plan, key path, cross-departmental dependence, risk and change, stage review, decision-making upgrade. Complex intelligent hardware has five sets of rhythms at the same time: hardware, software, algorithm, supplier and listing. Without Program Owner, they will not be automatically aligned.</p>

<h2 id="六五种管理原型与阶段吸收">VI. Five management prototypes and stage absorption</h2>

<p>Combine the previous founder types and company practices into a reference table:</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Manage the prototype</th>
      <th>Representative practice</th>
      <th>Main advantages</th>
      <th>Issues that need to be alerted</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Xinjiang-style technology and engineering drive</td>
      <td>Core technology is concentrated, and the founder is deeply involved in products and projects.</td>
      <td>Technical breakthrough, performance and quality</td>
      <td>The founder&#x27;s bottleneck, the market and organizational ability are too late to make up for</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Shadow stone user scenario drive</td>
      <td>Define products around the complete task chain, and the product, market and supply chain are close.</td>
      <td>Scenario insight, workflow and rapid iteration</td>
      <td>High-frequency feedback brings about scope expansion, and platform capacity building is insufficient.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Huawei-style system and process drive</td>
      <td>IPD, system engineering, stage gate and cross-functional product line</td>
      <td>Complex system integration and stable delivery</td>
      <td>The process is too heavy, and it is not suitable for prematurely set up in the exploration project.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Xiaomi-style platform and ecological drive</td>
      <td>Category combination, platform capability and ecological coordination</td>
      <td>Multi-catery expansion and resource reuse</td>
      <td>The ability between categories is inconsistent, and the product experience is easy to disperse.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Anke&#x27;s product line management drive</td>
      <td>Minimize business units, product line managers and management responsibilities</td>
      <td>Clear responsibility and large-scale operation</td>
      <td>Use short-term income and gross profit to evaluate long-term innovation too early</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>The innovation team does not have to copy any company, but there is a priority of absorption: 0-1 stage learning from Xinjiang and Yingshi - strong core team, prototype, technology and scene verification, direct participation of the founder; in the mass production stage, supplement the Huawei-style mechanism - System Owner, Program Owner, stage gate, risk and change management; multi-product stage introduces Xiaomi and Anke structure - product line, platform, business unit and business responsibility. In reverse, it is one of the most common organizational accidents in hardware startups.</p>

<h2 id="七组织如何随产品成熟">VII. How can the organization mature with the product?</h2>

<p>The construction of hardware innovation organizations should follow the main problems: whether the early solution of the product is established, the mid-term solution of whether the product can be mass-produced, and the later solution of successful replication. The founder decides the company&#x27;s product taste, technical route and decision-making method at an early stage, but the enterprise cannot rely on the founder himself for a long time - maturity means transforming the founder&#x27;s advantages into product principles and technical standards, making up for blind spots with different types of Owners, from roles to separation of responsibilities, and from personal judgment to evidence, baseline and stage governance.</p>

<p>Back to the beginning, the three companies: Xinjiang, Yingshi and Anke have taken different paths, but they have repeatedly completed the same cycle - finding opportunities, defining products, completing interdisciplinary translation, stabilizing mass production, continuous operation, learning from the market, and then entering the next round of innovation. Whether a hardware company can continue to launch products depends on whether the organization can repeat this cycle, not on a certain technology or a best-selling product.</p>
