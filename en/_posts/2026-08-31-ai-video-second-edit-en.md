    <h1>The moat of AI video software is hidden in the second modification.</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">Generating a version of a work and continuously completing a work are two product abilities.</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-08-31T00:10:00+08:00">2026-08-31</time>
      <span>5 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/ai-video-second-edit/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>
      
        <span class="post-tags">
          <a href="/en/tags/#ai%E8%A7%86%E9%A2%91">#AI video</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E5%88%9B%E4%BD%9C%E5%B7%A5%E5%85%B7">#Creative tools</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E4%BA%A7%E5%93%81%E5%88%A4%E6%96%AD">#Product judgment</a>
        </span>
      
    </div>
  </header>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>Assign such a modification task to a video tool:</p>

<p>The preliminary draft has been confirmed. The characters remain unchanged, the lines remain unchanged, and the duration of the camera remains unchanged, only the background is changed.</p>

<p>This is a work situation for discussion, not a customer case I quoted. It is ordinary enough, but it can distinguish between the two types of products: one is good at generating a good-looking video; the other knows which content has been accepted and can continue to work on the premise of maintaining them.</p>

<p>I prefer to use the last task to judge the value of AI video software.</p>

<p>The first generation is very suitable for showing the ability. The second revision begins to check whether the software understands the &quot;project&quot;.</p>

<h2 id="只改这里才是困难的要求">&quot;Only change here&quot; is the difficult requirement.</h2>

<p>People will constantly reduce the uncertainty in the process of creation.</p>

<p>At first, there may not even be a direction. After several rounds of discussion, the character was determined, the brand tone was determined, and the camera rhythm was also determined. Each subsequent revision was based on the previous decision.</p>

<p>If the tool resubjects the whole paragraph to the model every round, it may improve the background, but at the same time change the expression, action and commodity details. The creator has to check the part that has passed again. It takes a short time to generate itself, but the time it takes to recheck and coordinate is not necessarily short.</p>

<p>There is a cost that is easy to ignore here: the problem of how many closed ones are reopened every time they are modified.</p>

<p>Therefore, &quot;support partial modification&quot; should not only be understood as an area that can be circled on the interface. It also includes parts of time, parts of object, and parts of responsibility.</p>

<h2 id="模型的进步会改变边界不会取消项目">The progress of the model will change the boundaries and will not cancel the project.</h2>

<p>Reference diagrams, consistency control and editing capabilities are all developing. Runway&#x27;s Gen-4 Image References document is very specific about the workflow of character and scene reference. It processes images and can be used to prepare video materials. It cannot directly prove that the whole video can also maintain the same constraints. The manufacturer&#x27;s tutorial also needs specific task tests to test.<a href="https://help.runwayml.com/hc/en-us/articles/40042718905875-Creating-with-Gen-4-Image-References">Runway Document</a></p>

<p>If the model can accurately maintain all specified conditions in the future, some cumbersome remedial steps today can disappear. Software should not rely on the model to temporarily do bad things to maintain complexity.</p>

<p>But the project still needs to remember where the conditions come from.</p>

<p>Can the character confirmed this time be used in another film next time? Why can&#x27;t the version rejected by the customer appear again? After the colleague changed the subtitles, who can see which shots need to be rechecked? If another model is generated, which materials and decisions can continue to be used?</p>

<p>The model is good at performing a certain modification, and the software is responsible for remembering conditions and assuming long-term responsibilities, which are not contradictory.</p>

<h2 id="一个-mp4-装不下完整的创作过程">An MP4 can&#x27;t hold the complete creative process.</h2>

<p>The final video is a deliverable, but not all production assets.</p>

<p>Material identity, lens dependence, timeline, subtitles, audio track, version, modification requirements and approval records determine how the team can deal with it next time. If these things are only scattered in the chat, change the person, change the conversation, it is easy to start the work from the explanation background.</p>

<p>OpenTimelineIO provides a very specific engineering reference: it describes clip information such as fragments, tracks, time, tags and metadata, and the audio and video itself is still associated through external references. It does not contract the entire production system, but it shows that there is an actual difference between &quot;saving a video file&quot; and &quot;saving a project that can continue to work&quot;.<a href="https://opentimelineio.readthedocs.io/en/latest/index.html">OpenTimelineIO document</a></p>

<p>AI creation software can have its own project structure, and it can also be connected with existing formats. The important thing is that the previous decisions made by the creators can remain, and the next tools can use them.</p>

<p>This also affects the role of Agent.</p>

<p>Agent can organize production steps, select tools, and check output according to requirements, but if the tool only returns a video and there is no structure to continue the operation, the next round may still return to &quot;say the requirements again&quot;. A good tool interface should allow Agent to specify objects, read status, submit modifications and check differences, not just receive a prompt.</p>

<h2 id="价值积累在批准过的东西里">The value is accumulated in what has been approved.</h2>

<p>There is a class of assets in the creation software that is difficult to see from the single generation: things that have been confirmed to be used.</p>

<p>It may be a set of commodity materials, a character&#x27;s image constraints, an accepted subtitle style, or the rhythm and modification habits confirmed by the customer.</p>

<p>Their value comes from reducing the uncertainty of the next time. When changing tools, if you need to reconfirm all the conditions, even if the new tool is cheaper, the migration may not be worth it.</p>

<p>I think this is closer to the long-term barrier of professional software than &quot;how many models are connected&quot;. However, the accumulation must make it possible for users to migrate and check. Locking the material to make the exit cost and really reducing repetitive labor are two completely different product options.</p>

<p>The source and conditions of use should also be included in the project. C2PA provides the relevant mechanism of content source certificate, but the certificate will not automatically judge the authenticity of the work, let alone automatically grant the copyright of the material. The software still needs to retain the actual authorization and scope of application, and cannot be judged with a &quot;trustworthy&quot; icon.<a href="https://spec.c2pa.org/specifications/specifications/2.2/explainer/Explainer.html">C2PA Description</a></p>

<h2 id="把连续修改做成评测">Make continuous modifications into evaluations</h2>

<p>I will design a set of continuous modification tasks for the product.</p>

<p>Get the first draft of the same quality, and then modify the background, lines, frame and rhythm in turn. Each round is clear about what content is allowed to change and what must be kept, and finally hand over to the review and inspection results of those who do not know the tool route.</p>

<p>The key points of the record include: how long it took to complete the modification, how many conditions that have been passed were destroyed, how much manual return required, and whether the final delivery was up to standard. Comparison can include direct regeneration, traditional editing and a combination of the two.</p>

<p>In this way, there is a chance to distinguish: whether the tool only outputs results faster or does reduce the workload of the whole project.</p>

<p>The second project should also be observed. Will the characters and brand assets saved for the first time really save work next time? If you still need to explain from the beginning every time, the so-called &quot;project memory&quot; may be just another name for the history record.</p>

<h2 id="哪些用户未必需要这一套">Which users may not need this set?</h2>

<p>One-time entertainment content, exploration ideas or simple material generation may only need to be cheap enough, fast enough and surprising enough. Impulsing complex project management on these users will increase the burden.</p>

<p>Professional work is not the more functions the better. For things that can be solved by determinism editing, there is no need to call the generation model every time. The conditions that can be automatically maintained should not allow users to lock them manually.</p>

<p>My judgment applies to another type of task: the work should be repeatedly confirmed, multi-person collaboration, and delivered as clearly required. In these tasks, the more important the next revision is, the more valuable the project structure is.</p>

<p>When watching the next AI video software, I will read one less round of dazzling first draft and give it one more revision requirement:</p>

<p>Everything else will be kept, but only this part will be changed.</p>
