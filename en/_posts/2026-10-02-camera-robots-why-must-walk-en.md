    <h2 id="突然变热一边创纪录一边官宣不存在的产品">Sudden heat: while setting a record, the official announcement of non-existent products</h2>

<p>On September 6, 2026, Beni, an all-terrain photography robot on Kickstarter, ended crowdfunding: $4,584,657, 7,293 supporters, and an achievement rate of 9,169% (Kicktraq statistics, data source is Kickstarter public API). Two months ago, Yingshi officially announced a photography robot called Cameraman on the 11th anniversary. The official caliber is &quot;not a single hardware product to be released&quot; and &quot;there is no independent R&amp;D investment, team size, release node and product price&quot; (Yingshi&#x27;s response to Jiupai News, 2026-07). On the one hand, there is a new company whose crowdfunding amount is called the number one in the category by the media, and on the other hand, there is a listed company that clearly says &quot;I don&#x27;t have a product&quot; - this track lives in both states at the same time.</p>

<p>The caliber of the industry is also very neat. In the 2025 annual report, IDC wrote the final situation as: the handheld image terminal should become the &quot;base and brain of the intelligent image robot terminal&quot;, and grow from a passive image recording terminal to a &quot;super intelligent body that actively generates creative content&quot; (original press release of IDC official website). The wording of the start-up company is closely related to it: Miaodong said that the shooting equipment is divided according to the perspective, the drone controls the sky, the mobile phone controls the carry-on, and the sports camera controls the movement, &quot;only one fills the &#x27;first perspective close to the ground&#x27;&quot;; Yingshi Liu Jingkang said that the ultimate direction of the camera is a photography robot with fully autonomous shooting ability and allows users to be completely freed from operation; Yueqian Innovation Zeng Cheng said that indoor and desktop scenes &quot;still lack a robot that can really hold the mirror&quot; (Geek Park, 2026-09-22). There are also big differences under the consensus: in terms of form, two-wheeled feet, self-balancing, tricycles, four-leged, and robotic arms have their own things; in terms of autonomy, Yingshi publicly admits that the Luna gimbal camera on sale &quot;still needs the user to manually control the camera position&quot;, while Beni claims to follow fully automatically. This disagreement is a clue in itself.</p>

<h2 id="先别急着把它当一个品类">Don&#x27;t rush to treat it as a category.</h2>

<p>I did a round of track scanning in late September 2026: 89 product pools, 45 company and financing records, and the ground movement patterns were retrieved one by one in the public information that I could verify. The first discovery: The &quot;photographic robot category&quot; I want to write about does not exist - there are at least four types of products called by this name, and users buy different products in each category:</p>

<p>The pricing logic of the four categories is different from that of its competitors: Snoppa&#x27;s opponent is film and television rental equipment, and Beni&#x27;s opponent is a sports camera plus smart glasses. Putting 15 companies in a competitive list is equivalent to acquiescing that they are competing for the same market; they are actually answering four different questions. The main axis of this article is in the first category: only it takes &quot;movement&quot; itself as a selling point, and therefore bears the heaviest obligation to prove - why does the camera have to go?</p>

<h2 id="为什么相机一定要会走">Why does the camera have to go?</h2>

<p>Grading according to photography autonomy - L0 manual, L1 automatic tracking, L2 packaging mirror skills, L3 position recommendation, L4 autonomous operator, L5 independent director - what is really selling today is L1 and L2: Pivo&#x27;s rotating base, drone&#x27;s Follow/Orbit, Beni&#x27;s follow; L3-L4 is talked in the press conference and financing materials. To judge which level a company stands at, don&#x27;t look at demo, look<strong>The proportion of non-intervention conversation</strong>——The proportion of users who did not touch the equipment in the whole process and were willing to release it in the end. No company has disclosed this figure in the public information I checked.</p>

<p>There are no products that meet the three conditions for the purchase in the sample covered in this article at the same time: I must appear in person and can&#x27;t take the camera at the same time; fixed camera position or simply tracking can&#x27;t get the content I want; I repeat this frequently to save the cost of covering the equipment price. If one is missing, the product can&#x27;t be sold. Beni at least clearly covers the first article. The user himself needs to appear in the camera, and he can&#x27;t take the camera at the same time. Article 2, whether the mobile camera really produces content that cannot be obtained by fixed camera, is still being verified; Article 3 lacks evidence. The success of crowdfunding proves the purchase interest, which cannot prove whether the long-term use frequency and equipment price are worth it. The three conditions of the scene of &quot;full-body movement, lateral arc, flat ground, and site can be standardized&quot; such as dance studio and Pilates studio are theoretically met, and I haven&#x27;t found a product specialized in it yet.</p>

<p>The unique value of mobile chassis is parallas, occlusion removal, height change, and a sense of space for real advancement; the cost to be paid is noise, safety, endurance and failure rate. In this account, no mobile company covered by this article has been calculated with data.</p>

<h2 id="veo-反例跟随感不一定要求机位移动">Veo counterexample: The sense of following does not necessarily require the movement of the camera</h2>

<p>The most weighty judgment of this track now comes from a company that doesn&#x27;t make robots. Veo (Denmark) fixed panoramic camera to shoot the entire stadium, and then AI generates virtual follow-up shots; in fiscal year 2025, the group&#x27;s revenue is DKK 593.5 billion, gross margin of 62.9%, and the first annual profit (Annual Report of the Danish Registry of Industry and Commerce, CVR 37240834). The 180° panorama and AI reconstruction of the dual 4K lens make the &quot;change of camera position&quot; become a cropping. At least in standardized and visual-coverable sports scenarios such as the court, Veo has proved that the &quot;sense of following&quot; does not necessarily require the real-time movement of the physical machine - every step forward in this route weakens the necessity of moving chassis in similar scenarios.</p>

<p>The unit economy stands on the same side. The $385 million given by the research institution to the &quot;AI motion tracking camera market&quot; is pure hardware shipments (GlobalInfoResearch, 2026-01), excluding subscriptions - and subscriptions are the profit pool: the revenue of Veo alone is equivalent to about 23% of the global market of this caliber. Among the batch of companies I checked, the only one that can prove that &quot;I make money by this category&quot; with the audit financial report is a fixed position plus subscription, which has nothing to do with mobile robots.</p>

<h2 id="工程现实beni-与一份失败清单">Engineering reality: Beni and a list of failures</h2>

<p>Beni is one of the most fully verified mobile photography robot cases by public crowdfunding at present, and both sides are worth watching. Positive: 10 minutes to meet the standard, 7,293 supporters, per capita pledge $629 (Kicktraq); MKBHD in &quot;I Said Yes to Every Email for a Month! (Again)&quot; has evaluated more than ten new products in the collection, and the only 10/10 gave it (<a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mfmdXPT7nAM">The original video</a>). On the other hand, the actual measurement of overseas media (The Verge, etc.): the camera is about 20 cm above the ground, and the person is basically a back-up shot, and the height cannot be adjusted like the follower; the automatic follow-up needs to be turned on manually every time; the follow-up process will hit the corner and wall, and high-speed scenes such as skateboarding are more frequent. The evaluation quoted by Xiaoxiang Morning News is more blunt: the obstacle avoidance system is &quot;not enough to prevent it from hitting the corner, the wall, and even hitting my foot when trying to keep up with my scooter&quot;, and directly asks - is this a new species created by AI and robot iteration, or &quot;a new marketing concept deliberately packaged by the industry to break through the bottleneck after the growth of traditional image hardware has peaked&quot;?</p>

<p>Roll Cam AI is an extreme sample of marketing ahead of the project: claiming to be &quot;World&#x27;s First Robotic Ai Camera Man&quot;, the industry media CineD&#x27;s report title is &quot;How to insult all cameramers on earth with a mid-life crisis tripod&quot;. slashCAM pointed out that the company had no product record before; Kickstarter crowdfunding was launched in 2025, and 440 supporters pledged about $138,000, and the official website now jumps to the blank page.</p>

<p>Looking around again, the list of failures is not short: 10-Chairs crowdfunding has only completed 17%; German Sporttotal AG (free camera delivery, relying on platform cashing) filed for bankruptcy on 2025-03-12, and 135 Bavaria clubs were affected; France MOVE&#x27;N SEE (PIXEM follow-up camera) was ruled judicial liquidation by the court on September 15, 2026; Snap Pixy was discontinued; Skydio withdrew from the consumer level. These cases at least show that the tracking algorithm is not enough to ensure the establishment of the product; hardware cost, channel, subscription mode, frequency of use, security and strategic choice may be decisive factors.</p>

<p>Interestingly, there is an obvious outflow of Dajiang talents in this batch of entrepreneurial teams, but this shows that the engineering capabilities have begun to migrate to new forms, which cannot prove the establishment of the product route (see Appendix II for the list and two reverse facts).</p>

<h2 id="接下来半年可获得的验证信号">The next half year: available verification signals</h2>

<ul>
  <li>To evaluate a photography robot, look at three things: the height of the camera (whether it can only shoot on the back), noise (none of the manufacturers I checked has published the decibel value), and the non-intervention session ratio. Don&#x27;t watch the tracking demonstration video - the rehearsed demonstration and random scenes are two species.</li>
  <li>Judge whether the product is established or not, and do a control experiment: in the same scene, fixed camera position, fixed PTZ, mobile camera position, shoot one version each, and test which version the user is willing to release in the end. It measures the release behavior, not the tracking accuracy rate.</li>
  <li>Focus on the delivery, don&#x27;t focus on the press conference. Beni will start shipping from October 2026. The most informative signals in the next six months do not need to wait for the financial report: the actual delivery and postponement, the real videos taken by the first batch of users, the resale volume of second-hand platforms, and the update frequency of continuous use of content. The success of crowdfunding only proves that the category can be imagined, and delivery and retention prove whether the product is established. The second signal is Yuejiang ¥7,499 and Qiyuan ¥19,999. These two price anchors - they will force all special photography robots to answer: How are you more expensive than a robot dog that can take pictures by the way?</li>
</ul>

<h2 id="附录一玩家地图15-家按四类分组">Appendix I: Player Map (15, grouped by four categories)</h2>

<p>There are four types of identities: new (Beni, XbotGo), by-products of listed companies (Yuejiang, Qiyuan, No. 9), a drawing (Zixiafeng, Shentingji), and has fallen (Roll Cam AI, 10-Chairs); the price ranges from $799 to $5,579, and there is a fault between the consumer level and the professional level.</p>

<h2 id="附录二大疆人才外溢名单与两个反向事实">Appendix II: Talent Spill in Xinjiang - List and Two Reverse Facts</h2>

<p>I have counted 63 verifiable samples of Xinjiang-related entrepreneurship in &quot;One Frontier Falls, All Things Are Born&quot;, including 13 robots and 17 consumer hardware. Most of the people who left Xinjiang have no longer made drones. The battery, perception and control are recombined in other products. Photography robots are one of the latest landing points.</p>

<ul>
  <li>Miaodong Technology Trio (Investment Industry, Zhidongsi): CTO Yang Shuo, a former Phantom product manager, went to CMU Robotics College to study for a doctorate, and then became the core architect of Tesla Optimus, studied under Li Zexiang and Wang Tao&#x27;s younger brother; CEO Gao Jianrong, assistant general manager and head of supply chain of Xinjiang; Li Haonan, former head of Dajiang Flight System Technology. The investors of Miaodong are Meituan, Sequoia and Jingwei, and they are also the three major external shareholders (investment circle) of Yushu Technology.</li>
  <li>FarseerTech&#x27;s RocXZoom &quot;robot ultra-telephoto camera&quot;, former DJI engineer team, crowdfunding raised $250,000 (PetaPixel, 2026-08-31).</li>
</ul>

<p>Two reverse facts are equally important: DJI itself does not have ground mobile photography robot products; Yingshi officially denied the rumor that &quot;the Cameraman team comes from DJI, Covos and Hormizon, with a size of more than 100 people&quot;. The financing caliber should also be careful - the statement that &quot;Miaodong has financed 300 million US dollars&quot; is widely circulated, and Yang Shuo himself refuted the rumor: &quot;No. We don&#x27;t need to use such numbers to define the company. Only the 2025-04 Angel Wheel tens of millions of dollars and the 2025-10 follow-up round can be verified.</p>
