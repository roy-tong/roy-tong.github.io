    <h1>How to form a verifiable demand judgment from 200,000 feedbacks</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">A set of practical processes from decision-making issues, social media sampling, evidence collation to product verification</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-08-04T20:00:00+08:00">2026-08-04</time>
      <span>24 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/scene-user-demand-evidence-research/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>
      
        <span class="post-tags">
          <a href="/en/tags/#%E7%94%A8%E6%88%B7%E7%A0%94%E7%A9%B6">#User Research</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E4%BA%A7%E5%93%81%E6%96%B9%E6%B3%95">#Product method</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E6%95%B0%E6%8D%AE%E6%8C%96%E6%8E%98">#Data mining</a><a href="/en/tags/#%E7%A4%BE%E5%AA%92%E8%B0%83%E7%A0%94">#Social media research</a><a href="/en/tags/#ai">#AI</a>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>In the past period of time, I have done three large-scale user studies: one private computing power and local AI equipment, one AI glasses, one spatial display, 3D and virtual world. Among them, 200,732 original records were collected in the round of AI glasses. After date, text integrity, relevance and de-weight screening, only 45,630 entered the main sample - more than three-quarters of the materials were blocked by their own rules at the entrance. In the other round, 211,088 feedbacks of &quot;meeting the preliminary conditions&quot; were retained, and the single platform still accounted for 90.21%.</p>

<p>The research object, platform and industry language are very different, but the implementation process repeatedly collides with the same problem: the amount of data is growing rapidly, and the evidence that can support the product decision is still insufficient.</p>

<p>This group of experiences prompted me to reorganize the research process into SURE: Structured User Research with Evidence. Its core analysis unit is:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>User Role × Scene and Trigger × Task and Result × Current Alternative × Friction and Cost × Consequence × Evidence Level</p>
</blockquote>

<p>This update splits the method into two external versions:</p>

<ul>
  <li>This article is aimed at researchers, product managers and entrepreneurs, explaining how to do each step;</li>
  <li><a href="https://github.com/roy-tong/user-demand-research">User Demand Research Agent Skill</a>For Agent, it provides fixed directories, templates, execution protocols and audit CLIs without third-party dependencies.</li>
</ul>

<p>The two share the same set of evidence models: the human version is responsible for understanding and judgment, and the Agent version is responsible for stable execution and handover.</p>

<p>The following is a complete process with a whole case:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>A company is judging whether it is worth making a remote guidance prototype of AI glasses for on-site maintenance engineers.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>The example records and figures in the text are only used to illustrate the method and do not represent the real industry conclusions.</p>

<h2 id="先看最终产物">Let&#x27;s look at the final product first.</h2>

<p>At the end of a complete study, the product team needs to get a set of documents that can be checked back:</p>

<div class="language-text highlighter-rouge"><div class="highlight"><pre class="highlight"><code>repair-guidance-study/
├── study.json                         # 决策问题、假设、证伪条件和质量门槛
├── 01-sources/source-plan.csv         # 来源路线、证据角色、目标和已知偏差
├── 02-data/
│   ├── raw/                           # 原始或授权导入材料
│   ├── views/                         # 配平后的分析视图
│   └── evidence.jsonl                 # 可被结论引用的证据记录
├── 03-codebook/
│   ├── codebook.csv                   # 分类定义和版本
│   └── gold-set.jsonl                 # 人工校准样本
├── 04-findings/demand-judgments.json  # 需求判断、反证和下一项验证
└── 05-audit/                          # 质量检查结果
</code></pre></div></div>

<p>The most important product is a demand judgment that can be reviewed. For example:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>For on-site maintenance engineers who need to operate the equipment with both hands, it is a clear task to get accurate guidance in the complex disassembly steps. At present, mobile phones, paper manuals or remote voice guidance are commonly used. Operation interruption and communication round-trip will extend the repair time. Existing materials have supported the problem and alternative evidence; whether to accept AI glasses, who is willing to pay, and whether they can continue to use them remains to be verified.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>This judgment clearly describes the content that has been observed, how far the evidence can go, what is missing in the next step, and there is no rush to announce the product opportunity.</p>

<h2 id="第一步把研究题目改成一个产品决定">The first step: change the research topic to a product decision</h2>

<p>The following questions cannot guide sampling:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Research the user needs of AI glasses in the maintenance industry.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>The scope of research will continue to expand, and any material can be considered relevant. In the end, it is often a report that is rich in content but cannot decide the next step.</p>

<p>The executable research contract needs to be clearly written in five contents:</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Project</th>
      <th>How to write in the repair case</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Decision-making issues</td>
      <td>Decide whether to make an on-site maintenance remote guidance prototype before September 30.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Current options</td>
      <td>Make AI glasses prototypes; continue to optimize the mobile phone scheme; suspend this direction</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Target role</td>
      <td>Front-line maintenance engineers, remote experts, procurement managers, IT and security auditors</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Minimum evidence</td>
      <td>Tasks and alternatives, program acceptance, payment or deployment behavior, rejection and counterexample</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>It is forbidden to infer</td>
      <td>There is no need to estimate the market ratio of convenient samples, and do not write the number of comments as the number of users.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>Each core hypothesis is also equipped with an observable falsifying condition.</p>

<p>Hypothesis:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>Maintenance engineers need to continuously get visual guidance when their hands are occupied.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Conditions for falsifying:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>If the target task can be completed by mobile phone or voice in 30 seconds, and there is no clear acceptance of the hand-free scheme in the concept test, the prototype input of the current form will be stopped.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>After the falsification conditions are written in advance, the data will have a chance to change the decision. Otherwise, research can easily become a supplement to existing ideas.</p>

<h2 id="第二步按材料能证明什么设计来源">Step 2: Design the source according to &quot;What can the material prove&quot;</h2>

<p>Searching for AI glasses, smart glasses or AR glasses will mainly find product users, enthusiasts, evaluators and published discussions. They are suitable for the experience of studying existing programs, but they can&#x27;t see a large number of tasks that do not use product words.</p>

<p>Maintenance engineers may only say:</p>

<ul>
  <li>When disassembling the machine, you need to stop and check the manual repeatedly;</li>
  <li>Remote experts can&#x27;t see where they are operating;</li>
  <li>It is difficult to use the mobile phone when wearing gloves;</li>
  <li>Some factories prohibit the filming or external transmission of data;</li>
  <li>At present, the method of adding voice and pictures is enough.</li>
</ul>

<p>Therefore, the source plan should cover five types of evidence roles.</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>The role of evidence</th>
      <th>Materials that can be found in repair cases</th>
      <th>Main questions answered</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Direct program feedback</td>
      <td>AI glasses, remote collaboration equipment, enterprise deployment feedback</td>
      <td>Where is the plan available, where is the failure, and what are the conditions for acceptance?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Open scene</td>
      <td>Maintenance forum, work order review, training community, operation procedure discussion</td>
      <td>At what time do users complete what tasks?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Substitution and rejection</td>
      <td>Mobile phone video, tablet, paper manual, voice guidance, return and refusal to deploy</td>
      <td>Is the existing method sufficient? Why not change it?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Post-purchase and support</td>
      <td>Deployment work orders, equipment failures, battery life, network, security and maintenance records</td>
      <td>Whether it can run stably after use, and where is the cost?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Control sample</td>
      <td>Teams that are satisfied with mobile phones or voice solutions, tasks without remote experts</td>
      <td>Which scenarios lack problems, and which markets do not need to be entered?</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>The number of sources is not equal to the diversity of sources. Ten science and technology forums may still gather similar people; comments on five platforms may also come from the same group of product enthusiasts.</p>

<p>The research plan needs to indicate for each route: which evidence role it bears, the number of target records, the upper limit of the single route, the access status and the known deviation.</p>

<p>Once the replacement and rejection samples are empty, continuing to add product community comments cannot fix the gap. At this time, the search entrance should be changed, or it should be clearly written that the current conclusion lacks reversal evidence.</p>

<h2 id="第三步先试采集再决定是否扩量">Step 3: Try to collect first, and then decide whether to expand the quantity.</h2>

<p>Before the formal collection, a small batch of materials should be obtained from each route. The goal of trial collection is to judge the quality of the route, and the quantity is determined according to the platform and material form.</p>

<p>Each route records at least the following results:</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Target</th>
      <th>What does it help to judge?</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>The number of records reached</td>
      <td>The general scale of the route</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>The completeness rate of the text</td>
      <td>Can the context and the original words be kept?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Efficient after screening</td>
      <td>Is this route worth continuing to invest?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>The number of unique themes or products</td>
      <td>Is it dominated by a hot discussion?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Repetition rate</td>
      <td>Are there too many reposts, transfers and the same replies?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>E3–E5 output</td>
      <td>Can you find evidence of program acceptance, payment and continuous use?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Rejection and comparison output</td>
      <td>Can you see the situation that the product is not valid?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Time concentration</td>
      <td>Whether it is affected by a release, failure or controversy</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Access restrictions</td>
      <td>Do you encounter login, verification code, 403, 429 or platform restrictions?</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>Suppose a product release review route has 500 records, but 70% of them come from the same release week, and there are almost no returns, deployments and long-term use materials. This route is suitable for observing the release response, but not for long-term demand judgment.</p>

<p>When a route cannot provide evidence of its assignment, the route should be redesigned. Researchers need to keep failed and blocked sources to avoid later people understanding data blanks as &quot;no problems&quot;.</p>

<h2 id="平台数据怎么接先审开源代码再决定能不能抓">How to receive platform data: review the open source code first, and then decide whether to catch it.</h2>

<p>This system adopts a set of open source connectors that can be checked, modified and replaced. The connector is responsible for sending platform data into a unified format, and the research method is responsible for sampling, judgment and auditing.</p>

<p>But &quot;there is code on GitHub&quot; and &quot;this route can be used for research&quot; are two things. To choose an open source project, you need to pass four consecutive checks:</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Examine</th>
      <th>Questions to be answered</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Code</td>
      <td>Is there a clear license, can it be fixed to a specific commit, and depends on whether it can be audited?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Visit</td>
      <td>Actually use official API, public data set, or simulated login, internal interface and web page crawling</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Data</td>
      <td>Can this study save, refresh, delete and use the returned data?</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Research</td>
      <td>Whether the output retains key levels such as discussion strings, dialogues, videos, commodity variants or projects</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>The MIT license only answers the first question. It allows how to use the code, does not automatically grant access to the platform, nor does it automatically grant the right to reuse comments and user content.</p>

<p>As of August 27, 2026, I have compiled the review results into the following table:</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Terrace</th>
      <th>Reusable GitHub projects</th>
      <th>Current conclusion</th>
      <th>What is it suitable for?</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>

      <td><a href="https://github.com/praw-dev/praw">PRAW</a></td>

    </tr>
    <tr>

<p>Some other project codes themselves are not bad, but they cannot enter the default collection link:</p>

<ul>
  <li>X&#x27;s<a href="https://github.com/JustAnotherArchivist/snscrape">snscrape</a>And<a href="https://github.com/d60/twikit">twikit</a>Use unofficial crawling or internal interface, and X&#x27;s current rules require official API;</li>
  <li>YouTube&#x27;s<a href="https://github.com/egbertbouman/youtube-comment-downloader">youtube-comment-downloader</a>And<a href="https://github.com/jdepoix/youtube-transcript-api">youtube-transcript-api</a>All bypass the Data API, and YouTube&#x27;s current policy prohibits direct or indirect access to captured data;</li>
  <li>JD.com&#x27;s<a href="https://github.com/2274900/JD_comment_spider">JD_comment_spider</a>It has not been updated for many years, and still relies on the old direct connection port;</li>
  <li>Taobao&#x27;s<a href="https://github.com/l010306/taobao-review-playwright">taobao-review-playwright</a>You need to scan the code to log in, save the login state and simulate user operation;</li>
  <li>Kickstarter&#x27;s<a href="https://github.com/ImWhiteRabbit/KSInsights">KSInsights</a>The structured data is retained, but the latest snapshot of the warehouse is stuck in 2025, and the data comes from web crawlers.</li>
</ul>

<p>These projects are kept in the list of &quot;reviewed, not enabled&quot;, so that the next Agent can directly see the previous judgment and reason when searching GitHub.</p>

<h3 id="社媒平台连接器相同样本结构不同">Social media platform: the connector is the same, but the sample structure is different</h3>

<p>The three social media platforms still share a research process:</p>

<div class="language-text highlighter-rouge"><div class="highlight"><pre class="highlight"><code>确定证据角色
→ 选择通过审查的开源连接器
→ 固定代码版本和访问依据
→ 建立平台检索路线
→ 小批量试采集
→ 保存采集 manifest 与平台层级
→ 整理成 SURE 证据记录
→ 检查集中度
→ 用独立来源验证
</code></pre></div></div>

<p>Each collection should generate a manifest, record the connector, fixed commit, license, access basis, data rights, retrieval, request/touch/write quantity, quota, warning and stop reason. API keys, cookies and account information cannot be written into manifest.</p>

<h4 id="reddit把社区和讨论串当成样本结构">Reddit: Take the community and discussion chain as a sample structure</h4>

<p>Using PRAW does not mean that you can take unlimited numbers. The research still needs to confirm the API use, rate, retention and deletion rules first. Each route is recorded at least:</p>

<div class="language-text highlighter-rouge"><div class="highlight"><pre class="highlight"><code>subreddit × 检索主题 × 原始检索式 × 排序 × 时间范围 × 帖子/评论 × 历史/监听
</code></pre></div></div>

<p>In August 2026, Reddit announced the adjustment plan for the public Data API: existing API applications must be registered by September 30, 2026, and the new self-service application portal has been closed and changed to an approval system. The long-term direction is to migrate to the developer platform. The meaning for the research system is that research that has been approved for access can continue to use PRAW, but for research that has not been approved for credentials should record Reddit as an access gap instead of replacing it with a grabbing tool.</p>

<p>The same problem should be observed separately.<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">relevance</code>、<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">new</code>、<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">top</code>Wait for the view. Hot sorting is suitable for finding important discussions, and time sorting is closer to the recent baseline. Ten subreddit still belong to the same source family, and the fifty comments in the same discussion string are not confirmed fifty times independently.</p>

<h4 id="x把转发和事件扩散从需求中拆开">X: Remove forwarding and event diffusion from demand</h4>

<p>Tweepy is responsible for calling the official interface, and the research file should be kept:</p>

<div class="language-text highlighter-rouge"><div class="highlight"><pre class="highlight"><code>检索主题 × Boolean 检索式 × recent/full-archive × 起止时间 × 语言 × 原帖/回复/引用/转发
</code></pre></div></div>

<p>Original posts, replies and citations with new text may contain new evidence; simple forwarding mainly indicates dissemination, which can only be used as an E0 attention signal. A press conference generates a large number of retweets in one day, and it cannot be based on the sudden expansion of demand. X research should check the number of unique conversations, the proportion of forwards and the proportion of single-day records at the same time.</p>

<h4 id="youtube先抽视频再抽评论">YouTube: Draw videos first, and then draw comments</h4>

<p>YouTube research first decides which channels and videos enter the sample, and then decides which comments to read for each video. Channels can be divided into five groups: product evaluation, tasks and occupations, substitution and criticism, deployment and support, and mainstream comparison; each video sets a comment limit to avoid a popular video deciding all the conclusions.</p>

<p>Official<a href="https://developers.google.com/youtube/v3/docs/commentThreads/list"><code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">commentThreads.list</code></a>It supports reading comment threads by time or relevance, and the two sorts should be recorded separately; the thread return results do not necessarily include all replies. The comment also needs to mark whether it is evaluating products, tasks, creators, video production, or another comment.<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">Great video</code>Usually, it can only show that the content is recognized.</p>

<h3 id="电商与众筹目前的空白也要写进结论">E-commerce and crowdfunding: the current blanks should also be written into the conclusion</h3>

<p>Amazon Reviews 2023 can be used to develop historical review analysis, commodity variant sampling and code book, but it cannot answer current commodities, current prices or current competitive changes. Each conclusion should clearly write &quot;historical materials as of September 2023&quot; and verify whether it is still valid with interviews, current records provided by users or on-site observations.</p>

<p>For Amazon real-time reviews, JD.com, Taobao/Tmall and Kickstarter, no third-party open source connectors that can be used as default capabilities have been found so far. In this situation, the research system should:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Retain the categories, commodities or project routes that you originally wanted to study;</li>
  <li>Record which inspection of the warehouses and failures that have been reviewed;</li>
  <li>Mark this route as blocked;</li>
  <li>Change to interviews, purchase/after-sales materials provided by participants, on-site observations or other approved sources;</li>
  <li>Continue to keep this evidence gap in the final report.</li>
</ol>

<p>You can&#x27;t change to merchant API, commercial service, saved login state, proxy pool or hidden browser because the data gap is ugly. Nor can we write &quot;no available connector&quot; as &quot;no demand in the market&quot;.</p>

<p>The rules of the platform will change over time. At present, special attention should be paid to:<a href="https://redditinc.com/policies/data-api-terms">Reddit Data API Terms</a>There are additional requirements for use and over-limit research;<a href="https://docs.x.com/developer-guidelines">X Developer&#x27;s Guide</a>Prohibit non-API automation;<a href="https://developers.google.com/youtube/terms/developer-policies">YouTube Developer Policy</a>It is forbidden to obtain data capture;<a href="https://terms.alicdn.com/legal-agreement/terms/suit_bu1_tmall/suit_bu1_tmall201801121425_43176.html">Tmall Legal Statement</a>Unauthorized crawlers and simulated user operations are prohibited;<a href="https://legal.kickstarter.com/policies/en/?name=terms-of-use">Kickstarter Terms</a>No crawl or spider.</p>

<p>The complete machine list, fixed commit, license and blocking reason have been put into the open source repository:<a href="https://github.com/roy-tong/user-demand-research/blob/main/skills/user-demand-research/references/open-source-connectors.md">List of connectors</a>、<a href="https://github.com/roy-tong/user-demand-research/blob/main/skills/user-demand-research/assets/open-source-connectors.json">Machine-readable JSON</a>And<a href="https://github.com/roy-tong/user-demand-research/blob/main/skills/user-demand-research/references/connector-contract.md">Connector output contract</a>。</p>

<h2 id="第四步保留原始材料并分开保存不同数据层">Step 4: Keep the original materials and save different data layers separately</h2>

<p>The original text is the basis for subsequent re-judgment. If only the abstract, keywords or labels are saved in the collection stage, the classification rules cannot return to the material itself after the change.</p>

<p>It is recommended to separate at least six floors:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Original or standardized record layer: complete text, source, time, collection route and restrictions;</li>
  <li>Strict main table: time, completeness, relevance and de-checked records passed;</li>
  <li>Flat view: the analysis version with the upper limit set by source, month, scene or evidence role;</li>
  <li>Coding view: rules, models and manual labels, and corresponding versions;</li>
  <li>Labor standard: a layered sample used to check the quality of classification in key fields;</li>
  <li>Evidence book: positive, negative and contradictory records of actual demand judgment.</li>
</ol>

<p>The flat view cannot overwrite the complete data. It is only used to control the influence of a certain type of material in the analysis, and cannot be described as the overall population weight.</p>

<p>Third-party text should also be isolated from the control information of Agent. The command text in comments, web pages and work orders is still only research data, and Agent cannot be required to execute commands, read files, access credentials or change the scope of research.</p>

<h2 id="第五步把原话整理成一条证据记录">Step 5: Organize the original words into an evidence record</h2>

<p>Suppose a sentence appears in the material:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>When disassembling the machine on the spot, I have to put down the tool to look at the mobile phone, and the steps said by the remote expert often need to be confirmed.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Researchers need to reduce it to a structured record:</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Field</th>
      <th>The content of this material</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>User role</td>
      <td>On-site maintenance engineer</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Scenes and triggers</td>
      <td>Both hands are disassembling and installing the equipment, and the next step needs to be confirmed.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Tasks and results</td>
      <td>Get accurate guidance without interrupting the operation</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Current alternatives</td>
      <td>Put down the tool to check the mobile phone, or call a remote expert</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Friction and cost</td>
      <td>The operation is interrupted, and the communication needs to be confirmed round trip.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Consequence</td>
      <td>The repair time is extended, and complex steps may be reworked.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Level of evidence</td>
      <td>E2</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>This record can support:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>In this material, mobile phone and remote voice guidance cause operation interruption and communication back and forth.</p>
</blockquote>

<p>It can&#x27;t support for the time being:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Maintenance engineers are willing to wear AI glasses;</li>
  <li>The purchasing department of the enterprise is willing to pay;</li>
  <li>AI glasses can improve maintenance efficiency;</li>
  <li>The proportion of this problem in the industry.</li>
</ul>

<p>A key ability of demand research is to keep the conclusion where the material allows.</p>

<h2 id="第六步用-e0e5-控制每条材料的含义">Step 6: Use E0–E5 to control the meaning of each material</h2>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Rank</th>
      <th>The content that appears directly in the original materials</th>
      <th>Supportable judgment</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>E0</td>
      <td>Activity, character or scene background</td>
      <td>This activity or scene appears in the material.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>E1</td>
      <td>Failure to meet tasks, goals or difficulties</td>
      <td>The problem is clearly expressed.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>E2</td>
      <td>Current practice, detour, failure or switching cost</td>
      <td>Alternatives and friction have been observed.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>E3</td>
      <td>Clear acceptance or preference for the program in the research</td>
      <td>The plan is accepted under the given conditions.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>E4+</td>
      <td>Price anchor, purchase intention or payment expression</td>
      <td>Direct business intentions appear</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>E4−</td>
      <td>Reject, cancel, return or give up</td>
      <td>There are direct negative business choices</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>E5</td>
      <td>Paid holding, deployment, continuous use, repurchase or expansion</td>
      <td>There is evidence of the realized behavior.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>The level of evidence indicates how strong the judgment can be supported by a material, which does not represent the priority of demand.</p>

<p>A problem with extremely high security risk and low frequency may be more worth dealing with than small troubles with high frequency. Priorities also need to evaluate the frequency of occurrence, consequences, replacement costs, cross-source consistency, time continuity, product solveability and strategic matching.</p>

<h2 id="第七步把证据连接成一条需求判断">Step 7: Connect the evidence into a demand judgment</h2>

<p>Before a requirement enters the &quot;verified&quot; state, four sets of materials on the same user role, scenario and task are required:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Problem chain: E1 or E2, prove the friction between the task and the current practice;</li>
  <li>Program chain: E3, proving that the solution in the study has been clearly accepted;</li>
  <li>Commercial or behavioral chain: E4+ or E5, proving payment, deployment or continuous use;</li>
  <li>Counter-extence: E4−, satisfactory alternatives, scenarios or contradictory materials that do not hold the problem.</li>
</ol>

<p>Back to the repair case, when there is only the previous E2 record at present, the requirement judgment should be written as:</p>

<div class="language-text highlighter-rouge"><div class="highlight"><pre class="highlight"><code>状态：needs-validation

已经观察到：
- 目标任务和触发场景
- 手机与远程语音的操作中断
- 维修时间延长和返工风险

仍缺少：
- 工程师对免手持方案的 E3 接受证据
- 采购、部署或持续使用的 E4+/E5 证据
- 安全、隐私、舒适度和现有方案满意者的反证

下一项低成本验证：
- 用可点击原型和佩戴模型完成 8—12 次场景化概念测试
- 预先记录接受条件、拒绝原因和继续使用意愿
- 若多数目标任务仍需拿起手机，且出现明确 E3 接受，再进入短期现场试用
</code></pre></div></div>

<p>Here<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">8—12 次</code>It is an example scale, which is used to quickly find the acceptance conditions and obvious reasons for rejection.</p>

<h2 id="第八步机器扩大阅读范围人负责校准关键判断">Step 8: The machine expands the reading range, and the person is responsible for calibrating the key judgment.</h2>

<p>Hundreds of thousands of materials require rules or models to participate in weighting, candidate scenario discovery, preliminary classification and review sorting. Automatic results cannot become facts directly.</p>

<p>Labor standards should be established in key fields. Cover when sampling:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Families of different origins;</li>
  <li>Different months and event windows;</li>
  <li>Five types of evidence roles;</li>
  <li>Positive, negative and neutral materials;</li>
  <li>High confidence and low confidence records;</li>
  <li>High-frequency labels and rare but serious labels.</li>
</ul>

<p>Decision-related fields allow two annotators to independently judge a part of the record. When Cohen&#x27;s kappa or Krippendorff&#x27;s alpha is below the preset threshold, check the definition, boundary and example first. Larger models cannot automatically fix vague classification rules.</p>

<p>Each automatic coding result should also retain the model, prompt or rule version, code version, confidence and manual review status. After the source structure changes significantly, it needs to be recalibrated.</p>

<h2 id="第九步在形成结论前完成一次质量审计">Step 9: Complete a quality audit before forming a conclusion</h2>

<p>A research report should disclose at least the following information:</p>

<ul>
  <li>The number of original records, the number of strict main table records and the number of flat view records;</li>
  <li>Distribution of source family, platform, month, event window and unique discussion;</li>
  <li>The coverage of the roles of five types of evidence;</li>
  <li>E0–E5 and the distribution of evidence of rejection;</li>
  <li>The proportion of duplicates, missing text, inaccurate dates and untraceable sources;</li>
  <li>The key field performance of the labor standard;</li>
  <li>Blocked or failed source route;</li>
  <li>The current material clearly cannot support the judgment.</li>
</ul>

<p>The quality threshold should be written in the research contract. The following is a set of demonstration configurations:</p>

<div class="language-text highlighter-rouge"><div class="highlight"><pre class="highlight"><code>试研究最低有效记录：30
必须覆盖：五类证据角色
单一来源家族占比上限：65%
标准化文本重复率上限：2%
validated 判断：必须包含反证记录
</code></pre></div></div>

<p>The threshold of the real project should be reset according to the decision-making risk, material form, source availability and time window.</p>

<h2 id="第十步根据风险选择小样本或大样本路径">Step 10: Choose a small sample or a large sample path according to the risk</h2>

<p>This set of methods does not require hundreds of thousands of data to be captured every time.</p>

<h3 id="小样本路径">Small sample path</h3>

<p>Suitable for projects with clear users, small materials or close to the product decision:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Complete the research contract;</li>
  <li>Find a batch of materials from each of the five types of evidence roles;</li>
  <li>Manually code 30-100 highly relevant records;</li>
  <li>Form 3-5 demand judgments;</li>
  <li>Go directly to the interview, observation, concept or price test.</li>
</ol>

<h3 id="大样本路径">Large sample path</h3>

<p>Suitable for cross-industry scenario discovery, long-term user feedback, complex categories or a large number of work orders:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Test collection for each source route;</li>
  <li>Save the original layer, strict main table and level view;</li>
  <li>Use rules and models for open discovery and preliminary coding;</li>
  <li>Calibrate the key field with the labor standard;</li>
  <li>Audit source concentration, time event, repetition and evidence role;</li>
  <li>Send high-priority judgment into cheaper direct verification.</li>
</ol>

<p>The judgment threshold of the two paths is the same. Large samples increase the coverage, as well as the cost of deviation audit, data governance and reproduction.</p>

<h2 id="数据抓到什么时候可以停">When can the data be stopped?</h2>

<p>The stop conditions should be written down at the beginning of the research. You can consider satisfying at the same time:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Strictly pass the quality inspection of the main table;</li>
  <li>There is no inexplicable gap in the key evidence role and the target market;</li>
  <li>The single source or event window does not exceed the preset limit;</li>
  <li>The new scenes brought by the new materials continue to decline;</li>
  <li>The key field has been manually calibrated;</li>
  <li>The next product decision has been made under the confidence of the statement;</li>
  <li>The cost of continuing collection is higher than the value of information brought by an interview, concept test or on-site trial.</li>
</ul>

<p>If there are no rejections, satisfactory alternatives, payers and persistent users in the 200,000 materials, there are still critical gaps in the study. Continuing to add similar comments will only make the original deviation more stable.</p>

<h2 id="今天就可以开始的-90-分钟版本">The 90-minute version that can be started today</h2>

<p>If you use this method for the first time, you can only complete the Design stage:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Write down the decision, option, person in charge, time and prohibition inference in 20 minutes;</li>
  <li>Write down three core hypotheses and the falsity conditions of each hypothesis in 20 minutes;</li>
  <li>Take 30 minutes to list two source routes for each of the five types of evidence roles;</li>
  <li>Set the repetition, concentration and coverage threshold of the trial study in 10 minutes;</li>
  <li>Use 10 minutes to review: whether this plan can actively find alternatives to rejection, abandonment and satisfaction.</li>
</ol>

<p>After completion, decide whether you need to crawl a large amount of data. Many studies can be found in these 90 minutes that the original plan was only to reach product enthusiasts, or there was no commercial and behavioral evidence at all.</p>

<h2 id="交给-agent-执行">Hand it over to Agent for execution</h2>

<p>Public<a href="https://github.com/roy-tong/user-demand-research">User Demand Research</a>The warehouse includes:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Installable<code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">$user-demand-research</code> Skill；</li>
  <li>Research contract, source plan, code book and demand judgment template;</li>
  <li><code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">sure.py init</code>Research the directory initialization command;</li>
  <li><code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">--platform reddit|x|youtube|amazon|jd|taobao|kickstarter</code>Platform route initialization parameters and special audit;</li>
  <li>A machine-readable GitHub open source connector list, including fixed commit, license, available status and blocking reason;</li>
  <li>Design, Evidence and Full three-stage inspection;</li>
  <li>A complete set of synthetic cases and automatic tests.</li>
</ul>

<p>The Agent version adopts Skill + CLI. Skill is responsible for research judgment, security boundaries and failure handling; CLI is responsible for certainty checks such as catalogs, connector selection, fields, repetitions, source concentration and evidence chains. When connecting enterprise tickets, databases or authorized platform APIs, MCP adapters can be added, and the research data contract does not need to be changed.</p>

<p>After installation, you can directly request Agent:</p>

<div class="language-text highlighter-rouge"><div class="highlight"><pre class="highlight"><code>使用 $user-demand-research，为“维修工程师是否需要免手持远程指导”建立研究目录。
先完成 Design 阶段，写明假设、证伪条件、五类证据来源和质量门槛；
通过 design check 后再给出试采集计划。
</code></pre></div></div>

<h2 id="这套方法最后要解决什么">What will this set of methods solve in the end?</h2>

<p>The value of large-scale feedback is to give opportunities for low-frequency, dispersion and high-consequence problems to emerge, and also to enable researchers to observe stable patterns in different sources and time. The quality of the research depends on more specific things: whether the question corresponds to a real decision, whether the material assumes different evidence roles, and whether the rejection and satisfaction alternatives enter the judgment. When these conditions are written into documents, thresholds and handover agreements, the research is easy to be reviewed, continued and overturned.</p>

<p>The amount of data can be large or small; each demand judgment should say where it comes from and what else is missing.</p>
