    <h1>Body intelligent introductory map: how do I organize industries, companies and technologies?</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">Put industry, company, product, technology and career choice into the same framework</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-07-20T10:30:00+08:00">2026-07-20</time>
      <span>8 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/embodied-intelligence-beginners-guide/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>In the past few months, I have been systematically learning body intelligence.</p>

<p>After I began to delve into this field, I soon encountered a problem: there was a lot of information, but there was a lack of reading order.</p>

<p>I saw humanoid robots today and quadruped robots tomorrow; I just understood VLA, and I encountered world models, reinforcement learning, remote operation and synthetic data. Product releases, financing news, demonstration videos, papers and industry reports are increasing day by day, but they are often not discussed at the same level.</p>

<p>Product form, application scenario, industrial chain, technical route, company competitiveness and business progress are often mixed together. The more you read, the easier it is to remember fragmented nouns, but you still can&#x27;t tell the structure of the industry.</p>

<p>I have been doing software and AI products for a long time, and robots are also a new field for me that needs to be understood from scratch. In order to build a relatively complete map, I spent more than three months sorting out company information, industry reports, papers, videos, podcasts and official account articles, and tried to compare them with the same set of questions.</p>

<p>These notes later became &quot;Introduction to Body Intelligence: Industry, Company, Product, Technology and Career Map&quot;.</p>

<h2 id="先建立坐标再判断公司和技术">Establish the coordinates first, and then judge the company and technology.</h2>

<p>I regard body intelligence as a city that is still under construction.</p>

<p>When you first enter this field, it is easy to immediately ask which company is the strongest and which technical route will win. However, if there are no basic coordinates, these judgments often only compare financing, demonstration effects and technical terms together.</p>

<p>I will answer a few sets of basic questions first:</p>

<ul>
  <li>What links does the industrial chain met of, and what problems does each link solve?</li>
  <li>What tasks are suitable for humanoid, quadruped, wheeled arms and fixed robotic arms?</li>
  <li>Is a company doing a demonstration, pilot, delivery, or has it formed a repurchase?</li>
  <li>How do ontology, perception, control, model, data and deployment work together?</li>
  <li>How should a person with a non-robot background enter this industry?</li>
</ul>

<p>These questions constitute the basic framework of the whole book.</p>

<h2 id="第一部分产业与场景">Part I: Industry and Scenario</h2>

<p>This part first discusses the boundaries of embodent intelligence, the eight-layer industrial chain and the main product forms.</p>

<p>I didn&#x27;t put all the robots in the same ranking, but started from the task: do customers need to carry, inspect, operate tools, take care of, accompany, or home security? Different tasks have different requirements for ontology, degree of freedom, security, cost and deployment method.</p>

<p>The same kind of robot at the press conference and in the real scene may also be two completely different products. Between demonstrating one action and completing tasks stably for a long time, there is also reliability, maintenance, staffing, system integration and cost.</p>

<h2 id="第二部分公司研究">Part II: Corporate Research</h2>

<p>I sorted out more than 80 domestic and foreign companies and selected 39 to establish the first round of tracking matrix.</p>

<p>We don&#x27;t make a general list of companies here. I separate companies in different categories, tasks and business stages, and then observe them from the perspectives of business, product, technology, management, capital and organization, while marking the source of evidence and credibility.</p>

<p>I repeatedly used a question in the company research:</p>

<blockquote>
  <p>How can a robot product go from demonstration to pilot, delivery and repurchase?</p>
</blockquote>

<p>Financing amount, valuation and demo videos can provide information, but they cannot replace customer, deployment and revenue evidence. What&#x27;s more worth asking is: who is using it, what is the complete task, what stage it has entered, how much manpower is needed for deployment, and why customers are willing to continue to use it.</p>

<h2 id="第三部分产品与技术">Part Three: Products and Technologies</h2>

<p>The technical part pushes the system backwards from the product task, and does not explain it article by article according to the terms of the thesis.</p>

<p>If a robot wants to complete tasks in a real environment, what is the relationship between ontology, perception, motion control, VLA, world model, data, simulation, dexterity, touch, security and deployment? What abilities determine whether &quot;can be done&quot;, which abilities determine &quot;can be done stably&quot;, and what problems will ultimately fall on cost and maintenance?</p>

<p>I hope this part can help readers with non-algorithm backgrounds establish system relationships, and also help people who are only familiar with a certain local technology to understand its position in the complete product.</p>

<h2 id="第四部分职业与学习">Part Four: Career and Learning</h2>

<p>Entering a rapidly changing industry, career choices should not only be based on the company name and job title.</p>

<p>This part sorts out the job group, recruitment information, salary caliber, employee experience and separation agent signals, and also provides a set of company research worksheets. This material does not guarantee the result of career change; it is used to help readers judge where their experience can be transferred, what the target position is responsible for, and what knowledge and practice should be supplemented.</p>

<h2 id="这份地图适合怎样使用">How to use this map</h2>

<p>This is not a robot algorithm textbook, nor can it replace papers, development documents, real machine experiments and continuous research.</p>

<p>The following types of readers can use it as an introductory reference:</p>

<ul>
  <li>I want to understand the body intelligence systematically, but I don&#x27;t have a professional background in robotics;</li>
  <li>Need to study industries, companies, products or business opportunities;</li>
  <li>The embed intelligent company and position are being evaluated;</li>
  <li>I am familiar with local technology, and I hope to complete the industry, product and business perspectives.</li>
</ul>

<p>When using this map, you can put different companies and products back into their respective tasks and stages, and avoid judging the industry only by the financing amount, demonstration effect and popular concepts.</p>

<h2 id="关于版本与更新">About the version and update</h2>

<p>At present, the summer edition of 2026 has been completed, with a total of 120 pages.</p>

<p>This material records the stage judgment of the summer of 2026. Body intelligence changes rapidly, and many judgments need to be tested by products, customers and time. The subsequent version will record which companies have entered a new stage, which technical judgments have changed, and which scenarios that were originally optimistic have not developed as expected.</p>
