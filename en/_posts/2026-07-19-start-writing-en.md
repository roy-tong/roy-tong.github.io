    <h1>Start writing again</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">About this website, and what I&#x27;m going to record here</p>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>I have been doing work that requires a lot of input and judgment: understanding technological changes, finding real needs, designing products, and evaluating whether a direction is worth investing time in.</p>

<p>These jobs will produce a lot of scattered thoughts. They appear in meeting minutes, research reports, dialogues, and documents that have not been sorted out yet. There is a lot of information, but there are not many judgments that can be reused for a long time.</p>

<p>So there is this website.</p>

<h2 id="这里会写什么">What will be written here?</h2>

<p>At present, I am most concerned about four types of problems:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>AI and Agent</strong>: How will the product form, interaction mode and software structure change after the rapid change of model ability;</li>
  <li><strong>Body intelligence</strong>: When can robots enter the industry and home, and what is the difference between technological progress and commercial landing;</li>
  <li><strong>Products and entrepreneurship</strong>: How to find real needs, how to go from vague concepts to verifiable products;</li>
  <li><strong>Organization and personal growth</strong>: How do managers judge the direction and build a team, and how do they face uncertainty?</li>
</ol>

<p>These topics look different, but they all point to the same problem:<strong>How to make new technologies into products that can be used by people and delivered continuously.</strong></p>

<h2 id="我希望怎样写">How do I want to write?</h2>

<p>I don&#x27;t pursue daily updates here, and I don&#x27;t intend to turn every hot spot into an opinion. I have set three writing principles for myself:</p>

<ul>
  <li>Distinguish facts, inferences and positions as much as possible;</li>
  <li>Retain the process of judgment formation, not just show the conclusion;</li>
  <li>When new facts appear, allow yourself to modify the answer.</li>
</ul>

<p>Open writing will leave a version that can be tested, discussed, and can continue to be modified.</p>

<p>This is the first one. Take your time and keep writing.</p>
