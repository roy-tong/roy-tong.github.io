    <h1>AI interaction is waiting for its &quot;touch moment&quot;</h1>
    <p class="post-subtitle">Glasses, headphones and rings will not grow into a language by themselves. To see, point, pinch and say become common grammar, first of all, we need to solve the modal division of labor, body position and wearing cost.</p>
    <div class="post-meta">
      <time datetime="2026-08-13T21:40:00+08:00">2026-08-13</time>
      <span>14 minutes to read</span>
      
      <a class="post-translation" href="/notes/ai-interaction-grammar/" hreflang="zh-CN">阅读中文版 →</a>

  <div class="prose">
    <p>Open an AI Wearable competitive list, and the first column is usually glasses, headphones, rings, watches and pendants. This classification is suitable for placing goods, not for explaining competition: two pairs of glasses with similar appearance may be completely different products, and a pair of glasses, a headset and a ring may also compete for the same thing - who can most naturally get the user&#x27;s current context, understand the intention, and help without interrupting the actual task.</p>

<p>Use these devices one by one, and you will find another more fundamental problem. Eyes, hands, voice and controller can all be input, and the system does support them, but there is a long distance between &quot;supporting four types of inputs&quot; and &quot;users mastering a set of interactive languages&quot;: it can be seen, it can be pinched, it can be said that the demonstration is very smooth, but it can be changed to an application, the user does not know whether to see, point or open the mouth, and the designer does not know who to give the confirmation.</p>

<p>I call this gap: AI interaction has not yet ushered in its own &quot;touch moment&quot;.</p>

<p>The touch screen did not appear from the iPhone. What changes the habit of mobile computing is that clicking, sliding, dragging, and two-finger zooming gradually gain a stable meaning - there is no need to relearn how to browse, select and return to another App, and there is no need to reinvent the most basic operation to make a new App. The interaction between space and wearable devices is in the stage of &quot;touch screen, but no touch syntax&quot; today.</p>

<p>The sign of its arrival will be: in the face of a strange space application, users already know roughly how to start; the system can also answer stably - what are you looking at, what you have chosen, what will happen next, and how to come back if you make a mistake.</p>

<p>This article divides this problem into four layers: how to divide the work of input, what is the original language of the language, where the grammar grows in the body, and how to verify whether a set of grammar is really valid.</p>

<h2 id="一输入已经够多分工还没形成">I. There are enough inputs, and the division of labor has not been formed.</h2>

<p>Today&#x27;s space equipment can track the line of sight and hands, identify the environment, and fix the window in the real space. Apple uses eyes and hands as the main input in visionOS: look at the target first, and then tap with thumb and forefinger; Android XR integrates hands, eyes, voice, keyboard and mouse and controller into the system interaction. These abilities are enough to operate the &quot;GUI put into space&quot;.</p>

<p>The problem is that operating a floating window is not the same as operating the real world. The screen has a stable plane, the mouse has a clear cursor, and the fingers can be supported by the desktop; there are people with depth, cover and constantly moving in the space. The line of sight will naturally sweep over the object, the hands will tremble, and the arms will be tired after hanging in the air for a long time (the study of the head display has long quantified the fatigue of hanging gestures, that is, consumed endurance). In the office or subway, users may not be willing to continue to talk and wave.<a href="https://doi.org/10.1145/3025453.3025523">Hincapié-Ramos et al.：Consumed Endurance</a>More importantly, the spatial action may touch the real device: clicking an icon by mistake will close the window at most, closing a valve by mistake, and moving a machine by mistake, the consequences are completely different.</p>

<p>Therefore, the key, mouse and touch cannot be moved into the three-dimensional world as it is. The value of multi-modality is not to let users choose one of the four entrances, but to disassemble a complete intention into the most appropriate input:</p>

<ul>
  <li><strong>Speech sound</strong>: Expressing open and semantically complex intentions - but it is not good at accurately pointing out the &quot;third part on the right&quot; from dense objects, and may not be open in meetings and subways;</li>
  <li><strong>Line of vision</strong>: Establish attention and context, which is the most natural, but &quot;seeing&quot; cannot directly pretend to be &quot;agree&quot;;</li>
  <li><strong>Pinch and button</strong>: Expressing commitment and direct operation, suitable for confirmation and continuous actions, complex gestures are difficult to learn, and not suitable for hanging for a long time - for deterministic actions such as &quot;start recording&quot; and &quot;confirm payment&quot;, the entity button is usually more reliable than open statements;</li>
  <li><strong>Gestures, rings, wristbands</strong>: Issue discrete instructions when you don&#x27;t speak or touch the facial device;</li>
  <li><strong>Mobile or cellular phone</strong>: Undertake the first setting, complex editing, permission management and abnormal recovery.</li>
</ul>

<p>W3C has long regarded voice, touch, keyboard and handwriting as different inputs, and display, voice, audio and touch as different outputs. Entering the AI era, the real new problem is that models begin to understand semantics, maintain context and generate responses between these modals, so &quot;which input is responsible for which intention&quot; has become a product decision that must be designed for the first time, not the default of the system.<a href="https://www.w3.org/TR/mmi-arch/">W3C：Multimodal Architecture and Interfaces</a></p>

<p>In a word: how the input and output answer system perceives and expresses, and the control method answers how users reliably submit their intentions, and the first layer of competition for products such as AI glasses and AI headphones is the right choice of this pair. Google&#x27;s public description of Android XR glasses is an example: the camera, microphone and speaker provide visual and voice input and sound output, and the display is optional - whether to add the display is not &quot;high configuration and low configuration&quot;, but the redistribution of product tasks, feedback bandwidth and wearing cost.<a href="https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/platforms/android/android-xr-gemini-glasses-headsets/">Google：Android XR</a></p>

<h2 id="二四个原语看指捏说">II. Four original words: look, point, pinch, say</h2>

<p>A stable set of semantics is also needed in addition to the division of labor. I am willing to compress the spatial interaction into four original words:</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Original language</th>
      <th>Main responsibilities</th>
      <th>What is it suitable for?</th>
      <th>It can&#x27;t be acquiesced to what</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Look after</td>
      <td>Establish attention and context</td>
      <td>Highlight candidates, read status, and narrow the range</td>
      <td>Confirm, authorize or execute</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Finger</td>
      <td>Clarify the object, area and direction</td>
      <td>Eliminate the ambiguity of &quot;this&quot; and &quot;there&quot;</td>
      <td>High-precision continuous control</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Pinch</td>
      <td>Express commitment and operate directly</td>
      <td>Select, grab, drag, zoom, fine-tune</td>
      <td>Describe complex goals</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Say</td>
      <td>Express goals, relationships and constraints</td>
      <td>Ask questions, compare, generate and arrange multi-step tasks</td>
      <td>Complete accurate spatial positioning by yourself</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>Look, let the system know what I&#x27;m paying attention to at the moment. Refers to narrowing the &quot;piece in front of you&quot; into an object or area. Pinch, turn the attention into a clear confirmation, and also undertake to take, move and adjust. It is said that it is responsible for the parts that are difficult to express gestures: why to change, what to change, and what conditions to meet.</p>

<p>These four words are not industry standards, and they do not require each device to use the same set of sensors. What needs to be stable is the semantic boundary: you can use your eyes or your head to establish a focus, you can pinch your fingers or press the solid key to complete the confirmation - the input method can be adapted, and the core meaning cannot be repeated every application. The action mechanism of OpenXR has reflected this idea: the application statement of actions with business meaning such as &quot;select&quot; and &quot;grab&quot;, and map the specific inputs of different devices during operation. The platform should fix the public meaning of the action, not a certain button.<a href="https://registry.khronos.org/OpenXR/spec/1.0-khr/html/xrspec.html#input">Khronos：OpenXR Action</a></p>

<h2 id="三组合起来从-point-and-click-到-point-and-ask">III. Combination: from Point and Click to Point and Ask</h2>

<p>With the original language, there will be a more natural combination than the desktop era.</p>

<p>I pointed to a strange device and asked, &quot;Why does this light keep flashing?&quot; - pointing to the object, the context provided on the spot, and the voice provided the question. Users do not need to check the device model first, nor do they need to describe the &quot;black cylinder in the second row on the left&quot;. The space itself has entered the prompt. This is Point and Ask.</p>

<p>I pointed to a part of the mechanical structure and said, &quot;Disassemble this part and show it to me&quot;, or pointed to an area in the living room and said, &quot;Change this place to a layout that can be discussed by six people and without blocking the passage&quot; - the first half of the sentence determines the object, the second half of the sentence expresses the goal, and the Agent then disassembles the target into steps, calls tools or generate schemes. This is Point and Act.</p>

<p>The trouble also starts from here. The system must first let me see what it has selected, instead of guessing it directly; it should be previewed before modifying the real state, and the high-risk action should be confirmed again; the progress and scope of influence should be displayed during execution; after making a mistake, it should be able to cancel, roll back or return labor. Without these feedbacks, Point and Ask is just a more convenient question; without these controls, Point and Act will become a black box triggered by one sentence.</p>

<p>This leads to two circuits at the same time. I grabbed a three-dimensional model and turned it over. This should happen immediately - if each rotation has to wait for the Agent to understand, plan and reply, even if it is only half a second slow, the sense of control in the hand will disappear. This is a quick ring: look at the object, pinch and confirm, directly change the local state, and immediately feedback. And when I said, &quot;change this structure to be more suitable for children&#x27;s use, give three solutions first, and check the position where it is easy to pinch hands&quot;, it enters the slow loop: indicate the object, say the goal, Agent planning, display preview, user confirmation, execution and retreat.</p>

<p>The most difficult thing about the product is how to junction the two circuits. Users want to know who is in control at present - whether my hand is directly moving the object, or the Agent has begun to modify multiple states; which step it has done, where to speculate, and when I need to confirm; if I take over now, where the real world and digital state will stop. The stronger the Agent, the more important these boundary problems are: the ability increases, the control boundary is not clear, and the system will only become more unpredictable.</p>

<h2 id="四这套语法长在身体的什么位置">IV. Where does this set of grammar grow in the body?</h2>

<p>Public grammar cannot be suspended. It should be deployed on a specific device, and the device should grow in a specific position on the body. Mobile phones are usually in the pocket or desktop. To get the context, users need to take out, unlock, aim and input. The value of Wearable is to put sensors and feedback channels closer to the senses, actions and intentions to eliminate the start-up cost.</p>

<p>The context carried by different body areas is different:</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Body area</th>
      <th>The most accessible context</th>
      <th>The most suitable feedback or control</th>
      <th>The core cost</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>Near the eyes and face</td>
      <td>First-person picture, direction of attention, environmental space</td>
      <td>Visual display, sound, touch mirror legs</td>
      <td>Optics, weight, heat, appearance, prescription adaptation and shooting privacy</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Near the ear</td>
      <td>Environmental sound, dialogue, head movement</td>
      <td>Private audio, touch, head movement</td>
      <td>Long-term blockage, auditory occupation, hygiene and battery life</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Near the mouth and throat</td>
      <td>Voice, breathing, subtle voice and communication intention</td>
      <td>Voice input, silent voice potential</td>
      <td>Social visibility, noise, privacy and skin comfort</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Wrists and fingers</td>
      <td>Hand movement, confirmation intention, physiological and activity signals</td>
      <td>Gestures, touch, buttons, discrete commands</td>
      <td>The understanding of the scene is weak, and the action is touched by mistake and occupied by both hands.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>Neck and chest</td>
      <td>More stable orientation, sound, movement and larger carrying space</td>
      <td>Audio, tactile, camera or as a computing battery carrier</td>
      <td>Swinging, clothing cover, oppression, heat and appearance acceptance</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>&quot;Context density&quot; depends on whether a location can continue to be close to what the user is watching, hearing, saying, doing and deciding, rather than the number of sensors. It is important to be close to the five senses because it is close to the real input and the main perception channel of people at the same time; the neck and chest provide another possibility - to remove part of the weight, camera, battery or calculation from the face in exchange for a larger carrying space.</p>

<p>But the body position is not a piece of territory that can be snatched and owned forever. It is a continuous license: the device has a context only when the user is willing to wear it, allows perception, and can be used in real situations. Therefore, the strongest competitors are often mobile phones - mobile phones start up with more friction, but they have mature display, input, computing power, connection and application ecology. Wearable must prove that its reduced friction at high frequencies or critical moments is enough to cover the wearing, charging and management costs of new devices.</p>

<p>The controller does not have to grow on the main device. In visionOS, Apple uses eyes to point and gestures to confirm; Meta&#x27;s display glasses use EMG wristbands as a controller to issue commands with subtle hand movements; Samsung&#x27;s smart ring can control the mobile phone to shoot or turn off the alarm clock by pinching with two fingers.<a href="https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2023/10073/">Apple：Design for spatial input</a>、<a href="https://about.fb.com/news/2025/09/meta-ray-ban-display-ai-glasses-emg-wristband/">Meta: Display glasses and EMG wristband</a>、<a href="https://www.samsung.com/uk/support/mobile-devices/what-is-the-double-pinch-gesture-on-the-galaxy-ring/">Samsung: Galaxy Ring&#x27;s Double Pinch</a>This points to the same change: AI devices can be a set of personal interaction systems distributed between eyes, ears, fingers, wrists and mobile phones - glasses are responsible for the first perspective and display, headphones are responsible for private audio, rings or wristbands are responsible for silent confirmation, and mobile phones are responsible for high-density management. At this time, the analysis of competitive products can not only compare with the host, but also with the whole interaction chain composed of pairing, connection, charging, state synchronization and abnormal recovery.</p>

<h2 id="五佩戴是语法成立的前提">V. Wearing is the premise of the establishment of grammar.</h2>

<p>No matter how beautiful a set of grammar is, the user removes the device after an hour, and there is still very little context that can actually be used. The effective context depends not only on the perception ability, but also on the actual wearing time, scene coverage and user authorization - any one close to zero, the context that the device can actually get will be greatly reduced.</p>

<p>Weight is only a part of the cost of wearing, and there are at least five dimensions:</p>

<ol>
  <li><strong>Physical comfort</strong>: Total weight, center of gravity, contact area, clamping force, temperature rise, material and size adaptation. The study of the head-mounted display found that weight affects fatigue, and the position of the center of gravity changes this effect - the same gram, distributed in different ways on the bridge of the nose, ears and head, feels completely different.<a href="https://www.mdpi.com/2076-3417/11/15/6802">Applied Sciences: Head-mounted display weight and center of gravity</a></li>
  <li><strong>Sensory comfort</strong>: Display brightness, field of view occlusion, sound leakage, continuous prompts - the device does not have tenderness, which does not mean that it will not cause visual, auditory or cognitive fatigue.</li>
  <li><strong>Interactive and comfortable</strong>: Frequently saying wake-up words, raising hands, pinching, and repeating confirmation are all costs. It&#x27;s cool to do an action for the first time, but it doesn&#x27;t mean that it&#x27;s still natural to perform it a hundred times a day.</li>
  <li><strong>Social comfort</strong>: Whether users are willing to wear it to meet customers and take the subway; can bystanders know when the camera works.</li>
  <li><strong>Maintain comfort</strong>: How many times a day to charge, how many boxes to bring, how to clean after sweating, can it still be used if you forget to bring the controller?</li>
</ol>

<p>Comfort will directly constrain the sensor, computing power, structure and business model, and cannot be optimized after the product is completed. At present, many AI hardware are stuck in two extremes: one is just adding chat ability to traditional hardware, and the other is good at showing technology, but does not form a complete experience worth using repeatedly. There is also a natural contradiction between the uncertainty of AI output and the need for hardware to be stable, timely and predictable. The products that users are willing to use for a long time still need to answer those simple questions: does it solve high-frequency and clear needs? Is it more trouble-free than taking out a mobile phone? Can the key tasks be completed steadily?</p>

<h2 id="六再往远处从-spatial-app-到-world-canvas">VI. Further away: from Spatial App to World Canvas</h2>

<p>Today, most of the software is still organized by the App: retouching opens the image software, and navigation opens the map. The space equipment also follows this structure - open an App and put the window into the room. But the real space is not a set of windows: a chair has a position, direction and belonging to; a machine has a structure, state, history and authority; the same object will be used by design tools, maintenance tools and family agents at the same time.</p>

<p>If space computing continues to develop, its organizational unit will gradually move from App to a &quot;world canvas&quot;: real objects and digital objects form a whole that can be understood, operated and authorized, and what the user expresses is no longer a menu command, but &quot;what state do you want this part of the world to become&quot; - tell me why it is leaking here; move this table to a position that does not block the door; freeze the current layout and try another rule, and if the effect is not good, it will be restored.</p>

<p>This is a product direction, not an industry fact that has been completed. To make it valid, the platform needs to provide stable identity for objects, maintain spatial relationships and states, handle cross-application access, and define the respective permissions of people and agents - which is much more difficult than putting a few more windows in the room, and closer to the real difference between spatial computing and desktop computing.</p>

<h2 id="七别用一个漂亮-demo-证明一套语法">VII. Don&#x27;t use a beautiful Demo to prove a set of grammar.</h2>

<p>Space products can easily overcome all the difficulties with one visual effect. In order to judge where the product has gone, I will use a simple five-level scale:</p>

<table>
  <thead>
    <tr>
      <th>Rank</th>
      <th>The ability obtained by users</th>
    </tr>
  </thead>
  <tbody>
    <tr>
      <td>L0 Display</td>
      <td>You can see windows, images or three-dimensional content in space.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>L1 identification</td>
      <td>It can find, point and select objects stably.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>L2 operation</td>
      <td>Able to take, move, combine or modify objects, and get clear feedback</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>L3 Complete the task</td>
      <td>The system can understand the target, coordinate multi-step actions, and users can confirm and take over.</td>
    </tr>
    <tr>
      <td>L4 Continuous collaboration</td>
      <td>Objects, relationships, permissions and task status can be continued across time, scenarios and tools.</td>
    </tr>
  </tbody>
</table>

<p>A visually shocking Demo may still be in L0; a system with a simple picture but stable completion of maintenance guidance and return at any time may have reached L3. Any platform should start with the display. The problem is that you can&#x27;t use &quot;amazing at first time&quot; to prove that users will use it every day.</p>

<p>So if we make a spatial interaction prototype now, I will not create a grand virtual world first, but the task of three ordinary people who can judge success or failure in 30 to 60 seconds.<strong>The first one, covering and leaning</strong>: The target is partially blocked, and the user looks sideways and then points to it. Can the system continue to recognize the same object, and can the user understand which one he has selected?<strong>Second, identification, inquiry and disassembly</strong>: Point to the unfamiliar device to ask its function, and then ask to display the disassembly order, whether each subsequent step can continue the same object context, indicate whether it accurately falls into the corresponding part, and whether the user can correct the error and pause at any time.<strong>Third, freeze, change the rules and return</strong>: Freeze the scene and propose new constraints. The system will preview it first and then confirm the execution by the user. If the effect is not good, you can return to the modification with one click.</p>

<p>These three Demos test object selection, context continuation and Agent control respectively. The test should cover the real environment - quiet and noisy, alone and socializing, hands idle and occupied - should record the wearing behavior of the whole day (when to put on, take off, mute, switch to the mobile phone, forget to charge), and at the same time record the task results and wearing cost: start-up time, completion rate, false triggers, number of removals, effective wearing time, battery life coverage, bystander response. It is also necessary to write down the conditions in advance: if the high-frequency task still needs to return to the mobile phone; if the display improves the completion rate but significantly shortens the wearing time; if the voice cannot be used publicly in most target scenarios - the scheme should be reduced instead of continuing to increase the parameters.</p>

<p>The criterion for judgment finally converged into one sentence: whether the user has learned an action that can be reused in the future, not just thinking that the picture is cool.</p>

<h2 id="结语先把眼镜对耳机从标题里删掉">Conclusion: Delete &quot;glasses to headphones&quot; from the title first.</h2>

<p>Glasses, headphones, rings, watches and pendants will continue to exist, but these names are increasingly difficult to explain the boundaries of products. The competition focuses on four things: which inputs can obtain the context required for the task, which outputs can deliver results with minimal interference, which control methods can allow users to express their intentions reliably and privately, and which body position can make this ability accepted for a long time.</p>

<p>The device with the most sensors, the strongest display or the largest model may not win. The winner is more likely to combine modality, interaction, body position and wearing cost into a product with a low-friction use process.</p>

<p>Therefore, when analyzing AI interaction, you might as well delete &quot;glasses to headphones&quot; from the title first. First, ask: in the real task, which group of perception and feedback channels is the user willing to keep on them for how long? When faced with different applications, people naturally know where to look first, how to indicate, when to confirm, and how to say the goal. The system also steadily knows what is just attention and what is authorization - looking, pointing, pinching, saying is the public grammar that really becomes human, Agent and space objects work together.</p>
